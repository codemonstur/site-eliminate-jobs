Major Groups

The 1,016 professions are organized into 23 major groups, from Management and Business & Financial Operations through to Military occupations. Each group gathers related jobs that share a common skill base and career path, so it is a useful way to see the shape of the whole workforce at a glance. Select a group to browse the professions inside it and how each could be eliminated.
