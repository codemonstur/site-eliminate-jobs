Parts Salespersons

Description

Sell spare and replacement parts and equipment in repair shop or parts store. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They receive payment or obtain credit authorization. They assist customers, such as responding to customer complaints and updating them about back-ordered parts. They fill customer orders from stock, and place orders when requested items are out of stock. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
