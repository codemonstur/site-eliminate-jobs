Telephone Operators

Description

Provide information by accessing alphabetical, geographical, or other directories. Assist customers with special billing requests, such as charges to a third party and credits or refunds for incorrectly dialed numbers or bad connections. May handle emergency calls and assist children or people with physical disabilities to make telephone calls. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They observe signal lights on switchboards, and dial or press buttons to make connections. They operate telephone switchboards and systems to advance and complete connections, including those for local, long distance, pay telephone, mobile, person-to-person, and emergency calls. They listen to customer requests, referring to alphabetical or geographical directories to answer questions and provide telephone information. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
