Paramedics

Description

Administer basic or advanced emergency medical care and assess injuries and illnesses. May administer medication intravenously, use equipment such as EKGs, or administer advanced life support to sick or injured individuals. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They perform emergency cardiac care, such as cardioversion and manual defibrillation. They perform emergency invasive intervention before delivering patient to an acute care facility. They coordinate work with other emergency medical team members or police or fire department personnel. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
