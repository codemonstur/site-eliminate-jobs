Military

Major group 55 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 19 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

Autonomous systems and drone swarms

Autonomous military systems and drones reduce the need for personnel. They operate without human crews.

This compresses operations and reduces staffing.

AI command-and-control and decision support

AI for military command and decisions reduces the need for staff officers. It analyzes and recommends.

This compresses command work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Scale AI
  • Wikipedia: Command and control

Robotic logistics and resupply

Robotic supply and logistics reduce the need for logistics personnel. They handle resupply automatically.

This compresses logistics and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Ghost Robotics
  • Wikipedia: Military logistics

This compresses vehicle operation and reduces staffing.
