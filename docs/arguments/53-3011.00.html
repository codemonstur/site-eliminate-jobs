Ambulance Drivers and Attendants, Except Emergency Medical Technicians

Description

Drive ambulance or assist ambulance driver in transporting sick, injured, or convalescent persons. Assist in lifting patients. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They place patients on stretchers, and load stretchers into ambulances, usually with assistance from other attendants. They replace supplies and disposable items on ambulances. They accompany and assist emergency medical technicians on calls. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
