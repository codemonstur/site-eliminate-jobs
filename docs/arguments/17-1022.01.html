Geodetic Surveyors

Description

Measure large areas of the Earth’s surface using satellite observations, global navigation satellite systems (GNSS), light detection and ranging (LIDAR), or related sources. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They analyze control or survey data to ensure adherence to project specifications or land survey standards. They conduct surveys to determine exact positions, measurement of points, elevations, lines, areas, volumes, contours, or other features of land surfaces. They calculate the exact horizontal and vertical position of points on the Earth’s surface. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
