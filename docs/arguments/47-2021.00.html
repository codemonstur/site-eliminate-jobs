Brickmasons and Blockmasons

Description

Lay and bind building materials, such as brick, structural tile, concrete block, cinder block, glass block, and terra-cotta block, with mortar and other substances, to construct or repair walls, partitions, arches, sewers, and other structures. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They measure distance from reference points and mark guidelines to lay out work, using plumb bobs and levels. They construct corners by fastening in plumb position a corner pole or building a corner pyramid of bricks, and filling in between the corners using a line from corner to corner to guide each course, or layer, of brick. They apply and smooth mortar or other mixture over work surface. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
