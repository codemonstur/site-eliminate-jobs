Production

Major group 51 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 114 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

Industrial and collaborative robots

Robots for manufacturing and assembly reduce the need for production workers. They handle fabrication and assembly.

This compresses production and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • FANUC
  • Universal Robots
  • Wikipedia: Industrial robot

AI visual quality control

Computer vision for defect detection reduces the need for inspectors. It checks quality automatically.

This compresses inspection and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Cognex
  • Keyence
  • Wikipedia: Machine vision

Automated assembly and packaging

Automated assembly lines and packaging reduce the need for assemblers. They handle production automatically.

This compresses assembly and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Bosch
  • Rockwell Automation
  • Wikipedia: Automation

Additive manufacturing (3D printing)

3D printing for production reduces the need for machinists and toolmakers. It builds parts layer by layer.

This compresses manufacturing and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Stratasys
  • 3D Systems
  • Wikipedia: 3D printing

Autonomous warehouse and logistics systems

Automated warehouses and logistics reduce the need for warehouse workers. They store and retrieve goods automatically.

This compresses logistics and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Amazon Robotics
  • Locus Robotics
  • Wikipedia: Warehouse automation
