Derrick Operators, Oil and Gas

Description

Rig derrick equipment and operate pumps to circulate mud or fluid through drill hole. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They start pumps that circulate mud through drill pipes and boreholes to cool drill bits and flush out drill cuttings. They control the viscosity and weight of the drilling fluid. They listen to mud pumps and check regularly for vibration and other problems to ensure that rig pumps and drilling mud systems are working properly. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
