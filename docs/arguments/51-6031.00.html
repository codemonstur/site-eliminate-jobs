Sewing Machine Operators

Description

Operate or tend sewing machines to join, reinforce, decorate, or perform related sewing operations in the manufacture of garment or nongarment products. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They monitor machine operation to detect problems such as defective stitching, breaks in thread, or machine malfunctions. They place spools of thread, cord, or other materials on spindles, insert bobbins, and thread ends through machine guides and components. They position items under needles, using marks on machines, clamps, templates, or cloth as guides. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
