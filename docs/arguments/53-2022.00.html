Airfield Operations Specialists

Description

Ensure the safe takeoff and landing of commercial and military aircraft. Duties include coordination between air-traffic control and maintenance personnel, dispatching, using airfield landing and navigational aids, implementing airfield safety procedures, monitoring and maintaining flight records, and applying knowledge of weather information. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They inspect airfield conditions to ensure compliance with federal regulatory requirements. They implement airfield safety procedures to ensure a safe operating environment for personnel and aircraft operation. They conduct inspections of the airport property and perimeter to maintain controlled access to airfields. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
