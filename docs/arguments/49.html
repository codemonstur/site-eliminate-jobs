Installation, Maintenance, and Repair

Major group 49 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 52 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

Predictive maintenance and AI diagnostics

AI that predicts equipment failures reduces the need for maintenance staff. It forecasts failures and schedules repairs.

This compresses maintenance and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Uptake
  • Augury
  • Wikipedia: Predictive maintenance

AR-guided repair and remote assistance

Augmented reality for repair guidance reduces the need for expert technicians. It overlays instructions on equipment.

This compresses repair and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Microsoft HoloLens
  • TeamViewer
  • Wikipedia: Augmented reality

Autonomous inspection drones

Drones for equipment inspection reduce the need for inspectors. They capture imagery automatically.

This compresses inspection and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Percepto
  • Skydio
  • Wikipedia: Unmanned aerial vehicle

Self-diagnosing and self-repairing systems

Systems that detect and fix their own faults reduce the need for repair staff. They maintain themselves.

This compresses repair and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Siemens
  • GE
  • Wikipedia: Self-healing

This compresses maintenance and reduces staffing.
