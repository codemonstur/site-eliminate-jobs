Installation, Maintenance, and Repair

Major group 49 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 52 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • Predictive maintenance and AI diagnostics — AI that predicts equipment failures. Examples: Uptake, Augury.
  • AR-guided repair and remote assistance — Augmented reality for repair guidance. Examples: Microsoft HoloLens, TeamViewer.
  • Autonomous inspection drones — Drones for equipment inspection. Examples: Percepto, Skydio.
  • Self-diagnosing and self-repairing systems — Systems that detect and fix their own faults. Examples: Siemens, GE.
  • Robotic maintenance platforms — Robots for maintenance tasks. Examples: Boston Dynamics, ABB.
