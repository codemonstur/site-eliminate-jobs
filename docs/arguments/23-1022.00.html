Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators

Description

Facilitate negotiation and conflict resolution through dialogue. Resolve conflicts outside of the court system by mutual consent of parties involved. It belongs to the Legal major group (code 23). They prepare written opinions or decisions regarding cases. They apply relevant laws, regulations, policies, or precedents to reach conclusions. They conduct hearings to obtain information or evidence relative to disposition of claims. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
