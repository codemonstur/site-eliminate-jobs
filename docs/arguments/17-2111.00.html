Health and Safety Engineers, Except Mining Safety Engineers and Inspectors

Description

Health and Safety Engineers, Except Mining Safety Engineers and Inspectors is an occupation classified within Architecture and Engineering in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They investigate industrial accidents, injuries, or occupational diseases to determine causes and preventive measures. They conduct research to evaluate safety levels for products. They evaluate product designs for safety. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
