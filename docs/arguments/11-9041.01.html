Biofuels/Biodiesel Technology and Product Development Managers

Description

Define, plan, or execute biofuels/biodiesel research programs that evaluate alternative feedstock and process technologies with near-term commercial potential. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They design or conduct applied biodiesel or biofuels research projects on topics, such as transport, thermodynamics, mixing, filtration, distillation, fermentation, extraction, and separation. They analyze data from biofuels studies, such as fluid dynamics, water treatments, or solvent extraction and recovery processes. They prepare, or oversee the preparation of, experimental plans for biofuels research or development. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
