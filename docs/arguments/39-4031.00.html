Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers

Description

Perform various tasks to arrange and direct individual funeral services, such as coordinating transportation of body to mortuary, interviewing family or other authorized person to arrange details, selecting pallbearers, aiding with the selection of officials for religious rites, and providing transportation for mourners. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They oversee the preparation and care of the remains of people who have died. They obtain information needed to complete legal documents, such as death certificates or burial permits. They perform embalming duties, as necessary. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
