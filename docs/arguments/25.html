Educational Instruction and Library

Major group 25 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 68 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • AI tutors and adaptive learning platforms — Personalized AI instruction that adapts to each learner. Examples: Khanmigo, Duolingo.
  • Automated grading and feedback — AI that scores assignments and gives feedback. Examples: Gradescope, Turnitin.
  • LLM lesson planning and content generation — Models that draft curricula and teaching materials. Examples: MagicSchool, Eduaide.
  • Virtual classrooms and AI teaching assistants — Online platforms with AI support for instructors. Examples: Zoom, Google Classroom.
  • Personalized curriculum optimization — Adaptive systems that tailor learning paths. Examples: Knewton, Carnegie Learning.
