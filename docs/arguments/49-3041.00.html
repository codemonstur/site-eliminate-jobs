Farm Equipment Mechanics and Service Technicians

Description

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul farm machinery and vehicles, such as tractors, harvesters, dairy equipment, and irrigation systems. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They reassemble machines and equipment following repair, testing operation and making adjustments, as necessary. They maintain, repair, and overhaul farm machinery and vehicles, such as tractors, harvesters, and irrigation systems. They examine and listen to equipment, read inspection reports, and confer with customers to locate and diagnose malfunctions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
