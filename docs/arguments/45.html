Farming, Fishing, and Forestry

Major group 45 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 14 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

Precision agriculture and AI crop management

Data-driven farming and crop optimization reduce the need for manual farm management. They monitor and optimize crops.

This compresses management and reduces staffing.

This compresses operation and reduces staffing.
