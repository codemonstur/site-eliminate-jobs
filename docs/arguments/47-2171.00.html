Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers

Description

Position and secure steel bars or mesh in concrete forms in order to reinforce concrete. Use a variety of fasteners, rod-bending machines, blowtorches, and hand tools. Includes rod busters. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They position and secure steel bars, rods, cables, or mesh in concrete forms, using fasteners, rod-bending machines, blowtorches, or hand tools. They determine quantities, sizes, shapes, and locations of reinforcing rods from blueprints, sketches, or oral instructions. They space and fasten together rods in forms according to blueprints, using wire and pliers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
