Molecular and Cellular Biologists

Description

Research and study cellular molecules and organelles to understand cell function and organization. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They maintain accurate laboratory records and data. They design molecular or cellular laboratory experiments, oversee their execution, and interpret results. They write grant applications to obtain funding. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
