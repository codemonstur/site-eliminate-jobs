Heating, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration Mechanics and Installers

Description

Install or repair heating, central air conditioning, HVAC, or refrigeration systems, including oil burners, hot-air furnaces, and heating stoves. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They discuss heating or cooling system malfunctions with users to isolate problems or to verify that repairs corrected malfunctions. They test electrical circuits or components for continuity, using electrical test equipment. They repair or replace defective equipment, components, or wiring. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
