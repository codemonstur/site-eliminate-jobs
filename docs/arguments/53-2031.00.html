Flight Attendants

Description

Monitor safety of the aircraft cabin. Provide services to airline passengers, explain safety information, serve food and beverages, and respond to emergency incidents. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They verify that first aid kits and other emergency equipment, including fire extinguishers and oxygen bottles, are in working order. They announce and demonstrate safety and emergency procedures, such as the use of oxygen masks, seat belts, and life jackets. They monitor passenger behavior to identify threats to the safety of the crew and other passengers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
