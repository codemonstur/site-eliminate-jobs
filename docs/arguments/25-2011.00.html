Preschool Teachers, Except Special Education

Description

Instruct preschool-aged students, following curricula or lesson plans, in activities designed to promote social, physical, and intellectual growth. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They teach basic skills, such as color, shape, number and letter recognition, personal hygiene, and social skills. They establish and enforce rules for behavior and procedures for maintaining order. They adapt teaching methods and instructional materials to meet students’ varying needs and interests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
