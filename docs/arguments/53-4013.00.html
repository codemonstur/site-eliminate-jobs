Rail Yard Engineers, Dinkey Operators, and Hostlers

Description

Drive switching or other locomotive or dinkey engines within railroad yard, industrial plant, quarry, construction project, or similar location. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They observe and respond to wayside and cab signals, including color light signals, position signals, torpedoes, flags, and hot box detectors. They inspect engines before and after use to ensure proper operation. They apply and release hand brakes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
