Bridge and Lock Tenders

Description

Operate and tend bridges, canal locks, and lighthouses to permit marine passage on inland waterways, near shores, and at danger points in waterway passages. May supervise such operations. Includes drawbridge operators, lock operators, and slip bridge operators. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They control machinery to open and close canal locks and dams, railroad or highway drawbridges, or horizontally or vertically adjustable bridges. They direct movements of vessels in locks or bridge areas, using signals, telecommunication equipment, or loudspeakers. They observe position and progress of vessels to ensure best use of lock spaces or bridge opening spaces. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
