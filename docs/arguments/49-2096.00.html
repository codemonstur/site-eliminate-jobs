Electronic Equipment Installers and Repairers, Motor Vehicles

Description

Install, diagnose, or repair communications, sound, security, or navigation equipment in motor vehicles. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They estimate costs of repairs, based on parts and labor charges. They install equipment and accessories, such as stereos, navigation equipment, communication equipment, and security systems. They inspect and test electrical or electronic systems to locate and diagnose malfunctions, using visual inspections and testing instruments, such as oscilloscopes and voltmeters. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
