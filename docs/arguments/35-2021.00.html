Food Preparation Workers

Description

Perform a variety of food preparation duties other than cooking, such as preparing cold foods and shellfish, slicing meat, and brewing coffee or tea. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They clean and sanitize work areas, equipment, utensils, dishes, or silverware. They assist cooks and kitchen staff with various tasks as needed, and provide cooks with needed items. They take and record temperature of food and food storage areas, such as refrigerators and freezers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
