Compensation, Benefits, and Job Analysis Specialists

Description

Conduct programs of compensation and benefits and job analysis for employer. May specialize in specific areas, such as position classification and pension programs. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They administer employee insurance, pension, and savings plans, working with insurance brokers and plan carriers. They ensure company compliance with federal and state laws, including reporting requirements. They research employee benefit and health and safety practices, and recommend changes or modifications to existing policies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
