Social and Human Service Assistants

Description

Assist other social and human service providers in providing client services in a wide variety of fields, such as psychology, rehabilitation, or social work, including support for families. May assist clients in identifying and obtaining available benefits and social and community services. May assist social workers with developing, organizing, and conducting programs to prevent and resolve problems relevant to substance abuse, human relationships, rehabilitation, or dependent care. It belongs to the Community and Social Service major group (code 21). They assess clients’ cognitive abilities and physical and emotional needs to determine appropriate interventions. They develop and implement behavioral management and care plans for clients. They keep records or prepare reports for owner or management concerning visits with clients. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
