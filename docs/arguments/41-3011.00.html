Advertising Sales Agents

Description

Sell or solicit advertising space, time, or media in publications, signage, TV, radio, or Internet establishments or public spaces. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They explain to customers how specific types of advertising will help promote their products or services in the most effective way possible. They prepare and deliver sales presentations to new and existing customers to sell new advertising programs and to protect and increase existing advertising. They obtain and study information about clients’ products, needs, problems, advertising history, and business practices to offer effective sales presentations and appropriate product assistance. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
