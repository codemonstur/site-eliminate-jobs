Fraud Examiners, Investigators and Analysts

Description

Obtain evidence, take statements, produce reports, and testify to findings regarding resolution of fraud allegations. May coordinate fraud detection and prevention activities. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They document all investigative activities. They create and maintain logs, records, or databases of information about fraudulent activity. They prepare written reports of investigation findings. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
