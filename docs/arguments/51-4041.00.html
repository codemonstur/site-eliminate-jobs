Machinists

Description

Set up and operate a variety of machine tools to produce precision parts and instruments out of metal. Includes precision instrument makers who fabricate, modify, or repair mechanical instruments. May also fabricate and modify parts to make or repair machine tools or maintain industrial machines, applying knowledge of mechanics, mathematics, metal properties, layout, and machining procedures. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They calculate dimensions or tolerances, using instruments, such as micrometers or vernier calipers. They machine parts to specifications, using machine tools, such as lathes, milling machines, shapers, or grinders. They measure, examine, or test completed units to check for defects and ensure conformance to specifications, using precision instruments, such as micrometers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
