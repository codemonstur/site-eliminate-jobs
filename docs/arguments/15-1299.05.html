Information Security Engineers

Description

Develop and oversee the implementation of information security procedures and policies. Build, maintain and upgrade security technology, such as firewalls, for the safe use of computer networks and the transmission and retrieval of information. Design and implement appropriate security controls to identify vulnerabilities and protect digital files and electronic infrastructures. Monitor and respond to computer security breaches, viruses, and intrusions, and perform forensic investigation. May oversee the assessment of information security systems. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They identify security system weaknesses, using penetration tests. They coordinate monitoring of networks or systems for security breaches or intrusions. They assess the quality of security controls, using performance indicators. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
