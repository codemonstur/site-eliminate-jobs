Exercise Trainers and Group Fitness Instructors

Description

Instruct or coach groups or individuals in exercise activities for the primary purpose of personal fitness. Demonstrate techniques and form, observe participants, and explain to them corrective measures necessary to improve their skills. Develop and implement individualized approaches to exercise. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They observe participants and inform them of corrective measures necessary for skill improvement. They offer alternatives during classes to accommodate different levels of fitness. They monitor participants’ progress and adapt programs as needed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
