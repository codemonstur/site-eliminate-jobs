Furniture Finishers

Description

Shape, finish, and refinish damaged, worn, or used furniture or new high-grade furniture to specified color or finish. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They brush, spray, or hand-rub finishing ingredients, such as paint, oil, stain, or wax, onto and into wood grain and apply lacquer or other sealers. They fill and smooth cracks or depressions, remove marks and imperfections, and repair broken parts, using plastic or wood putty, glue, nails, or screws. They smooth, shape, and touch up surfaces to prepare them for finishing, using sandpaper, pumice stones, steel wool, chisels, sanders, or grinders. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
