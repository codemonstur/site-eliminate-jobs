Fish and Game Wardens

Description

Patrol assigned area to prevent fish and game law violations. Investigate reports of damage to crops or property by wildlife. Compile biological data. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They participate in search-and-rescue operations. They compile and present evidence for court actions. They protect and preserve native wildlife, plants, or ecosystems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
