Healthcare Practitioners and Technical

Description

Healthcare practitioner and technical occupations are the licensed professionals who diagnose, treat, and prevent illness and injury. The group includes physicians, surgeons, registered nurses, pharmacists, dentists, therapists, and laboratory and radiologic technologists. Their work involves examining patients, ordering and interpreting tests, prescribing treatment, performing procedures, and coordinating care. Most occupations require advanced education, ranging from a nursing or pharmacy degree to medical or dental school and residency, plus professional licensure. These professionals deliver the direct clinical care that keeps people healthy and saves lives.

Major group 29 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 96 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

This compresses diagnosis and reduces staffing.
