Curators

Description

Administer collections, such as artwork, collectibles, historic items, or scientific specimens of museums or other institutions. May conduct instructional, research, or public service activities of institution. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They plan and organize the acquisition, storage, and exhibition of collections and related materials, including the selection of exhibition themes and designs, and develop or install exhibit materials. They develop and maintain an institution’s registration, cataloging, and basic record-keeping systems, using computer databases. They plan and conduct special research projects in area of interest or expertise. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
