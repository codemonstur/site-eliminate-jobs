Rock Splitters, Quarry

Description

Separate blocks of rough dimension stone from quarry mass using jackhammers, wedges, or chop saws. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They locate grain line patterns to determine how rocks will split when cut. They remove pieces of stone from larger masses, using jackhammers, wedges, and other tools. They insert wedges and feathers into holes, and drive wedges with sledgehammers to split stone sections from masses. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
