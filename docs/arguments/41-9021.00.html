Real Estate Brokers

Description

Operate real estate office, or work for commercial real estate firm, overseeing real estate transactions. Other duties usually include selling real estate or renting properties and arranging loans. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They obtain agreements from property owners to place properties for sale with real estate firms. They sell, for a fee, real estate owned by others. They compare a property with similar properties that have recently sold to determine its competitive market price. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
