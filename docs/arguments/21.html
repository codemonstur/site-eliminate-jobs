Community and Social Service

Major group 21 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 18 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • AI triage chatbots for counseling and intake — Conversational agents that assess needs and route cases. Examples: Woebot, Wysa.
  • Automated case management and eligibility checks — Systems that process applications and verify eligibility. Examples: Salesforce Nonprofit, Casebook.
  • Telehealth triage and remote support — Remote assessment and care coordination platforms. Examples: Teladoc, Amwell.
  • AI-assisted benefit and service navigation — Guides that match people to programs and services. Examples: Benefits Data Trust, mRelief.
