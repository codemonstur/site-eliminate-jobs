Transportation Inspectors

Description

Transportation Inspectors is an occupation classified within Transportation and Material Moving in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They prepare and submit reports after completion of freight shipments. They inspect shipments to ensure that freight is securely braced and blocked. They record details about freight conditions, handling of freight, and any problems encountered. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
