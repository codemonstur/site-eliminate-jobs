Logisticians

Description

Analyze and coordinate the ongoing logistical functions of a firm or organization. Responsible for the entire life cycle of a product, including acquisition, distribution, internal allocation, delivery, and final disposal of resources. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They maintain and develop positive business relationships with a customer’s key personnel involved in, or directly relevant to, a logistics activity. They develop an understanding of customers’ needs and take actions to ensure that such needs are met. They manage subcontractor activities, reviewing proposals, developing performance specifications, and serving as liaisons between subcontractors and organizations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
