Facilities Managers

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate operations and functionalities of facilities and buildings. May include surrounding grounds or multiple facilities of an organization’s campus. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They monitor the facility to ensure that it remains safe, secure, and well-maintained. They oversee the maintenance and repair of machinery, equipment, and electrical and mechanical systems. They oversee construction and renovation projects to improve efficiency and to ensure that facilities meet environmental, health, and security standards, and comply with government regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
