Cutters and Trimmers, Hand

Description

Use hand tools or hand-held power tools to cut and trim a variety of manufactured items, such as carpet, fabric, stone, glass, or rubber. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They mark or discard items with defects such as spots, stains, scars, snags, chips, scratches, or unacceptable shapes or finishes. They trim excess material or cut threads off finished products, such as cutting loose ends of plastic off a manufactured toy for a smoother finish. They cut, shape, and trim materials, such as textiles, food, glass, stone, and metal, using knives, scissors, and other hand tools, portable power tools, or bench-mounted tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
