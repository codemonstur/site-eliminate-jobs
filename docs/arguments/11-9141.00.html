Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate the selling, buying, leasing, or governance activities of commercial, industrial, or residential real estate properties. Includes managers of homeowner and condominium associations, rented or leased housing units, buildings, or land (including rights-of-way). It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They manage and oversee operations, maintenance, administration, and improvement of commercial, industrial, or residential properties. They direct collection of monthly assessments, rental fees, and deposits and payment of insurance premiums, mortgage, taxes, and incurred operating expenses. They plan, schedule, and coordinate general maintenance, major repairs, and remodeling or construction projects for commercial or residential properties. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
