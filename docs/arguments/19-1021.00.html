Biochemists and Biophysicists

Description

Study the chemical composition or physical principles of living cells and organisms, their electrical and mechanical energy, and related phenomena. May conduct research to further understanding of the complex chemical combinations and reactions involved in metabolism, reproduction, growth, and heredity. May determine the effects of foods, drugs, serums, hormones, and other substances on tissues and vital processes of living organisms. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They share research findings by writing scientific articles or by making presentations at scientific conferences. They teach or advise undergraduate or graduate students or supervise their research. They study physical principles of living cells or organisms and their electrical or mechanical energy, applying methods and knowledge of mathematics, physics, chemistry, or biology. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
