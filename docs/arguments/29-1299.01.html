Naturopathic Physicians

Description

Diagnose, treat, and help prevent diseases using a system of practice that is based on the natural healing capacity of individuals. May use physiological, psychological or mechanical methods. May also use natural medicines, prescription or legend drugs, foods, herbs, or other natural remedies. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They document patients’ histories, including identifying data, chief complaints, illnesses, previous medical or family histories, or psychosocial characteristics. They educate patients about health care management. They advise patients about therapeutic exercise and nutritional medicine regimens. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
