Wind Energy Development Managers

Description

Lead or manage the development and evaluation of potential wind energy business opportunities, including environmental studies, permitting, and proposals. May also manage construction of projects. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They coordinate or direct development, energy assessment, engineering, or construction activities to ensure that wind project needs and objectives are met. They manage wind project costs to stay within budget limits. They lead or support negotiations involving tax agreements or abatements, power purchase agreements, land use, or interconnection agreements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
