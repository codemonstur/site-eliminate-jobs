Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles

Description

Apply blocks, strips, or sheets of shock-absorbing, sound-deadening, or decorative coverings to floors. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They cut covering and foundation materials, according to blueprints and sketches. They trim excess covering materials, tack edges, and join sections of covering material to form tight joint. They cut flooring material to fit around obstructions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
