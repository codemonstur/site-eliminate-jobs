Healthcare Social Workers

Description

Provide individuals, families, and groups with the psychosocial support needed to cope with chronic, acute, or terminal illnesses. Services include advising family caregivers. Provide patients with information and counseling, and make referrals for other services. May also provide case and care management or interventions designed to promote health, prevent disease, and address barriers to access to healthcare. It belongs to the Community and Social Service major group (code 21). They advocate for clients or patients to resolve crises. They educate clients about end-of-life symptoms and options to assist them in making informed decisions. They collaborate with other professionals to evaluate patients’ medical or physical condition and to assess client needs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
