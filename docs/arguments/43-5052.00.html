Postal Service Mail Carriers

Description

Sort and deliver mail for the United States Postal Service (USPS). Deliver mail on established route by vehicle or on foot. Includes postal service mail carriers employed by USPS contractors. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They scan labels on letters or parcels to confirm receipt. They obtain signed receipts for registered, certified, and insured mail, collect associated charges, and complete any necessary paperwork. They return to the post office with mail collected from homes, businesses, and public mailboxes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
