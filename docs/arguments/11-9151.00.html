Social and Community Service Managers

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate the activities of a social service program or community outreach organization. Oversee the program or organization’s budget and policies regarding participant involvement, program requirements, and benefits. Work may involve directing social workers, counselors, or probation officers. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They establish and oversee administrative procedures to meet objectives set by boards of directors or senior management. They direct activities of professional and technical staff members and volunteers. They evaluate the work of staff and volunteers to ensure that programs are of appropriate quality and that resources are used effectively. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
