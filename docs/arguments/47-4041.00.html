Hazardous Materials Removal Workers

Description

Identify, remove, pack, transport, or dispose of hazardous materials, including asbestos, lead-based paint, waste oil, fuel, transmission fluid, radioactive materials, or contaminated soil. Specialized training and certification in hazardous materials handling or a confined entry permit are generally required. May operate earth-moving equipment or trucks. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They remove asbestos or lead from surfaces, using hand or power tools such as scrapers, vacuums, or high-pressure sprayers. They build containment areas prior to beginning abatement or decontamination work. They prepare hazardous material for removal or storage. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
