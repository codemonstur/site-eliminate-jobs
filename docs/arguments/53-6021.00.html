Parking Attendants

Description

Park vehicles or issue tickets for customers in a parking lot or garage. May park or tend vehicles in environments such as a car dealership or rental car facility. May collect fee. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They take numbered tags from customers, locate vehicles, and deliver vehicles, or provide customers with instructions for locating vehicles. They inspect vehicles to detect any damage. They greet customers and open their car doors. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
