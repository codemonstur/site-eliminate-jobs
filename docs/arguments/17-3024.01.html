Robotics Technicians

Description

Build, install, test, or maintain robotic equipment or related automated production systems. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They make repairs to robots or peripheral equipment, such as replacement of defective circuit boards, sensors, controllers, encoders, or servomotors. They troubleshoot robotic systems, using knowledge of microprocessors, programmable controllers, electronics, circuit analysis, mechanics, sensor or feedback systems, hydraulics, or pneumatics. They install, program, or repair programmable controllers, robot controllers, end-of-arm tools, or conveyors. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
