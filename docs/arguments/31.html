Healthcare Support

Description

Healthcare support occupations are the people who assist practitioners and care for patients in settings such as hospitals, clinics, and nursing homes. The group includes nursing assistants, home health and personal care aides, medical assistants, phlebotomists, and pharmacy technicians. Typical duties involve helping patients with daily activities, taking vital signs, collecting samples, preparing exam rooms, and supporting the smooth running of clinical workflow. Most roles require a certificate, postsecondary nondegree award, or on-the-job training rather than a four-year degree. These workers provide the hands-on care and practical support that clinical treatment depends on.

Major group 31 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 20 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Care and service robots

Robots assisting with patient care tasks reduce the need for aides. They handle lifting, delivery, and monitoring.

This compresses care work and reduces staffing.
