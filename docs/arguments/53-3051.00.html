Bus Drivers, School

Description

Drive a school bus to transport students. Ensure adherence to safety rules. May assist students in boarding or exiting. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They comply with traffic regulations to operate vehicles in a safe and courteous manner. They follow safety rules as students board and exit buses or cross streets near bus stops. They check the condition of a vehicle’s tires, brakes, windshield wipers, lights, oil, fuel, water, and safety equipment to ensure that everything is in working order. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
