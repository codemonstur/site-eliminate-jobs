Log Graders and Scalers

Description

Grade logs or estimate the marketable content or value of logs or pulpwood in sorting yards, millpond, log deck, or similar locations. Inspect logs for defects or measure logs to determine volume. It belongs to the Farming, Fishing, and Forestry major group (code 45). They evaluate log characteristics and determine grades, using established criteria. They record data about individual trees or load volumes into tally books or hand-held collection terminals. They measure felled logs or loads of pulpwood to calculate volume, weight, dimensions, and marketable value, using measuring devices and conversion tables. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
