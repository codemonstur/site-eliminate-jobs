Surveyors

Description

Make exact measurements and determine property boundaries. Provide data relevant to the shape, contour, gravitation, location, elevation, or dimension of land or land features on or near the earth’s surface for engineering, mapmaking, mining, land evaluation, construction, and other purposes. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They direct or conduct surveys to establish legal boundaries for properties, based on legal deeds and titles. They prepare and maintain sketches, maps, reports, and legal descriptions of surveys to describe, certify, and assume liability for work performed. They write descriptions of property boundary surveys for use in deeds, leases, or other legal documents. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
