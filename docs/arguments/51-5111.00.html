Prepress Technicians and Workers

Description

Format and proof text and images submitted by designers and clients into finished pages that can be printed. Includes digital and photo typesetting. May produce printing plates. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They generate prepress proofs in digital or other format to approximate the appearance of the final printed piece. They proofread and perform quality control of text and images. They enter, position, and alter text size, using computers, to make up and arrange pages so that printed materials can be produced. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
