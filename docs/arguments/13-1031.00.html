Claims Adjusters, Examiners, and Investigators

Description

Review settled claims to determine that payments and settlements are made in accordance with company practices and procedures. Confer with legal counsel on claims requiring litigation. May also settle insurance claims. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They examine claims forms and other records to determine insurance coverage. They analyze information gathered by investigation and report findings and recommendations. They pay and process claims within designated authority level. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
