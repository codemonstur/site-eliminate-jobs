Animal Scientists

Description

Conduct research in the genetics, nutrition, reproduction, growth, and development of domestic farm animals. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They study nutritional requirements of animals and nutritive values of animal feed materials. They write up or orally communicate research findings to the scientific community, producers, and the public. They develop improved practices in feeding, housing, sanitation, or parasite and disease control of animals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
