Architecture and Engineering

Major group 17 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 59 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • Generative design and AI-assisted engineering — AI that proposes optimized structures and components from constraints. Examples: Autodesk Generative Design, nTopology.
  • Building information modeling (BIM) automation — Automated generation and coordination of building models. Examples: Autodesk Revit, ArchiCAD.
  • Autonomous surveying with drones and LiDAR — Unmanned aerial and laser scanning of sites and terrain. Examples: DJI, Leica Geosystems.
  • AI structural optimization and simulation — Machine learning to test and optimize designs under load. Examples: Ansys, SimScale.
  • Robotic construction planning and scheduling — Automated sequencing and resource planning for builds. Examples: Procore, PlanGrid.
