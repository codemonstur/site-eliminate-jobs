Shampooers

Description

Shampoo and rinse customers’ hair. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They massage, shampoo, and condition patron’s hair and scalp to clean them and remove excess oil. They advise patrons with chronic or potentially contagious scalp conditions to seek medical treatment. They treat scalp conditions and hair loss, using specialized lotions, shampoos, or equipment such as infrared lamps or vibrating equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
