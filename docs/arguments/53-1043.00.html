First-Line Supervisors of Material-Moving Machine and Vehicle Operators

Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of material-moving machine and vehicle operators and helpers. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They enforce safety rules and regulations. They interpret transportation or tariff regulations, shipping orders, safety regulations, or company policies and procedures for workers. They resolve worker problems or collaborate with employees to assist in problem resolution. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
