Tool Grinders, Filers, and Sharpeners

Description

Perform precision smoothing, sharpening, polishing, or grinding of metal objects. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They monitor machine operations to determine whether adjustments are necessary, stopping machines when problems occur. They select and mount grinding wheels on machines, according to specifications, using hand tools and applying knowledge of abrasives and grinding procedures. They set up and operate grinding or polishing machines to grind metal workpieces, such as dies, parts, and tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
