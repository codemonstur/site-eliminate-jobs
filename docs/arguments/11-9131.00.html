Postmasters and Mail Superintendents

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate operational, administrative, management, and support services of a U.S. post office; or coordinate activities of workers engaged in postal and related work in assigned post office. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They monitor employees’ work schedules and attendance for payroll purposes. They organize and supervise activities, such as the processing of incoming and outgoing mail. They resolve customer complaints. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
