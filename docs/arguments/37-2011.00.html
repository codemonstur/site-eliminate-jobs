Janitors and Cleaners, Except Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners

Description

Keep buildings in clean and orderly condition. Perform heavy cleaning duties, such as cleaning floors, shampooing rugs, washing walls and glass, and removing rubbish. Duties may include tending furnace and boiler, performing routine maintenance activities, notifying management of need for repairs, and cleaning snow or debris from sidewalk. It belongs to the Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance major group (code 37). They service, clean, or supply restrooms. They gather and empty trash. They clean building floors by sweeping, mopping, scrubbing, or vacuuming. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
