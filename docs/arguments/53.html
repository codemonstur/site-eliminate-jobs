Transportation and Material Moving

Major group 53 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 57 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

Autonomous vehicles and self-driving trucks

Self-driving cars and trucks reduce the need for drivers. They navigate and transport automatically.

This compresses driving and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Waymo
  • Tesla
  • Wikipedia: Self-driving car

Delivery drones and sidewalk robots

Autonomous delivery systems reduce the need for couriers. They deliver goods automatically.

This compresses delivery and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Starship
  • Zipline
  • Wikipedia: Delivery drone

Automated ports, cranes, and warehouses

Automated port and warehouse operations reduce the need for dockworkers. They handle cargo automatically.

This compresses port work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Konecranes
  • Liebherr
  • Wikipedia: Automated guided vehicle

AI routing and fleet optimization

AI for route and fleet management reduces the need for dispatchers. It optimizes routes and assignments.

This compresses dispatch and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Samsara
  • Geotab
  • Wikipedia: Vehicle routing problem

Autonomous forklifts and material handlers

Self-driving forklifts and handlers reduce the need for operators. They move materials automatically.

This compresses handling and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Seegrid
  • OTTO Motors
  • Wikipedia: Forklift truck
