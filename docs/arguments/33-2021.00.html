Fire Inspectors and Investigators

Description

Inspect buildings to detect fire hazards and enforce local ordinances and state laws, or investigate and gather facts to determine cause of fires and explosions. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They prepare and maintain reports of investigation results, and records of convicted arsonists and arson suspects. They testify in court cases involving fires, suspected arson, and false alarms. They package collected pieces of evidence in securely closed containers, such as bags, crates, or boxes, to protect them. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
