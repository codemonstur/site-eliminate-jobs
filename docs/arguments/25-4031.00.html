Library Technicians

Description

Assist librarians by helping readers in the use of library catalogs, databases, and indexes to locate books and other materials; and by answering questions that require only brief consultation of standard reference. Compile records; sort and shelve books or other media; remove or repair damaged books or other media; register patrons; and check materials in and out of the circulation process. Replace materials in shelving area (stacks) or files. Includes bookmobile drivers who assist with providing services in mobile libraries. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They reserve, circulate, renew, and discharge books and other materials. They answer routine telephone or in-person reference inquiries, referring patrons to librarians for further assistance, when necessary. They help patrons find and use library resources, such as reference materials, audio-visual equipment, computers, and other electronic resources and provide technical assistance when needed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
