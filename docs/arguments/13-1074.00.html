Farm Labor Contractors

Description

Recruit and hire seasonal or temporary agricultural laborers. May transport, house, and provide meals for workers. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They pay wages of contracted farm laborers. They provide food, drinking water, and field sanitation facilities to contracted workers. They recruit and hire agricultural workers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
