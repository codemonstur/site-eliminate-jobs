Painting, Coating, and Decorating Workers

Description

Paint, coat, or decorate articles, such as furniture, glass, plateware, pottery, jewelry, toys, books, or leather. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They apply coatings, such as paint, ink, or lacquer, to protect or decorate workpiece surfaces, using spray guns, pens, or brushes. They examine finished surfaces of workpieces to verify conformance to specifications and retouch any defective areas. They clean and maintain tools and equipment, using solvents, brushes, and rags. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
