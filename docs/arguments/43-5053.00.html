Postal Service Mail Sorters, Processors, and Processing Machine Operators

Description

Prepare incoming and outgoing mail for distribution for the United States Postal Service (USPS). Examine, sort, and route mail. Load, operate, and occasionally adjust and repair mail processing, sorting, and canceling machinery. Keep records of shipments, pouches, and sacks, and perform other duties related to mail handling within the postal service. Includes postal service mail sorters and processors employed by USPS contractors. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They clear jams in sorting equipment. They operate various types of equipment, such as computer scanning equipment, addressographs, mimeographs, optical character readers, and bar-code sorters. They sort odd-sized mail by hand, sort mail that other workers have been unable to sort, and segregate items requiring special handling. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
