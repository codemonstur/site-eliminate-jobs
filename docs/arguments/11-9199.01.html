Regulatory Affairs Managers

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate production activities of an organization to ensure compliance with regulations and standard operating procedures. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They develop regulatory strategies and implementation plans for the preparation and submission of new products. They review all regulatory agency submission materials to ensure timeliness, accuracy, comprehensiveness, or compliance with regulatory standards. They direct the preparation and submission of regulatory agency applications, reports, or correspondence. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
