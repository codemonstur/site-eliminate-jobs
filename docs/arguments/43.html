Office and Administrative Support

Major group 43 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 55 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • Robotic process automation (RPA) — Bots that automate repetitive office tasks. Examples: UiPath, Blue Prism.
  • LLM document processing and data entry — AI that extracts and enters document data. Examples: ABBYY, Hyperscience.
  • AI email drafting and triage — AI that writes and sorts email. Examples: Superhuman, Gmail.
  • Automated scheduling and calendar management — AI scheduling assistants. Examples: Calendly, Motion.
  • Voice assistants and transcription — Speech-to-text and voice-driven tools. Examples: Otter.ai, Dragon.
