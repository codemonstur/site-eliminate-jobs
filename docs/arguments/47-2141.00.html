Painters, Construction and Maintenance

Description

Paint walls, equipment, buildings, bridges, and other structural surfaces, using brushes, rollers, and spray guns. May remove old paint to prepare surface prior to painting. May mix colors or oils to obtain desired color or consistency. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They cover surfaces with dropcloths or masking tape and paper to protect surfaces during painting. They read work orders or receive instructions from supervisors or homeowners to determine work requirements. They apply paint, stain, varnish, enamel, or other finishes to equipment, buildings, bridges, or other structures, using brushes, spray guns, or rollers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
