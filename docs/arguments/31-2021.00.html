Physical Therapist Assistants

Description

Assist physical therapists in providing physical therapy treatments and procedures. May, in accordance with state laws, assist in the development of treatment plans, carry out routine functions, document the progress of treatment, and modify specific treatments in accordance with patient status and within the scope of treatment plans established by a physical therapist. Generally requires formal training. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They instruct, motivate, safeguard, and assist patients as they practice exercises or functional activities. They document patient information, such as notes on their progress. They observe patients during treatments to compile and evaluate data on their responses and progress and provide results to physical therapist in person or through progress notes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
