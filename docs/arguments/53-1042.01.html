Recycling Coordinators

Description

Supervise curbside and drop-off recycling programs for municipal governments or private firms. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They oversee recycling pick-up or drop-off programs to ensure compliance with community ordinances. They maintain logs of recycling materials received or shipped to processing companies. They supervise recycling technicians, community service workers, or other recycling operations employees or volunteers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
