Neurologists

Description

Diagnose, manage, and treat disorders and diseases of the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves, with a primarily nonsurgical focus. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They interview patients to obtain information, such as complaints, symptoms, medical histories, and family histories. They examine patients to obtain information about functional status of areas, such as vision, physical strength, coordination, reflexes, sensations, language skills, cognitive abilities, and mental status. They perform or interpret the outcomes of procedures or diagnostic tests, such as lumbar punctures, electroencephalography, electromyography, and nerve conduction velocity tests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
