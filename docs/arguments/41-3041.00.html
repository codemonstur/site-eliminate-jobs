Travel Agents

Description

Plan and sell transportation and accommodations for customers. Determine destination, modes of transportation, travel dates, costs, and accommodations required. May also describe, plan, and arrange itineraries and sell tour packages. May assist in resolving clients’ travel problems. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They collect payment for transportation and accommodations from customer. They plan, describe, arrange, and sell itinerary tour packages and promotional travel incentives offered by various travel carriers. They converse with customer to determine destination, mode of transportation, travel dates, financial considerations, and accommodations required. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
