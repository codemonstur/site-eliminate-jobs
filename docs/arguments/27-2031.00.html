Dancers

Description

Perform dances. May perform on stage, for broadcasting, or for video recording. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They study and practice dance moves required in roles. They harmonize body movements to rhythm of musical accompaniment. They train, exercise, and attend dance classes to maintain high levels of technical proficiency, physical ability, and physical fitness. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
