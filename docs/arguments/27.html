Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media

Major group 27 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 45 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • Generative AI for image, video, and music — Models that create media from text prompts. Examples: Midjourney, Runway.
  • AI editing, dubbing, and post-production — Automated editing, translation, and voice cloning. Examples: Descript, ElevenLabs.
  • Automated content and copy generation — AI that writes articles, ads, and marketing copy. Examples: Jasper, Copy.ai.
  • Virtual influencers and synthetic media — AI-generated personas and digital avatars. Examples: Synthesia, HeyGen.
  • AI game and level design — Procedural and AI-assisted game content creation. Examples: Unity, Unreal Engine.
