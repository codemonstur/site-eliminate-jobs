Bookkeeping, Accounting, and Auditing Clerks

Description

Compute, classify, and record numerical data to keep financial records complete. Perform any combination of routine calculating, posting, and verifying duties to obtain primary financial data for use in maintaining accounting records. May also check the accuracy of figures, calculations, and postings pertaining to business transactions recorded by other workers. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They operate computers programmed with accounting software to record, store, and analyze information. They check figures, postings, and documents for correct entry, mathematical accuracy, and proper codes. They comply with federal, state, and company policies, procedures, and regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
