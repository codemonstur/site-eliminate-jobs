Business and Financial Operations

Major group 13 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 50 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

  • AI accounting, audit, and reconciliation automation — Systems that categorize transactions, detect anomalies, and reconcile books. Examples: QuickBooks, Xero.
  • Algorithmic trading and robo-advisory — Automated portfolio management and trade execution driven by models. Examples: Betterment, Wealthfront.
  • Automated financial reporting and forecasting — Tools that generate statements and projections from live data. Examples: Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning.
  • LLM tax preparation and compliance review — Language models that prepare returns and check regulatory compliance. Examples: TurboTax, H&R Block.
  • RPA for invoice and expense processing — Bots that capture, validate, and post invoices and expenses. Examples: Bill.com, Expensify.
