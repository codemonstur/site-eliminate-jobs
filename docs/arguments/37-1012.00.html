First-Line Supervisors of Landscaping, Lawn Service, and Groundskeeping Workers

Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of workers engaged in landscaping or groundskeeping activities. Work may involve reviewing contracts to ascertain service, machine, and workforce requirements; answering inquiries from potential customers regarding methods, material, and price ranges; and preparing estimates according to labor, material, and machine costs. It belongs to the Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance major group (code 37). They establish and enforce operating procedures and work standards that will ensure adequate performance and personnel safety. They schedule work for crews, depending on work priorities, crew or equipment availability, or weather conditions. They tour grounds, such as parks, botanical gardens, cemeteries, or golf courses, to inspect conditions of plants and soil. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
