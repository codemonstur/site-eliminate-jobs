Forest and Conservation Workers

Description

Under supervision, perform manual labor necessary to develop, maintain, or protect areas such as forests, forested areas, woodlands, wetlands, and rangelands through such activities as raising and transporting seedlings; combating insects, pests, and diseases harmful to plant life; and building structures to control water, erosion, and leaching of soil. Includes forester aides, seedling pullers, tree planters, and gatherers of nontimber forestry products such as pine straw. It belongs to the Farming, Fishing, and Forestry major group (code 45). They check equipment to ensure that it is operating properly. They fight forest fires or perform prescribed burning tasks under the direction of fire suppression officers or forestry technicians. They perform fire protection or suppression duties, such as constructing fire breaks or disposing of brush. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
