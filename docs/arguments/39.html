Personal Care and Service

Major group 39 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 34 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

Care and companion robots

Robots for elderly and personal care reduce the need for caregivers. They assist with daily tasks and companionship.

This compresses care work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Intuition Robotics
  • PARO
  • Wikipedia: Care robot

AI personal assistants and concierges

Virtual assistants for personal services reduce the need for concierges. They handle requests and scheduling.

This compresses service work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Alexa
  • Google Assistant
  • Wikipedia: Virtual assistant

Automated salon and grooming devices

Robotic and automated grooming tools reduce the need for salon staff. They handle grooming tasks.

This compresses grooming work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Dyson
  • Philips
  • Wikipedia: Hair clipper

Wearable health and wellness monitoring

Devices tracking health and fitness reduce the need for wellness coaches. They monitor and advise.

This compresses coaching and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Apple Watch
  • Fitbit
  • Wikipedia: Wearable technology
