Animal Caretakers

Description

Feed, water, groom, bathe, exercise, or otherwise provide care to promote and maintain the well-being of pets and other animals that are not raised for consumption, such as dogs, cats, race horses, ornamental fish or birds, zoo animals, and mice. Work in settings such as kennels, animal shelters, zoos, circuses, and aquariums. May keep records of feedings, treatments, and animals received or discharged. May clean, disinfect, and repair cages, pens, or fish tanks. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They feed and water animals according to schedules and feeding instructions. They provide treatment to sick or injured animals, or contact veterinarians to secure treatment. They examine and observe animals to detect signs of illness, disease, or injury. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
