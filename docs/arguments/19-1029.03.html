Geneticists

Description

Research and study the inheritance of traits at the molecular, organism or population level. May evaluate or treat patients with genetic disorders. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They supervise or direct the work of other geneticists, biologists, technicians, or biometricians working on genetics research projects. They plan or conduct basic genomic and biological research related to areas such as regulation of gene expression, protein interactions, metabolic networks, and nucleic acid or protein complexes. They prepare results of experimental findings for presentation at professional conferences or in scientific journals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
