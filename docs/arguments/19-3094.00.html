Political Scientists

Description

Study the origin, development, and operation of political systems. May study topics, such as public opinion, political decisionmaking, and ideology. May analyze the structure and operation of governments, as well as various political entities. May conduct public opinion surveys, analyze election results, or analyze public documents. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They teach political science. They maintain current knowledge of government policy decisions. They develop and test theories, using information from interviews, newspapers, periodicals, case law, historical papers, polls, or statistical sources. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
