Education and Childcare Administrators, Preschool and Daycare

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate academic or nonacademic activities of preschools or childcare centers and programs, including before- and after-school care. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They recruit, hire, train, and evaluate primary and supplemental staff and recommend personnel actions for programs and services. They confer with parents and staff to discuss educational activities and policies and students’ behavioral or learning problems. They teach classes or courses or provide direct care to children. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
