Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders

Description

Operate or tend equipment such as cooling and freezing units, refrigerators, batch freezers, and freezing tunnels, to cool or freeze products, food, blood plasma, and chemicals. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They record temperatures, amounts of materials processed, or test results on report forms. They monitor pressure gauges, ammeters, flowmeters, thermometers, or products, and adjust controls to maintain specified conditions, such as feed rate, product consistency, temperature, air pressure, and machine speed. They read dials and gauges on panel control boards to ascertain temperatures, alkalinities, and densities of mixtures, and turn valves to obtain specified mixtures. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
