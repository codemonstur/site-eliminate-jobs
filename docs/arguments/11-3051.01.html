Quality Control Systems Managers

Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate quality assurance programs. Formulate quality control policies and control quality of laboratory and production efforts. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They stop production if serious product defects are present. They review and update standard operating procedures or quality assurance manuals. They monitor performance of quality control systems to ensure effectiveness and efficiency. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
