Recreational Vehicle Service Technicians

Description

Diagnose, inspect, adjust, repair, or overhaul recreational vehicles including travel trailers. May specialize in maintaining gas, electrical, hydraulic, plumbing, or chassis/towing systems as well as repairing generators, appliances, and interior components. Includes workers who perform customized van conversions. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They diagnose and repair furnace or air conditioning systems. They connect electrical systems to outside power sources, and activate switches to test the operation of appliances or light fixtures. They examine or test operation of parts or systems to ensure completeness of repairs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
