Insulation Workers, Floor, Ceiling, and Wall

Description

Line and cover structures with insulating materials. May work with batt, roll, or blown insulation materials. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They measure and cut insulation for covering surfaces, using tape measures, handsaws, power saws, knives, or scissors. They fit, wrap, staple, or glue insulating materials to structures or surfaces, using hand tools or wires. They cover and line structures with blown or rolled forms of materials to insulate against cold, heat, or moisture, using saws, knives, rasps, trowels, blowers, or other tools and implements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
