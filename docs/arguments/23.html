Legal

Major group 23 of the U.S. O*NET-SOC register, containing 8 of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

Ways these jobs could be eliminated

LLM contract review and legal research

Models that read contracts and surface risks and precedents reduce the need for junior lawyers and paralegals. They compress document review.

This reduces staffing for routine legal work.

Examples

  • Harvey
  • LexisNexis
  • Wikipedia: Legal research

Automated discovery and document analysis

AI that sorts and analyzes large document sets reduces the need for document reviewers. It automates classification and extraction.

This compresses discovery work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Relativity
  • DISCO
  • Wikipedia: Electronic discovery

AI drafting of briefs, wills, and filings

Generative tools that draft legal documents reduce the need for drafting lawyers. They produce first drafts from templates and facts.

This compresses drafting work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • LegalZoom
  • DoNotPay
  • Wikipedia: Legal document

Compliance automation and regulatory monitoring

Systems that track regulatory changes and compliance status reduce the need for compliance officers. They automate monitoring and reporting.

This compresses compliance work and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Thomson Reuters
  • ComplyAdvantage
  • Wikipedia: Regulatory compliance

Online dispute resolution platforms

Digital platforms that resolve conflicts without courts reduce the need for litigators. They automate negotiation and mediation.

This compresses dispute resolution and reduces staffing.

Examples

  • Modria
  • Tyler Technologies
  • Wikipedia: Online dispute resolution
