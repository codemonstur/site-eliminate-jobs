## Description

Address organizational sustainability issues, such as waste stream management, green building practices, and green procurement plans. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They develop sustainability project goals, objectives, initiatives, or strategies in collaboration with other sustainability professionals. They monitor or track sustainability indicators, such as energy usage, natural resource usage, waste generation, and recycling. They assess or propose sustainability initiatives, considering factors such as cost effectiveness, technical feasibility, and acceptance. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
