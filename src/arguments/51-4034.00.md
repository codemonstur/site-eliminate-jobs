## Description

Set up, operate, or tend lathe and turning machines to turn, bore, thread, form, or face metal or plastic materials, such as wire, rod, or bar stock. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They adjust machine controls and change tool settings to keep dimensions within specified tolerances. They move controls to set cutting speeds and depths and feed rates, and to position tools in relation to workpieces. They study blueprints, layouts or charts, and job orders for information on specifications and tooling instructions, and to determine material requirements and operational sequences. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
