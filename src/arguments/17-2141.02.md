## Description

Develop new or improved designs for vehicle structural members, engines, transmissions, or other vehicle systems, using computer-assisted design technology. Direct building, modification, or testing of vehicle or components. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They conduct or direct system-level automotive testing. They provide technical direction to other engineers or engineering support personnel. They perform failure, variation, or root cause analyses. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
