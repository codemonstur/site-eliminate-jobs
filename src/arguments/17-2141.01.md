## Description

Design, evaluate, modify, or construct fuel cell components or systems for transportation, stationary, or portable applications. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They plan or conduct experiments to validate new materials, optimize startup protocols, reduce conditioning time, or examine contaminant tolerance. They provide technical consultation or direction related to the development or production of fuel cell systems. They characterize component or fuel cell performances by generating operating maps, defining operating conditions, identifying design refinements, or executing durability assessments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
