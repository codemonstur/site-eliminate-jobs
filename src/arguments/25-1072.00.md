## Description

Demonstrate and teach patient care in classroom and clinical units to nursing students. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They evaluate and grade students' class work, laboratory and clinic work, assignments, and papers. They supervise students' laboratory and clinical work. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
