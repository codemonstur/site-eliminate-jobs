## Description

Design or implement programs and strategies related to water resource issues such as supply, quality, and regulatory compliance issues. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They perform hydrologic, hydraulic, or water quality modeling. They analyze storm water systems to identify opportunities for water resource improvements. They conduct, or oversee the conduct of, investigations on matters such as water storage, wastewater discharge, pollutants, permits, or other compliance and regulatory issues. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
