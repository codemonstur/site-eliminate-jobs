## Description

Organize activities to raise funds or otherwise solicit and gather monetary donations or other gifts for an organization. May design and produce promotional materials. May also raise awareness of the organization's work, goals, and financial needs. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They identify and build relationships with potential donors. They secure commitments of participation or donation from individuals or corporate donors. They write and send letters of thanks to donors. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
