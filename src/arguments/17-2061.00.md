## Description

Research, design, develop, or test computer or computer-related equipment for commercial, industrial, military, or scientific use. May supervise the manufacturing and installation of computer or computer-related equipment and components. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They update knowledge and skills to keep up with rapid advancements in computer technology. They design and develop computer hardware and support peripherals, including central processing units (CPUs), support logic, microprocessors, custom integrated circuits, and printers and disk drives. They confer with engineering staff and consult specifications to evaluate interface between hardware and software and operational and performance requirements of overall system. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
