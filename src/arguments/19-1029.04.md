## Description

Research or study basic principles of plant and animal life, such as origin, relationship, development, anatomy, and functions. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They program and use computers to store, process, and analyze data. They prepare technical and research reports, such as environmental impact reports, and communicate the results to individuals in industry, government, or the general public. They supervise biological technicians and technologists and other scientists. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
