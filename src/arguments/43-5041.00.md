## Description

Read meter and record consumption of electricity, gas, water, or steam. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They read electric, gas, water, or steam consumption meters and enter data in route books or hand-held computers. They upload into office computers all information collected on hand-held computers during meter rounds, or return route books or hand-held computers to business offices so that data can be compiled. They walk or drive vehicles along established routes to take readings of meter dials. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
