## Description

Help electricians by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They strip insulation from wire ends, using wire stripping pliers, and attach wires to terminals for subsequent soldering. They trace out short circuits in wiring, using test meter. They measure, cut, and bend wire and conduit, using measuring instruments and hand tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
