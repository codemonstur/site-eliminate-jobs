## Description

Prepare detailed working diagrams of machinery and mechanical devices, including dimensions, fastening methods, and other engineering information. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They produce three-dimensional models, using computer-aided design (CAD) software. They develop detailed design drawings and specifications for mechanical equipment, dies, tools, and controls, using computer-assisted drafting (CAD) equipment. They check dimensions of materials to be used and assign numbers to the materials. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
