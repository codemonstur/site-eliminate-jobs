## Description

Operate mechanical boom and cable or tower and cable equipment to lift and move materials, machines, or products in many directions. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They move levers, depress foot pedals, or turn dials to operate cranes, cherry pickers, electromagnets, or other moving equipment for lifting, moving, or placing loads. They inspect crane site conditions to determine ground stability. They inspect and adjust crane mechanisms or lifting accessories to prevent malfunctions or damage. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
