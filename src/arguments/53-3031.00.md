## Description

Drive truck or other vehicle over established routes or within an established territory and sell or deliver goods, such as food products, including restaurant take-out items, or pick up or deliver items such as commercial laundry. May also take orders, collect payment, or stock merchandise at point of delivery. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They drive trucks to deliver such items as food, medical supplies, or newspapers. They inform regular customers of new products or services and price changes. They record sales or delivery information on daily sales or delivery record. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
