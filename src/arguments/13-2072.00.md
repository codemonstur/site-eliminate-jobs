## Description

Evaluate, authorize, or recommend approval of commercial, real estate, or credit loans. Advise borrowers on financial status and payment methods. Includes mortgage loan officers and agents, collection analysts, loan servicing officers, loan underwriters, and payday loan officers. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They meet with applicants to obtain information for loan applications and to answer questions about the process. They analyze applicants' financial status, credit, and property evaluations to determine feasibility of granting loans. They approve loans within specified limits, and refer loan applications outside those limits to management for approval. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
