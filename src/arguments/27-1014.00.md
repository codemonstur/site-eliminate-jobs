## Description

Create special effects or animations using film, video, computers, or other electronic tools and media for use in products, such as computer games, movies, music videos, and commercials. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They design complex graphics and animation, using independent judgment, creativity, and computer equipment. They create basic designs, drawings, and illustrations for product labels, cartons, direct mail, or television. They participate in design and production of multimedia campaigns, handling budgeting and scheduling, and assisting with such responsibilities as production coordination, background design, and progress tracking. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
