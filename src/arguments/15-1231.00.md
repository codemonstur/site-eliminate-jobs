## Description

Analyze, test, troubleshoot, and evaluate existing network systems, such as local area networks (LAN), wide area networks (WAN), cloud networks, servers, and other data communications networks. Perform network maintenance to ensure networks operate correctly with minimal interruption. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They back up network data. They configure security settings or access permissions for groups or individuals. They analyze and report computer network security breaches or attempted breaches. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
