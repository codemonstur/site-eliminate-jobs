## Description

Recruit, screen, interview, or place individuals within an organization. May perform other activities in multiple human resources areas. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They interpret and explain human resources policies, procedures, laws, standards, or regulations. They hire employees and process hiring-related paperwork. They maintain current knowledge of Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) and affirmative action guidelines and laws, such as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
