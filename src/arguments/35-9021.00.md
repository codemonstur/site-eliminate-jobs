## Description

Clean dishes, kitchen, food preparation equipment, or utensils. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They wash dishes, glassware, flatware, pots, or pans, using dishwashers or by hand. They maintain kitchen work areas, equipment, or utensils in clean and orderly condition. They place clean dishes, utensils, or cooking equipment in storage areas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
