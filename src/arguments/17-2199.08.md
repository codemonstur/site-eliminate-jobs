## Description

Research, design, develop, or test robotic applications. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They review or approve designs, calculations, or cost estimates. They process or interpret signals or sensor data. They debug robotics programs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
