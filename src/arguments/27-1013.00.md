## Description

Create original artwork using any of a wide variety of media and techniques. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They use materials such as pens and ink, watercolors, charcoal, oil, or computer software to create artwork. They integrate and develop visual elements, such as line, space, mass, color, and perspective, to produce desired effects, such as the illustration of ideas, emotions, or moods. They confer with clients, editors, writers, art directors, and other interested parties regarding the nature and content of artwork to be produced. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
