## Description

Teach academic and social skills to students at the elementary school level. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They instruct students individually and in groups, using teaching methods such as lectures, discussions, and demonstrations. They establish and enforce rules for behavior and procedures for maintaining order among the students. They guide and counsel students with adjustment or academic problems or with special academic interests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
