## Description

Set up, operate, or tend welding, soldering, or brazing machines or robots that weld, braze, solder, or heat treat metal products, components, or assemblies. Includes workers who operate laser cutters or laser-beam machines. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They read blueprints, work orders, or production schedules to determine product or job instructions or specifications. They inspect, measure, or test completed metal workpieces to ensure conformance to specifications, using measuring and testing devices. They record operational information on specified production reports. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
