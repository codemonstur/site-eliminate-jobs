## Description

Stand watch to look for obstructions in path of vessel, measure water depth, turn wheel on bridge, or use emergency equipment as directed by captain, mate, or pilot. Break out, rig, overhaul, and store cargo-handling gear, stationary rigging, and running gear. Perform a variety of maintenance tasks to preserve the painted surface of the ship and to maintain line and ship equipment. Must hold government-issued certification and tankerman certification when working aboard liquid-carrying vessels. Includes able seamen and ordinary seamen. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They maintain government-issued certifications, as required. They handle lines to moor vessels to wharfs, to tie up vessels to other vessels, or to rig towing lines. They stand watch in ships' bows or bridge wings to look for obstructions in a ship's path or to locate navigational aids, such as buoys or lighthouses. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
