## Description

Diagnose, treat, and help prevent mental disorders. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They prescribe, direct, or administer psychotherapeutic treatments or medications to treat mental, emotional, or behavioral disorders. They gather and maintain patient information and records, including social or medical history obtained from patients, relatives, or other professionals. They collaborate with physicians, psychologists, social workers, psychiatric nurses, or other professionals to discuss treatment plans and progress. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
