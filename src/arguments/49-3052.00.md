## Description

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, dirt bikes, or similar motorized vehicles. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They mount, balance, change, or check condition or pressure of tires. They replace defective parts, using hand tools, arbor presses, flexible power presses, or power tools. They dismantle engines and repair or replace defective parts, such as magnetos, carburetors, or generators. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
