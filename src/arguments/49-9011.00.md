## Description

Install, service, or repair automatic door mechanisms and hydraulic doors. Includes garage door mechanics. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They wind large springs with upward motion of arm. They complete required paperwork, such as work orders, according to services performed or required. They repair or replace worn or broken door parts, using hand tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
