## Description

Operate power pumps and auxiliary equipment to produce flow of oil or gas from wells in oil field. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They monitor pumps and flow lines for gas and fluid leaks. They gauge oil and gas production. They start compressor engines and divert oil from storage tanks into compressor units and auxiliary equipment to recover natural gas from oil. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
