## Description

Diagnose, treat, and help prevent injuries that occur during sporting events, athletic training, and physical activities. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They diagnose or treat disorders of the musculoskeletal system. They order and interpret the results of laboratory tests and diagnostic imaging procedures. They record athletes' medical histories, and perform physical examinations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
