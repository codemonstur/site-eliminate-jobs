## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines that knit, loop, weave, or draw in textiles. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They observe woven cloth to detect weaving defects. They thread yarn, thread, and fabric through guides, needles, and rollers of machines for weaving, knitting, or other processing. They remove defects in cloth by cutting and pulling out filling. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
