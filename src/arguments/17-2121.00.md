## Description

Design, develop, and evaluate the operation of marine vessels, ship machinery, and related equipment, such as power supply and propulsion systems. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They perform monitoring activities to ensure that ships comply with international regulations and standards for life-saving equipment and pollution preventatives. They design complete hull and superstructure according to specifications and test data, in conformity with standards of safety, efficiency, and economy. They conduct analyses of ships, such as stability, structural, weight, and vibration analyses. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
