## Description

Perform routine administrative functions such as drafting correspondence, scheduling appointments, organizing and maintaining paper and electronic files, or providing information to callers. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They answer telephones and give information to callers, take messages, or transfer calls to appropriate individuals. They greet visitors or callers and handle their inquiries or direct them to the appropriate persons according to their needs. They create, maintain, and enter information into databases. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
