## Description

Teach courses in law. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They compile, administer, and grade examinations, or assign this work to others. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
