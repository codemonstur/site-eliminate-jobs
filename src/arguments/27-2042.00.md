## Description

Play one or more musical instruments or sing. May perform on stage, for broadcasting, or for sound or video recording. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They perform before live audiences in concerts, recitals, educational presentations, and other social gatherings. They practice performances, individually or in rehearsal with other musicians, to master individual pieces of music or to maintain and improve skills. They specialize in playing a specific family of instruments or a particular type of music. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
