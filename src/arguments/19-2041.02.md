## Description

Collaborate with field and biology staff to oversee the implementation of restoration projects and to develop new products. Process and synthesize complex scientific data into practical strategies for restoration, monitoring or management. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They develop environmental restoration project schedules and budgets. They provide technical direction on environmental planning to energy engineers, biologists, geologists, or other professionals working to develop restoration plans or strategies. They create habitat management or restoration plans, such as native tree restoration and weed control. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
