## Description

Design clothing and accessories. Create original designs or adapt fashion trends. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They sketch rough and detailed drawings of apparel or accessories, and write specifications such as color schemes, construction, material types, and accessory requirements. They examine sample garments on and off models, modifying designs to achieve desired effects. They confer with sales and management executives or with clients to discuss design ideas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
