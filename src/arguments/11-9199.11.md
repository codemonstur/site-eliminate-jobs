## Description

Plan and direct cleanup and redevelopment of contaminated properties for reuse. Does not include properties sufficiently contaminated to qualify as Superfund sites. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They identify environmental contamination sources. They coordinate on-site activities for environmental cleanup or remediation projects to ensure compliance with environmental laws, standards, regulations, or other requirements. They identify and apply for project funding. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
