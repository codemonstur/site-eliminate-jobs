## Description

Assist patrons at entertainment events by performing duties, such as collecting admission tickets and passes from patrons, assisting in finding seats, searching for lost articles, and helping patrons locate such facilities as restrooms and telephones. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They sell or collect admission tickets, passes, or facility memberships from patrons at entertainment events. They clean facilities. They provide assistance with patrons' special needs, such as helping those with wheelchairs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
