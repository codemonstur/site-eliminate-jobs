## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines to saw, cut, shear, slit, punch, crimp, notch, bend, or straighten metal or plastic material. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They measure completed workpieces to verify conformance to specifications, using micrometers, gauges, calipers, templates, or rulers. They examine completed workpieces for defects, such as chipped edges or marred surfaces and sort defective pieces according to types of flaws. They read work orders or production schedules to determine specifications, such as materials to be used, locations of cutting lines, or dimensions and tolerances. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
