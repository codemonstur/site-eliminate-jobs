## Description

Devise methods to improve oil and gas extraction and production and determine the need for new or modified tool designs. Oversee drilling and offer technical advice. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They specify and supervise well modification and stimulation programs to maximize oil and gas recovery. They monitor production rates, and plan rework processes to improve production. They maintain records of drilling and production operations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
