## Description

Diagnose and treat diseases relating to the skin, hair, and nails. May perform both medical and dermatological surgery functions. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They conduct complete skin examinations. They diagnose and treat pigmented lesions such as common acquired nevi, congenital nevi, dysplastic nevi, Spitz nevi, blue nevi, or melanoma. They perform incisional biopsies to diagnose melanoma. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
