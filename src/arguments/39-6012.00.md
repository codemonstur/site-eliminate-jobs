## Description

Assist patrons at hotel, apartment, or office building with personal services. May take messages; arrange or give advice on transportation, business services, or entertainment; or monitor guest requests for housekeeping and maintenance. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They provide information about local features, such as shopping, dining, nightlife, or recreational destinations. They make reservations for patrons, such as for dinner, spa treatments, or golf tee times, and obtain tickets to special events. They provide directions to guests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
