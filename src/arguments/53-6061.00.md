## Description

Provide services to ensure the safety of passengers aboard ships, buses, trains, or within the station or terminal. Perform duties such as explaining the use of safety equipment, serving meals or beverages, or answering questions related to travel. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They perform equipment safety checks prior to departure. They explain and demonstrate safety procedures and safety equipment use. They signal transportation operators to stop or to proceed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
