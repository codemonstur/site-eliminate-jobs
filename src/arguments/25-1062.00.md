## Description

Teach courses pertaining to the culture and development of an area, an ethnic group, or any other group, such as Latin American studies, women's studies, or urban affairs. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as race and ethnic relations, gender studies, and cross-cultural perspectives. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
