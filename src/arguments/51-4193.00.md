## Description

Set up, operate, or tend plating machines to coat metal or plastic products with chromium, zinc, copper, cadmium, nickel, or other metal to protect or decorate surfaces. Typically, the product being coated is immersed in molten metal or an electrolytic solution. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They immerse workpieces in coating solutions or liquid metal or plastic for specified times. They adjust dials to regulate flow of current and voltage supplied to terminals to control plating processes. They inspect coated or plated areas for defects, such as air bubbles or uneven coverage. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
