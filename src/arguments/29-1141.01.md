## Description

Provide advanced nursing care for patients with acute conditions such as heart attacks, respiratory distress syndrome, or shock. May care for pre- and post-operative patients or perform advanced, invasive diagnostic or therapeutic procedures. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They discuss illnesses and treatments with patients and family members. They diagnose acute or chronic conditions that could result in rapid physiological deterioration or life-threatening instability. They perform emergency medical procedures, such as basic cardiac life support (BLS), advanced cardiac life support (ACLS), and other condition-stabilizing interventions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
