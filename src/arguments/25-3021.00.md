## Description

Teach or instruct individuals or groups for the primary purpose of self-enrichment or recreation, rather than for an occupational objective, educational attainment, competition, or fitness. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They instruct students individually and in groups, using various teaching methods, such as lectures, discussions, and demonstrations. They adapt teaching methods and instructional materials to meet students' varying needs and interests. They prepare students for further development by encouraging them to explore learning opportunities and to persevere with challenging tasks. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
