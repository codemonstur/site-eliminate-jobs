## Description

Prepare, sterilize, install, or clean laboratory or healthcare equipment. May perform routine laboratory tasks and operate or inspect equipment. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They examine equipment to detect leaks, worn or loose parts, or other indications of disrepair. They check sterile supplies to ensure that they are not outdated. They record sterilizer test results. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
