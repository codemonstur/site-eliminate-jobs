## Description

Teach occupational, vocational, career, or technical subjects to students at the middle, intermediate, or junior high school level. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They instruct students individually and in groups, using various teaching methods, such as lectures, discussions, and demonstrations. They prepare materials and classrooms for class activities. They establish and enforce rules for behavior and procedures for maintaining order among students. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
