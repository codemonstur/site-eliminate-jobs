## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate the activities of buyers, purchasing officers, and related workers involved in purchasing materials, products, and services. Includes wholesale or retail trade merchandising managers and procurement managers. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They develop and implement purchasing and contract management instructions, policies, and procedures. They locate vendors of materials, equipment or supplies, and interview them to determine product availability and terms of sales. They prepare bid awards requiring board approval. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
