## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of entertainment and recreation related workers. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They assign work schedules, following work requirements, to ensure quality and timely delivery of service. They train workers in proper operational procedures and functions and explain company policies. They furnish customers with information on events or activities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
