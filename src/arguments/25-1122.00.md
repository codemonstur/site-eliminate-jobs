## Description

Teach courses in communications, such as organizational communications, public relations, radio/television broadcasting, and journalism. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
