## Description

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul mobile mechanical, hydraulic, and pneumatic equipment, such as cranes, bulldozers, graders, and conveyors, used in construction, logging, and mining. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They repair and replace damaged or worn parts. They test mechanical products and equipment after repair or assembly to ensure proper performance and compliance with manufacturers' specifications. They operate and inspect machines or heavy equipment to diagnose defects. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
