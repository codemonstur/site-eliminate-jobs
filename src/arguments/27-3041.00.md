## Description

Plan, coordinate, revise, or edit written material. May review proposals and drafts for possible publication. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They verify facts, dates, and statistics, using standard reference sources. They write text, such as headlines, stories, articles, editorials, or newsletters. They read copy or proof to detect and correct errors in spelling, punctuation, and syntax. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
