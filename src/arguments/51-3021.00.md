## Description

Cut, trim, or prepare consumer-sized portions of meat for use or sale in retail establishments. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They prepare and place meat cuts and products in display counter to appear attractive and catch the shopper's eye. They wrap, weigh, label, and price cuts of meat. They cut, trim, bone, tie, and grind meats, such as beef, pork, poultry, and fish, to prepare in cooking form. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
