## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines that extrude and form continuous filaments from synthetic materials, such as liquid polymer, rayon, and fiberglass. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They set up, operate, or tend machines that extrude and form filaments from synthetic materials such as rayon, fiberglass, or liquid polymers. They press buttons to stop machines when processes are complete or when malfunctions are detected. They notify other workers of defects, and direct them to adjust extruding and forming machines. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
