## Description

Compile and record employee time and payroll data. May compute employees' time worked, production, and commission. May compute and post wages and deductions, or prepare paychecks. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They verify attendance, hours worked, and pay adjustments, and post information onto designated records. They process and issue employee paychecks and statements of earnings and deductions. They compute wages and deductions, and enter data into computers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
