## Description

Pediatric Surgeons is an occupation classified within Healthcare Practitioners and Technical in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They analyze patient's medical history, medication allergies, physical condition, and examination results to verify operation's necessity and to determine best procedure. They conduct research to develop and test surgical techniques that can improve operating procedures and outcomes. They consult with patient's other medical care specialists, such as cardiologist and endocrinologist, to determine if surgery is necessary. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
