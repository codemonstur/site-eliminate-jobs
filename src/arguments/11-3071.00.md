## Description

Transportation, Storage, and Distribution Managers is an occupation classified within Management in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They supervise the activities of workers engaged in receiving, storing, testing, and shipping products or materials. They plan, develop, or implement warehouse safety and security programs and activities. They inspect physical conditions of warehouses, vehicle fleets, or equipment and order testing, maintenance, repairs, or replacements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
