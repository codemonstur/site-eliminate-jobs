## Description

Building and grounds cleaning and maintenance occupations are the people who keep structures, interiors, and landscapes clean, safe, and usable. The group includes janitors and building cleaners, housekeepers, groundskeeping workers, landscapers, and pest control workers. Their work involves sweeping, mopping, and disinfecting surfaces, emptying waste, mowing and trimming, applying treatments, and reporting needed repairs. Most roles require only a high school diploma or less and provide substantial on-the-job training. These workers maintain the physical environments where people live, work, and gather.

Major group `37` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**10** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `37` accounts for **5,699,600** workers, or **3.35%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Autonomous cleaning and floor-care robots

Robotic vacuums and scrubbers for facilities reduce the need for cleaners. They clean floors automatically.

This compresses cleaning and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [iRobot](https://www.irobot.com/)
- [Tennant](https://www.tennantco.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Robotic vacuum cleaner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_vacuum_cleaner)

### Robotic lawn mowers and grounds equipment

Autonomous mowing and landscaping reduce the need for groundskeepers. They maintain lawns automatically.

This compresses grounds care and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Husqvarna](https://www.husqvarna.com/)
- [John Deere](https://www.deere.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Robotic lawn mower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_lawn_mower)

### Automated window and facade cleaning

Robotic window and building cleaning reduce the need for window cleaners. They clean facades automatically.

This compresses cleaning and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Skyline Robotics](https://www.skylinerobotics.com/)
- [Serbot](https://www.serbot.ch/)
- [Wikipedia: Window cleaning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_cleaning)

### AI scheduling and route optimization

Automated task scheduling and routing reduce the need for coordinators. They optimize routes and tasks.

This compresses scheduling and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [ServiceTitan](https://www.servicetitan.com/)
- [Jobber](https://getjobber.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Vehicle routing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_routing_problem)
