## Description

Provide specialized nursing care for patients in critical or coronary care units. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They evaluate patients' vital signs or laboratory data to determine emergency intervention needs. They monitor patients for changes in status and indications of conditions such as sepsis or shock and institute appropriate interventions. They administer medications intravenously, by injection, orally, through gastric tubes, or by other methods. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
