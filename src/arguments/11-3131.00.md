## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate the training and development activities and staff of an organization. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They analyze training needs to develop new training programs or modify and improve existing programs. They evaluate instructor performance and the effectiveness of training programs, providing recommendations for improvement. They plan, develop, and provide training and staff development programs, using knowledge of the effectiveness of methods such as classroom training, demonstrations, on-the-job training, meetings, conferences, and workshops. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
