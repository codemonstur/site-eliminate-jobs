## Description

Apply hard tile, stone, and comparable materials to walls, floors, ceilings, countertops, and roof decks. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They align and straighten tile using levels, squares, and straightedges. They finish and dress the joints and wipe excess grout from between tiles, using damp sponge. They cut and shape tile to fit around obstacles and into odd spaces and corners, using hand and power cutting tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
