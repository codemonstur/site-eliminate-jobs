## Description

Format typescript and graphic elements using computer software to produce publication-ready material. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They operate desktop publishing software and equipment to design, lay out, and produce camera-ready copy. They position text and art elements from a variety of databases in a visually appealing way to design print or web pages, using knowledge of type styles and size and layout patterns. They check preliminary and final proofs for errors and make necessary corrections. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
