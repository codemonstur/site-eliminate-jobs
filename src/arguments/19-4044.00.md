## Description

Hydrologic Technicians is an occupation classified within Life, Physical, and Social Science in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They analyze ecological data about the impact of pollution, erosion, floods, and other environmental problems on bodies of water. They answer technical questions from hydrologists, policymakers, or other customers developing water conservation plans. They apply research findings to minimize the environmental impacts of pollution, waterborne diseases, erosion, or sedimentation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
