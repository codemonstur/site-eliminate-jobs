## Description

Conduct research in breeding, physiology, production, yield, and management of crops and agricultural plants or trees, shrubs, and nursery stock, their growth in soils, and control of pests; or study the chemical, physical, biological, and mineralogical composition of soils as they relate to plant or crop growth. May classify and map soils and investigate effects of alternative practices on soil and crop productivity. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They communicate research or project results to other professionals or the public or teach related courses, seminars, or workshops. They develop methods of conserving or managing soil that can be applied by farmers or forestry companies. They provide information or recommendations to farmers or other landowners regarding ways in which they can best use land, promote plant growth, or avoid or correct problems such as erosion. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
