Major group `39` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**34** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Care and companion robots** — Robots for elderly and personal care. Examples: [Intuition Robotics](https://www.intuitionrobotics.com/), [PARO](https://www.parorobots.com/).
- **AI personal assistants and concierges** — Virtual assistants for personal services. Examples: [Alexa](https://www.alexa.com/), [Google Assistant](https://assistant.google.com/).
- **Automated salon and grooming devices** — Robotic and automated grooming tools. Examples: [Dyson](https://www.dyson.com/), [Philips](https://www.philips.com/).
- **Wearable health and wellness monitoring** — Devices tracking health and fitness. Examples: [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch/), [Fitbit](https://www.fitbit.com/).
