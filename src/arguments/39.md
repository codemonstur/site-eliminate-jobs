## Description

Personal care and service occupations are the people who provide grooming, wellness, hospitality, and other services directly to individuals. The group includes hairdressers, barbers, cosmetologists, fitness trainers, childcare workers, baggage porters, and concierges. Typical duties involve consulting with clients, performing treatments or services, scheduling appointments, ensuring comfort, and maintaining clean and welcoming spaces. Most roles require a license, certificate, or moderate training, and many rely heavily on interpersonal skill. These workers help people look, feel, and live better in their day-to-day lives.

Major group `39` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**34** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `39` accounts for **4,549,600** workers, or **2.67%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Care and companion robots

Robots for elderly and personal care reduce the need for caregivers. They assist with daily tasks and companionship.

This compresses care work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Intuition Robotics](https://www.intuitionrobotics.com/)
- [PARO](https://www.parorobots.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Care robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Care_robot)

### AI personal assistants and concierges

Virtual assistants for personal services reduce the need for concierges. They handle requests and scheduling.

This compresses service work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Alexa](https://www.alexa.com/)
- [Google Assistant](https://assistant.google.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Virtual assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_assistant)

### Automated salon and grooming devices

Robotic and automated grooming tools reduce the need for salon staff. They handle grooming tasks.

This compresses grooming work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Dyson](https://www.dyson.com/)
- [Philips](https://www.philips.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Hair clipper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_clipper)

### Wearable health and wellness monitoring

Devices tracking health and fitness reduce the need for wellness coaches. They monitor and advise.

This compresses coaching and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch/)
- [Fitbit](https://www.fitbit.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Wearable technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wearable_technology)
