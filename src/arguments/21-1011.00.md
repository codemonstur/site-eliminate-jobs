## Description

Counsel and advise individuals with alcohol, tobacco, drug, or other problems, such as gambling and eating disorders. May counsel individuals, families, or groups or engage in prevention programs. It belongs to the Community and Social Service major group (code 21). They counsel clients or patients, individually or in group sessions, to assist in overcoming dependencies, adjusting to life, or making changes. They attend training sessions to increase knowledge and skills. They develop client treatment plans based on research, clinical experience, and client histories. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
