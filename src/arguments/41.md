## Description

Sales and related occupations are the people who sell goods, services, and ideas to customers on behalf of businesses and organizations. The group includes retail salespersons, cashiers, real estate brokers and agents, insurance and securities sales representatives, and telemarketers. Their work involves identifying customer needs, demonstrating products, negotiating terms, processing transactions, and following up to build lasting accounts. Most entry-level roles require a high school diploma and learn through on-the-job training, though specialized sales often require licensing or a degree. These workers connect what an organization offers to the people who buy it.

Major group `41` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**23** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### AI sales agents and chatbots

Conversational AI for sales and support reduces the need for sales reps. They handle inquiries and qualification.

This compresses sales work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Drift](https://www.drift.com/)
- [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Chatbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot)

### Automated pricing and promotion optimization

Dynamic pricing and promotion engines reduce the need for pricing analysts. They optimize prices automatically.

This compresses pricing work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Prisync](https://prisync.com/)
- [Competera](https://competera.net/)
- [Wikipedia: Dynamic pricing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_pricing)

### Recommendation and personalization engines

Systems that personalize product suggestions reduce the need for sales staff. They recommend products automatically.

This compresses selling and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/)
- [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Recommender system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recommender_system)

### Virtual shopping assistants

AI helpers for online shopping reduce the need for sales associates. They assist customers automatically.

This compresses sales support and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/)
- [Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Virtual assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_assistant)

### AI lead scoring and CRM automation

Automated lead qualification and CRM updates reduce the need for sales ops. They score and route leads automatically.

This compresses sales ops and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/)
- [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Customer relationship management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_relationship_management)
