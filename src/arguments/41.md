Major group `41` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**23** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **AI sales agents and chatbots** — Conversational AI for sales and support. Examples: [Drift](https://www.drift.com/), [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/).
- **Automated pricing and promotion optimization** — Dynamic pricing and promotion engines. Examples: [Prisync](https://prisync.com/), [Competera](https://competera.net/).
- **Recommendation and personalization engines** — Systems that personalize product suggestions. Examples: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/), [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/).
- **Virtual shopping assistants** — AI helpers for online shopping. Examples: [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/), [Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/).
- **AI lead scoring and CRM automation** — Automated lead qualification and CRM updates. Examples: [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/), [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/).
