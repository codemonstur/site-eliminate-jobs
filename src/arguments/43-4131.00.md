## Description

Interview loan applicants to elicit information; investigate applicants' backgrounds and verify references; prepare loan request papers; and forward findings, reports, and documents to appraisal department. Review loan papers to ensure completeness, and complete transactions between loan establishment, borrowers, and sellers upon approval of loan. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They interview loan applicants to obtain personal and financial data and to assist in completing applications. They answer questions and advise customers regarding loans and transactions. They submit loan applications with recommendation for underwriting approval. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
