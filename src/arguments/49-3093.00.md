## Description

Repair and replace tires. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They unbolt and remove wheels from vehicles, using lug wrenches or other hand or power tools. They place wheels on balancing machines to determine counterweights required to balance wheels. They identify tire size and ply and inflate tires accordingly. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
