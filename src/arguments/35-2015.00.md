## Description

Prepare and cook to order a variety of foods that require only a short preparation time. May take orders from customers and serve patrons at counters or tables. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They clean food preparation equipment, work areas, and counters or tables. They restock kitchen supplies, rotate food, and stamp the time and date on food in coolers. They complete orders from steam tables, placing food on plates and serving customers at tables or counters. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
