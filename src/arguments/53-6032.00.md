## Description

Service aircraft with fuel. May de-ice aircraft, refill water and cooling agents, empty sewage tanks, service air and oxygen systems, or clean and polish exterior. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They guide aircraft to designated areas using hand signals, batons, or other methods. They radio to flight dispatchers or other personnel to discuss incoming or outgoing aircraft. They complete forms describing tasks completed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
