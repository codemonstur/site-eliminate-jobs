## Description

Prepare or serve specialty coffee or other beverages. Serve food such as baked goods or sandwiches to patrons. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They clean or sanitize work areas, utensils, or equipment. They receive and process customer payments. They prepare or serve hot or cold beverages, such as coffee, espresso drinks, blended coffees, or teas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
