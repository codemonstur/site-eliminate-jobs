## Description

Install, adjust, or maintain mobile electronics communication equipment, including sound, sonar, security, navigation, and surveillance systems on trains, watercraft, or other mobile equipment. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They inspect and test electrical systems and equipment to locate and diagnose malfunctions, using visual inspections, testing devices, and computer software. They reassemble and test equipment after repairs. They adjust, repair, or replace defective wiring and relays in ignition, lighting, air-conditioning, and safety control systems, using electrician's tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
