## Description

Sell life, property, casualty, health, automotive, or other types of insurance. May refer clients to independent brokers, work as an independent broker, or be employed by an insurance company. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They customize insurance programs to suit individual customers, often covering a variety of risks. They sell various types of insurance policies to businesses and individuals on behalf of insurance companies, including automobile, fire, life, property, medical and dental insurance, or specialized policies, such as marine, farm/crop, and medical malpractice. They explain features, advantages, and disadvantages of various policies to promote sale of insurance plans. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
