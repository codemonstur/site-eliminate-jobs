## Description

Communicate and coordinate with management, shareholders, customers, and employees to address sustainability issues. Enact or oversee a corporate sustainability strategy. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They monitor and evaluate effectiveness of sustainability programs. They develop or execute strategies to address issues such as energy use, resource conservation, recycling, pollution reduction, waste elimination, transportation, education, and building design. They develop, or oversee the development of, sustainability evaluation or monitoring systems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
