## Description

Drive logging tractor or wheeled vehicle equipped with one or more accessories, such as bulldozer blade, frontal shear, grapple, logging arch, cable winches, hoisting rack, or crane boom, to fell tree; to skid, load, unload, or stack logs; or to pull stumps or clear brush. Includes operating stand-alone logging machines, such as log chippers. It belongs to the Farming, Fishing, and Forestry major group (code 45). They inspect equipment for safety prior to use, and perform necessary basic maintenance tasks. They control hydraulic tractors equipped with tree clamps and booms to lift, swing, and bunch sheared trees. They grade logs according to characteristics such as knot size and straightness, and according to established industry or company standards. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
