## Description

Command or supervise operations of ships and water vessels, such as tugboats and ferryboats. Required to hold license issued by U.S. Coast Guard. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They direct courses and speeds of ships, based on specialized knowledge of local winds, weather, water depths, tides, currents, and hazards. They prevent ships under navigational control from engaging in unsafe operations. They serve as a vessel's docking master upon arrival at a port or at a berth. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
