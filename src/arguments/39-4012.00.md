## Description

Crematory Operators is an occupation classified within Personal Care and Service in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They clean the crematorium, including tables, floors, and equipment. They document divided remains to ensure parts are not misplaced. They embalm, dress, or otherwise prepare the deceased for viewing. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
