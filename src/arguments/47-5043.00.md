## Description

Operate machinery to install roof support bolts in underground mine. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They drill bolt holes into roofs at specified distances from ribs or adjacent bolts. They pull down loose rock that cannot be supported. They position bolting machines, and insert drill bits into chucks. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
