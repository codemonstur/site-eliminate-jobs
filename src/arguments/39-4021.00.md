## Description

Perform a variety of tasks during funeral, such as placing casket in parlor or chapel prior to service, arranging floral offerings or lights around casket, directing or escorting mourners, closing casket, and issuing and storing funeral equipment. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They greet people at the funeral home. They perform a variety of tasks during funerals to assist funeral directors and to ensure that services run smoothly and as planned. They close caskets at appropriate point in services. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
