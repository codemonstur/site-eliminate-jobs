## Description

Appraise real estate, exclusively, and estimate its fair value. May assess taxes in accordance with prescribed schedules. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They compute final estimation of property values, taking into account such factors as depreciation, replacement costs, value comparisons of similar properties, and income potential. They prepare written reports that estimate property values, outline methods by which the estimations were made, and meet appraisal standards. They photograph interiors and exteriors of properties to assist in estimating property value, substantiate findings, and complete appraisal reports. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
