## Description

Operate hand-controlled mechanisms to pour and regulate the flow of molten metal into molds to produce castings or ingots. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They pour and regulate the flow of molten metal into molds and forms to produce ingots or other castings, using ladles or hand-controlled mechanisms. They read temperature gauges and observe color changes, adjusting furnace flames, torches, or electrical heating units as necessary to melt metal to specifications. They remove solidified steel or slag from pouring nozzles, using long bars or oxygen burners. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
