## Description

Observe, research, and interpret astronomical phenomena to increase basic knowledge or apply such information to practical problems. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They analyze research data to determine its significance, using computers. They present research findings at scientific conferences and in papers written for scientific journals. They study celestial phenomena, using a variety of ground-based and space-borne telescopes and scientific instruments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
