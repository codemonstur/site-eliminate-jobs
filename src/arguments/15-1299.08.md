## Description

Design and develop solutions to complex applications problems, system administration issues, or network concerns. Perform systems management and integration functions. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They communicate with staff or clients to understand specific system requirements. They investigate system component suitability for specified purposes, and make recommendations regarding component use. They provide customers or installation teams guidelines for implementing secure systems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
