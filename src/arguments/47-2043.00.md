## Description

Scrape and sand wooden floors to smooth surfaces using floor scraper and floor sanding machine, and apply coats of finish. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They buff and vacuum floors to ensure their cleanliness prior to the application of finish. They scrape and sand floor edges and areas inaccessible to floor sanders, using scrapers, disk-type sanders, and sandpaper. They inspect floors for smoothness. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
