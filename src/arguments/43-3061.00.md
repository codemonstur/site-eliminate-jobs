## Description

Compile information and records to draw up purchase orders for procurement of materials and services. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They track the status of requisitions, contracts, and orders. They perform buying duties when necessary. They prepare purchase orders and send copies to suppliers and to departments originating requests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
