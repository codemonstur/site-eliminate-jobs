## Description

Serve food to individuals outside of a restaurant environment, such as in hotel rooms, hospital rooms, residential care facilities, or cars. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They place food servings on plates or trays according to orders or instructions. They clean or sterilize dishes, kitchen utensils, equipment, or facilities. They monitor food distribution, ensuring that meals are delivered to the correct recipients and that guidelines, such as those for special diets, are followed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
