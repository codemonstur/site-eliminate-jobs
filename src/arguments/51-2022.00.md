## Description

Assemble or modify electrical or electronic equipment, such as computers, test equipment telemetering systems, electric motors, and batteries. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They inspect or test wiring installations, assemblies, or circuits for resistance factors or for operation, and record results. They assemble electrical or electronic systems or support structures and install components, units, subassemblies, wiring, or assembly casings, using rivets, bolts, soldering or micro-welding equipment. They explain assembly procedures or techniques to other workers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
