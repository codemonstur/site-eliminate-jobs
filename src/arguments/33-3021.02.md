## Description

Collect evidence at crime scene, classify and identify fingerprints, and photograph evidence for use in criminal and civil cases. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They photograph crime or accident scenes for evidence records. They maintain records of evidence and write and review reports. They submit evidence to supervisors, crime labs, or court officials for legal proceedings. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
