## Description

Prepare meals in private homes. Includes personal chefs. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They peel, wash, trim, and cook vegetables and meats, and bake breads and pastries. They cool, package, label, and freeze foods for later consumption and provide instructions for reheating. They plan menus according to employers' needs and diet restrictions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
