## Description

Research or study range land management practices to provide sustained production of forage, livestock, and wildlife. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They tailor conservation plans to landowners' goals, such as livestock support, wildlife, or recreation. They study grazing patterns to determine number and kind of livestock that can be most profitably grazed and to determine the best grazing seasons. They maintain soil stability and vegetation for non-grazing uses, such as wildlife habitats and outdoor recreation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
