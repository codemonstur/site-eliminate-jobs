## Description

Work below surface of water, using surface-supplied air or scuba equipment to inspect, repair, remove, or install equipment and structures. May use a variety of power and hand tools, such as drills, sledgehammers, torches, and welding equipment. May conduct tests or experiments, rig explosives, or photograph structures or marine life. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They check and maintain diving equipment, such as helmets, masks, air tanks, harnesses, or gauges. They take appropriate safety precautions, such as monitoring dive lengths and depths and registering with authorities before diving expeditions begin. They communicate with workers on the surface while underwater, using signal lines or telephones. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
