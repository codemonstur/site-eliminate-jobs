## Description

Assist patients in obtaining services, understanding policies and making health care decisions. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They coordinate communication between patients, family members, medical staff, administrative staff, or regulatory agencies. They interview patients or their representatives to identify problems relating to care. They maintain knowledge of community services and resources available to patients. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
