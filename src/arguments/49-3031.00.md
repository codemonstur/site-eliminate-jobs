## Description

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul buses and trucks, or maintain and repair any type of diesel engines. Includes mechanics working primarily with automobile or marine diesel engines. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They use handtools, such as screwdrivers, pliers, wrenches, pressure gauges, or precision instruments, as well as power tools, such as pneumatic wrenches, lathes, welding equipment, or jacks and hoists. They inspect brake systems, steering mechanisms, wheel bearings, and other important parts to ensure that they are in proper operating condition. They raise trucks, buses, and heavy parts or equipment using hydraulic jacks or hoists. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
