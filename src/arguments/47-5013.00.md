## Description

Operate equipment to increase oil flow from producing wells or to remove stuck pipe, casing, tools, or other obstructions from drilling wells. Includes fishing-tool technicians. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They maintain and perform safety inspections on equipment and tools. They operate controls that raise derricks or level rigs. They listen to engines, rotary chains, or other equipment to detect faulty operations or unusual well conditions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
