## Description

Apply knowledge of general preventive medicine and public health issues to promote health care to groups or individuals, and aid in the prevention or reduction of risk of disease, injury, disability, or death. May practice population-based medicine or diagnose and treat patients in the context of clinical health promotion and disease prevention. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They direct or manage prevention programs in specialty areas such as aerospace, occupational, infectious disease, and environmental medicine. They document or review comprehensive patients' histories with an emphasis on occupation or environmental risks. They identify groups at risk for specific preventable diseases or injuries. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
