## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of workers engaged in preparing and serving food. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They resolve customer complaints regarding food service. They train workers in food preparation, and in service, sanitation, and safety procedures. They assign duties, responsibilities, and work stations to employees in accordance with work requirements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
