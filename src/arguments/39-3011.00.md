## Description

Operate table games. Stand or sit behind table and operate games of chance by dispensing the appropriate number of cards or blocks to players, or operating other gambling equipment. Distribute winnings or collect players' money or chips. May compare the house's hand against players' hands. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They pay winnings or collect losing bets as established by the rules and procedures of a specific game. They greet customers and make them feel welcome. They exchange paper currency for playing chips or coin money. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
