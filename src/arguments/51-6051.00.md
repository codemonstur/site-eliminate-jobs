## Description

Sew, join, reinforce, or finish, usually with needle and thread, a variety of manufactured items. Includes weavers and stitchers. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They select thread, twine, cord, or yarn to be used, and thread needles. They measure and align parts, fasteners, or trimmings, following seams, edges, or markings on parts. They trim excess threads or edges of parts, using scissors or knives. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
