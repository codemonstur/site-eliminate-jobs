## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of security workers and security guards. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They investigate disturbances on the premises, such as security alarms, altercations, and suspicious activity. They patrol the premises to prevent or detect intrusion, protect property, or preserve order. They monitor and authorize entry of employees, visitors, or other persons. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
