## Description

Assist lawyers by investigating facts, preparing legal documents, or researching legal precedent. Conduct research to support a legal proceeding, to formulate a defense, or to initiate legal action. It belongs to the Legal major group (code 23). They prepare affidavits or other documents, such as legal correspondence, and organize and maintain documents in paper or electronic filing system. They prepare, edit, or review legal documents, including legislation, briefs, pleadings, appeals, wills, contracts, and real estate closing statements. They investigate facts and law of cases and search pertinent sources, such as public records and internet sources, to determine causes of action and to prepare cases. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
