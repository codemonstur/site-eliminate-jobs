## Description

Edit moving images on film, video, or other media. May work with a producer or director to organize images for final production. May edit or synchronize soundtracks with images. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They organize and string together raw footage into a continuous whole according to scripts or the instructions of directors and producers. They edit films and videotapes to insert music, dialogue, and sound effects, to arrange films into sequences, and to correct errors, using editing equipment. They select and combine the most effective shots of each scene to form a logical and smoothly running story. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
