## Description

Provide inpatient care predominantly in settings such as medical wards, acute care units, intensive care units, rehabilitation centers, or emergency rooms. Manage and coordinate patient care throughout treatment. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They diagnose, treat, or provide continuous care to hospital inpatients. They prescribe medications or treatment regimens to hospital inpatients. They order or interpret the results of tests such as laboratory tests and radiographs (x-rays). Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
