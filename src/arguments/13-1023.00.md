## Description

Purchase machinery, equipment, tools, parts, supplies, or services necessary for the operation of an establishment. Purchase raw or semifinished materials for manufacturing. May negotiate contracts. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They monitor and follow applicable laws and regulations. They purchase the highest quality merchandise at the lowest possible price and in correct amounts. They formulate policies and procedures for bid proposals and procurement of goods and services. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
