## Description

Assess and treat persons with speech, language, voice, and fluency disorders. May select alternative communication systems and teach their use. May perform research related to speech and language problems. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They evaluate hearing or speech and language test results, barium swallow results, or medical or background information to diagnose and plan treatment for speech, language, fluency, voice, or swallowing disorders. They write reports and maintain proper documentation of information, such as client Medicaid or billing records or caseload activities, including the initial evaluation, treatment, progress, and discharge of clients. They monitor patients' progress and adjust treatments accordingly. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
