**59** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **AI agents for planning, scheduling, and resource allocation** — Autonomous software agents that optimize staffing, calendars, and budgets without human schedulers. Examples: [AutoGPT](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT), [Microsoft Copilot Studio](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/microsoft-copilot-studio).
- **Automated performance analytics and workforce dashboards** — Systems that continuously track KPIs and surface management insights automatically. Examples: [Tableau](https://www.tableau.com/), [Power BI](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/).
- **LLM-based decision support and executive briefing** — Large language models that synthesize reports and recommend actions for leaders. Examples: [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/), [Claude](https://claude.ai/).
- **Robotic process automation for approvals and compliance** — Bots that route, validate, and approve routine administrative workflows. Examples: [UiPath](https://www.uipath.com/), [Automation Anywhere](https://www.automationanywhere.com/).
- **Autonomous project-management and OKR tracking tools** — Software that plans sprints, tracks objectives, and flags risks automatically. Examples: [Asana](https://asana.com/), [Monday.com](https://monday.com/).
