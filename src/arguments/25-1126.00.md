## Description

Teach courses in philosophy, religion, and theology. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students and the community on topics such as ethics, logic, and contemporary religious thought. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
