## Description

Assemble, install, or maintain solar photovoltaic (PV) systems on roofs or other structures in compliance with site assessment and schematics. May include measuring, cutting, assembling, and bolting structural framing and solar modules. May perform minor electrical work such as current checks. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They install photovoltaic (PV) systems in accordance with codes and standards, using drawings, schematics, and instructions. They check electrical installation for proper wiring, polarity, grounding, or integrity of terminations. They identify electrical, environmental, and safety hazards associated with photovoltaic (PV) installations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
