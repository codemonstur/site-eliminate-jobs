## Description

Lay out, machine, fit, and assemble castings and parts to metal or plastic foundry patterns, core boxes, or match plates. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They read and interpret blueprints or drawings of parts to be cast or patterns to be made, compute dimensions, and plan operational sequences. They verify conformance of patterns or template dimensions to specifications, using measuring instruments such as calipers, scales, and micrometers. They assemble pattern sections, using hand tools, bolts, screws, rivets, glue, or welding equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
