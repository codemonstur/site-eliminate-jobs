## Description

Apply theory and principles of mechanical engineering to modify, develop, test, or adjust machinery and equipment under direction of engineering staff or physical scientists. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They assemble or disassemble complex mechanical systems. They interpret engineering sketches, specifications, or drawings. They calculate required capacities for equipment of proposed system to obtain specified performance and submit data to engineering personnel for approval. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
