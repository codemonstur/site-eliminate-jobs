## Description

Officiate at competitive athletic or sporting events. Detect infractions of rules and decide penalties according to established regulations. Includes all sporting officials, referees, and competition judges. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They officiate at sporting events, games, or competitions, to maintain standards of play and to ensure that game rules are observed. They inspect game sites for compliance with regulations or safety requirements. They resolve claims of rule infractions or complaints by participants and assess any necessary penalties, according to regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
