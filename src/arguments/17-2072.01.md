## Description

Design and implement radio frequency identification device (RFID) systems used to track shipments or goods. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They identify operational requirements for new systems to inform selection of technological solutions. They integrate tags, readers, or software in radio frequency identification device (RFID) designs. They perform systems analysis or programming of radio frequency identification device (RFID) technology. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
