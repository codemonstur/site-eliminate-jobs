## Description

Lay pipe for storm or sanitation sewers, drains, and water mains. Perform any combination of the following tasks: grade trenches or culverts, position pipe, or seal joints. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They grade or level trench bases, using tamping machines or hand tools. They dig trenches to desired or required depths, by hand or using trenching tools. They cut pipes to required lengths. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
