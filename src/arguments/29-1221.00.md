## Description

Diagnose, treat, and help prevent diseases and injuries in children. May refer patients to specialists for further diagnosis or treatment, as needed. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They prescribe or administer treatment, therapy, medication, vaccination, and other specialized medical care to treat or prevent illness, disease, or injury in infants and children. They examine children regularly to assess their growth and development. They treat children who have minor illnesses, acute and chronic health problems, and growth and development concerns. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
