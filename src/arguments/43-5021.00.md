## Description

Pick up and deliver messages, documents, packages, and other items between offices or departments within an establishment or directly to other business concerns, traveling by foot, bicycle, motorcycle, automobile, or public conveyance. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They deliver and pick up medical records, lab specimens, and medications to and from hospitals and other medical facilities. They walk, ride bicycles, drive vehicles, or use public conveyances to reach destinations to deliver messages or materials. They load vehicles with listed goods, ensuring goods are loaded correctly and taking precautions with hazardous goods. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
