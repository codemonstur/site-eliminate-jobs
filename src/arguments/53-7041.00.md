## Description

Operate or tend hoists or winches to lift and pull loads using power-operated cable equipment. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They signal and assist other workers loading or unloading materials. They observe equipment gauges and indicators and hand signals of other workers to verify load positions or depths. They start engines of hoists or winches and use levers and pedals to wind or unwind cable on drums. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
