## Description

Prepare bodies for interment in conformity with legal requirements. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They conform to laws of health and sanitation and ensure that legal requirements concerning embalming are met. They apply cosmetics to impart lifelike appearance to the deceased. They join lips, using needles and thread or wire. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
