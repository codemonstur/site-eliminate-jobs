## Description

Escort individuals or groups on sightseeing tours or through places of interest, such as industrial establishments, public buildings, and art galleries. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They describe tour points of interest to group members, and respond to questions. They escort individuals or groups on cruises, sightseeing tours, or through places of interest, such as industrial establishments, public buildings, or art galleries. They monitor visitors' activities to ensure compliance with establishment or tour regulations and safety practices. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
