## Description

Prepare medications under the direction of a pharmacist. May measure, mix, count out, label, and record amounts and dosages of medications according to prescription orders. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They receive written prescription or refill requests and verify that information is complete and accurate. They enter prescription information into computer databases. They establish or maintain patient profiles, including lists of medications taken by individual patients. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
