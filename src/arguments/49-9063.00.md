## Description

Repair percussion, stringed, reed, or wind instruments. May specialize in one area, such as piano tuning. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They adjust string tensions to tune instruments, using hand tools and electronic tuning devices. They compare instrument pitches with tuning tool pitches to tune instruments. They play instruments to evaluate their sound quality and to locate any defects. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
