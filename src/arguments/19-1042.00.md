## Description

Conduct research dealing with the understanding of human diseases and the improvement of human health. Engage in clinical investigation, research and development, or other related activities. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They follow strict safety procedures when handling toxic materials to avoid contamination. They evaluate effects of drugs, gases, pesticides, parasites, and microorganisms at various levels. They plan and direct studies to investigate human or animal disease, preventive methods, and treatments for disease. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
