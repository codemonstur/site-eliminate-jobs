## Description

Prepare and sort materials or products for recycling. Identify and remove hazardous substances. Dismantle components of products such as appliances. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They sort materials, such as metals, glass, wood, paper or plastics, into appropriate containers for recycling. They clean recycling yard by sweeping, raking, picking up broken glass and loose paper debris, or moving barrels and bins. They operate forklifts, pallet jacks, power lifts, or front-end loaders to load bales, bundles, or other heavy items onto trucks for shipping to smelters or other recycled materials processing facilities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
