## Description

Review, evaluate, and analyze work environments and design programs and procedures to control, eliminate, and prevent disease or injury caused by chemical, physical, and biological agents or ergonomic factors. May conduct inspections and enforce adherence to laws and regulations governing the health and safety of individuals. May be employed in the public or private sector. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They recommend measures to help protect workers from potentially hazardous work methods, processes, or materials. They develop or maintain hygiene programs, such as noise surveys, continuous atmosphere monitoring, ventilation surveys, or asbestos management plans. They order suspension of activities that pose threats to workers' health or safety. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
