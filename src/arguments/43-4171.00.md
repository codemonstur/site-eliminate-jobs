## Description

Answer inquiries and provide information to the general public, customers, visitors, and other interested parties regarding activities conducted at establishment and location of departments, offices, and employees within the organization. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They operate telephone switchboard to answer, screen, or forward calls, providing information, taking messages, or scheduling appointments. They greet persons entering establishment, determine nature and purpose of visit, and direct or escort them to specific destinations. They receive payment and record receipts for services. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
