## Description

Maintain a sterile field to provide support for physicians and nurses during endoscopy procedures. Prepare and maintain instruments and equipment. May obtain specimens. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They clean, disinfect, or calibrate scopes or other endoscopic instruments according to manufacturer recommendations and facility standards. They collect specimens from patients, using standard medical procedures. They perform safety checks to verify proper equipment functioning. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
