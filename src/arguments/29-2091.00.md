## Description

Design, measure, fit, and adapt orthopedic braces, appliances or prostheses, such as limbs or facial parts for patients with disabling conditions. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They fit, test, and evaluate devices on patients, and make adjustments for proper fit, function, and comfort. They instruct patients in the use and care of orthoses and prostheses. They maintain patients' records. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
