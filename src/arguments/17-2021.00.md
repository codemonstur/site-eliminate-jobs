## Description

Apply knowledge of engineering technology and biological science to agricultural problems concerned with power and machinery, electrification, structures, soil and water conservation, and processing of agricultural products. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They prepare reports, sketches, working drawings, specifications, proposals, and budgets for proposed sites or systems. They visit sites to observe environmental problems, to consult with contractors, or to monitor construction activities. They meet with clients, such as district or regional councils, farmers, and developers, to discuss their needs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
