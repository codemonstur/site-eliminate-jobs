## Description

Construct, maintain, or repair medical supportive devices such as braces, orthotics and prosthetic devices, joints, arch supports, and other surgical and medical appliances. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They repair, modify, or maintain medical supportive devices, such as artificial limbs, braces, or surgical supports, according to specifications. They polish artificial limbs, braces, or supports, using grinding and buffing wheels. They construct or receive casts or impressions of patients' torsos or limbs for use as cutting and fabrication patterns. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
