## Description

Drive a tractor-trailer combination or a truck with a capacity of at least 26,001 pounds Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW). May be required to unload truck. Requires commercial drivers' license. Includes tow truck drivers. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They check all load-related documentation for completeness and accuracy. They inspect loads to ensure that cargo is secure. They check vehicles to ensure that mechanical, safety, and emergency equipment is in good working order. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
