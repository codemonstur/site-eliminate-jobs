## Description

Generate radiation treatment plans, develop radiation dose calculations, communicate and supervise the treatment plan implementation, and consult with members of radiation oncology team. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They design the arrangement of radiation fields to reduce exposure to critical patient structures, such as organs, using computers, manuals, and guides. They plan the use of beam modifying devices, such as compensators, shields, and wedge filters, to ensure safe and effective delivery of radiation treatment. They identify and outline bodily structures, using imaging procedures, such as x-ray, magnetic resonance imaging, computed tomography, or positron emission tomography. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
