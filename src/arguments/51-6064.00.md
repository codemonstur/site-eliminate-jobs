## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines that wind or twist textiles; or draw out and combine sliver, such as wool, hemp, or synthetic fibers. Includes slubber machine and drawing frame operators. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They notify supervisors or mechanics of equipment malfunctions. They thread yarn, thread, or fabric through guides, needles, and rollers of machines. They start machines, monitor operation, and make adjustments as needed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
