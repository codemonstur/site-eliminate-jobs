## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate entertainment and recreational activities and operations of a recreational facility, including cruise ships and parks. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They write budgets to plan recreational activities or programs. They talk to coworkers using electronic devices, such as computers and radios. They talk to customers to convey information about events or activities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
