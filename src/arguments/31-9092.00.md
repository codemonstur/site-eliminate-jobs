## Description

Perform administrative and certain clinical duties under the direction of a physician. Administrative duties may include scheduling appointments, maintaining medical records, billing, and coding information for insurance purposes. Clinical duties may include taking and recording vital signs and medical histories, preparing patients for examination, drawing blood, and administering medications as directed by physician. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They interview patients to obtain medical information and measure their vital signs, weight, and height. They clean and sterilize instruments and dispose of contaminated supplies. They record patients' medical history, vital statistics, or information such as test results in medical records. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
