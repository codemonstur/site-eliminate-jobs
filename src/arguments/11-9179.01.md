## Description

Manage or coordinate fitness and wellness programs and services. Manage and train staff of wellness specialists, health educators, or fitness instructors. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They maintain wellness- and fitness-related schedules, records, or reports. They develop or coordinate fitness and wellness programs or services. They recommend or approve new program or service offerings to promote wellness and fitness, produce revenues, or minimize costs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
