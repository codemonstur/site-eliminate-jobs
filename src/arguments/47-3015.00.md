## Description

Help plumbers, pipefitters, steamfitters, or pipelayers by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They fit or assist in fitting valves, couplings, or assemblies to tanks, pumps, or systems, using hand tools. They mount brackets and hangers on walls and ceilings to hold pipes, and set sleeves or inserts to provide support for pipes. They perform rough-ins, repair and replace fixtures and water heaters, and locate, repair, or remove leaking or broken pipes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
