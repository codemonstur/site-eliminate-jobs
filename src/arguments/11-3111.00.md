## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate compensation and benefits activities of an organization. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They direct preparation and distribution of written and verbal information to inform employees of benefits, compensation, and personnel policies. They design, evaluate, and modify benefits policies to ensure that programs are current, competitive, and in compliance with legal requirements. They fulfill all reporting requirements of all relevant government rules and regulations, including the Employee Retirement Income Security Act (ERISA). Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
