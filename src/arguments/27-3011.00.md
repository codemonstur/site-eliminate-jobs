## Description

Speak or read from scripted materials, such as news reports or commercial messages, on radio, television, or other communications media. May play and queue music, announce artist or title of performance, identify station, or interview guests. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They read news flashes to inform audiences of important events. They announce musical selections, station breaks, commercials, or public service information, and accept requests from listening audience. They operate control consoles. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
