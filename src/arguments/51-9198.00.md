## Description

Help production workers by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include supplying or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They lift raw materials, finished products, and packed items, manually or using hoists. They mark or tag identification on parts. They observe equipment operations so that malfunctions can be detected, and notify operators of any malfunctions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
