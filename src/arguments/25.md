## Description

Educational instruction and library occupations are the people who teach, train, and organize knowledge for learners of every age. The group includes elementary, middle, and secondary teachers, professors, special education and ESL instructors, librarians, archivists, and instructional coordinators. Their work involves planning lessons, delivering instruction, assessing progress, managing classrooms or collections, and guiding students toward independent learning. Most teaching roles require a bachelor's degree and a state-issued license, while higher education and library roles often require a master's degree. These professionals pass on skills and knowledge and give people access to the information they need.

Major group `25` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**68** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `25` accounts for **13,092,400** workers, or **7.69%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### AI tutors and adaptive learning platforms

Personalized AI instruction that adapts to each learner reduces the need for human tutors. They provide one-on-one support at scale.

This compresses tutoring work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Khanmigo](https://www.khanacademy.org/khan-labs)
- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Intelligent tutoring system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligent_tutoring_system)

### Automated grading and feedback

AI that scores assignments and gives feedback reduces the need for grading teachers. It automates assessment.

This compresses grading work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/)
- [Turnitin](https://www.turnitin.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Educational assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_assessment)

### LLM lesson planning and content generation

Models that draft curricula and teaching materials reduce the need for lesson planning. They generate resources automatically.

This compresses planning work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [MagicSchool](https://www.magicschool.ai/)
- [Eduaide](https://www.eduaide.ai/)
- [Wikipedia: Lesson plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesson_plan)

### Virtual classrooms and AI teaching assistants

Online platforms with AI support for instructors reduce the need for classroom aides. They automate support tasks.

This compresses support work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Zoom](https://zoom.us/)
- [Google Classroom](https://classroom.google.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Virtual learning environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_learning_environment)

### Personalized curriculum optimization

Adaptive systems that tailor learning paths reduce the need for curriculum designers. They optimize content automatically.

This compresses design work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Knewton](https://www.knewton.com/)
- [Carnegie Learning](https://www.carnegielearning.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Adaptive learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_learning)
