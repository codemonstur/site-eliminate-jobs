## Description

Geological Technicians, Except Hydrologic Technicians is an occupation classified within Life, Physical, and Social Science in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They test and analyze samples to determine their content and characteristics, using laboratory apparatus or testing equipment. They collect or prepare solid or fluid samples for analysis. They compile, log, or record testing or operational data for review and further analysis. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
