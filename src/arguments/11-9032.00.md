## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate the academic, administrative, or auxiliary activities of kindergarten, elementary, or secondary schools. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They counsel and provide guidance to students regarding personal, academic, vocational, or behavioral issues. They confer with parents and staff to discuss educational activities, policies, and student behavior or learning problems. They observe teaching methods and examine learning materials to evaluate and standardize curricula and teaching techniques and to determine areas for improvement. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
