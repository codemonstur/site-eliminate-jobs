## Description

Make and confirm reservations for transportation or lodging, or sell transportation tickets. May check baggage and direct passengers to designated concourse, pier, or track; deliver tickets and contact individuals and groups to inform them of package tours; or provide tourists with travel or transportation information. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They make and confirm reservations for transportation and accommodations, using telephones, faxes, mail, and computers. They confer with customers to determine their service requirements and travel preferences. They prepare customer invoices and accept payment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
