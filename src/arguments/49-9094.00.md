## Description

Repair and open locks, make keys, change locks and safe combinations, and install and repair safes. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They cut new or duplicate keys, using impressions or code key machines. They insert new or repaired tumblers into locks to change combinations. They move picklocks in cylinders to open door locks without keys. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
