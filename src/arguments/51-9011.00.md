## Description

Operate or tend equipment to control chemical changes or reactions in the processing of industrial or consumer products. Equipment used includes devulcanizers, steam-jacketed kettles, and reactor vessels. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They observe safety precautions to prevent fires or explosions. They patrol work areas to detect leaks or equipment malfunctions or to monitor operating conditions. They monitor gauges, recording instruments, flowmeters, or products to ensure that specified conditions are maintained. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
