## Description

Fabricate, finish, or evaluate the quality of gems and diamonds used in jewelry or industrial tools. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They assign polish, symmetry, and clarity grades to stones, according to established grading systems. They identify and document stones' clarity characteristics, using plot diagrams. They estimate wholesale and retail value of gems, following pricing guides, market fluctuations, and other relevant economic factors. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
