## Description

Teach courses in anthropology or archeology. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They advise students on academic and vocational curricula, career issues, and laboratory and field research. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as research methods, urban anthropology, and language and culture. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
