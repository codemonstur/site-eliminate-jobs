## Description

Teach students on a short-term basis as a temporary replacement for a regular classroom teacher, typically using the regular teacher's lesson plan. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They enforce school and class rules to maintain order in the classroom. They answer students' questions. They follow lesson plans designed by absent teachers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
