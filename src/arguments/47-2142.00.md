## Description

Cover interior walls or ceilings of rooms with decorative wallpaper or fabric, or attach advertising posters on surfaces such as walls and billboards. May remove old materials or prepare surfaces to be papered. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They place strips or sections of paper on surfaces, aligning section edges and patterns. They smooth strips or sections of paper with brushes or rollers to remove wrinkles and bubbles and to smooth joints. They staple or tack advertising posters onto fences, walls, billboards, or poles. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
