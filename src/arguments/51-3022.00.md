## Description

Use hands or hand tools to perform routine cutting and trimming of meat, poultry, and seafood. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They use knives, cleavers, meat saws, bandsaws, or other equipment to perform meat cutting and trimming. They weigh meats and tag containers for weight and contents. They inspect meat products for defects, bruises or blemishes and remove them along with any excess fat. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
