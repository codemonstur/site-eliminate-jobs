## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines that cut or slice materials, such as glass, stone, cork, rubber, tobacco, food, paper, or insulating material. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They remove defective or substandard materials from machines, and readjust machine components so that products meet standards. They maintain production records, such as quantities, types, and dimensions of materials produced. They start machines to verify setups, and make any necessary adjustments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
