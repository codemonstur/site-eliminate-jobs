## Description

Handle baggage for travelers at transportation terminals or for guests at hotels or similar establishments. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They receive and mark baggage by completing and attaching claim checks. They greet incoming guests and escort them to their rooms. They transport guests about premises and local areas, or arrange for transportation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
