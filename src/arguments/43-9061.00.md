## Description

Perform duties too varied and diverse to be classified in any specific office clerical occupation, requiring knowledge of office systems and procedures. Clerical duties may be assigned in accordance with the office procedures of individual establishments and may include a combination of answering telephones, bookkeeping, typing or word processing, office machine operation, and filing. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They operate office machines, such as photocopiers and scanners, facsimile machines, voice mail systems, and personal computers. They answer telephones, direct calls, and take messages. They communicate with customers, employees, and other individuals to answer questions, disseminate or explain information, take orders, and address complaints. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
