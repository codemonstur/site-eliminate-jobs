## Description

Diagnose and treat visual system disorders such as binocular vision and eye movement impairments. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They evaluate, diagnose, or treat disorders of the visual system with an emphasis on binocular vision or abnormal eye movements. They examine patients with problems related to ocular motility, binocular vision, amblyopia, or strabismus. They perform diagnostic tests or measurements, such as motor testing, visual acuity testing, lensometry, retinoscopy, and color vision testing. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
