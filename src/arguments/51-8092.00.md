## Description

Distribute or process gas for utility companies and others by controlling compressors to maintain specified pressures on main pipelines. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They monitor equipment functioning, observe temperature, level, and flow gauges, and perform regular unit checks to ensure that all equipment is operating as it should. They distribute or process gas for utility companies or industrial plants, using panel boards, control boards, and semi-automatic equipment. They control operation of compressors, scrubbers, evaporators, and refrigeration equipment to liquefy, compress, or regasify natural gas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
