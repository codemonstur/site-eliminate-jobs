## Description

Operate or control petroleum refining or processing units. May specialize in controlling manifold and pumping systems, gauging or testing oil in storage tanks, or regulating the flow of oil into pipelines. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They signal other workers by telephone or radio to operate pumps, open and close valves, and check temperatures. They maintain and repair equipment, or report malfunctioning equipment to supervisors so that repairs can be scheduled. They monitor process indicators, instruments, gauges, and meters to detect and report any possible problems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
