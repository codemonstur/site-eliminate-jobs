## Description

Calculate, measure, load, mix, and process refined feedstock with additives in fermentation or reaction process vessels and monitor production process. Perform, and keep records of, plant maintenance, repairs, and safety inspections. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They monitor batch, continuous flow, or hybrid biofuels production processes. They operate valves, pumps, engines, or generators to control and adjust biofuels production. They monitor and record biofuels processing data. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
