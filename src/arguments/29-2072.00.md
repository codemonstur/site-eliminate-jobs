## Description

Compile, process, and maintain medical records of hospital and clinic patients in a manner consistent with medical, administrative, ethical, legal, and regulatory requirements of the healthcare system. Classify medical and healthcare concepts, including diagnosis, procedures, medical services, and equipment, into the healthcare industry's numerical coding system. Includes medical coders. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They protect the security of medical records to ensure that confidentiality is maintained. They review records for completeness, accuracy, and compliance with regulations. They scan patients' health records into electronic formats. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
