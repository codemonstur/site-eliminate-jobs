## Description

Operate or monitor railroad track switches or locomotive instruments. May couple or uncouple rolling stock to make up or break up trains. Watch for and relay traffic signals. May inspect couplings, air hoses, journal boxes, and hand brakes. May watch for dragging equipment or obstacles on rights-of-way. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They signal locomotive engineers to start or stop trains when coupling or uncoupling cars, using hand signals, lanterns, or radio communication. They observe train signals along routes and verify their meanings for engineers. They inspect couplings, air hoses, journal boxes, and handbrakes to ensure that they are securely fastened and functioning properly. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
