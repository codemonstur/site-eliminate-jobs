## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines that cut textiles. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They inspect products to ensure that the quality standards and specifications are met. They place patterns on top of layers of fabric and cut fabric following patterns, using electric or manual knives, cutters, or computer numerically controlled cutting devices. They start machines, monitor operations, and make adjustments as needed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
