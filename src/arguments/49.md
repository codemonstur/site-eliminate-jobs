## Description

Installation, maintenance, and repair occupations are the people who install equipment, keep it working, and fix it when it breaks. The group includes automotive mechanics, HVAC technicians, industrial machinery mechanics, electronics installers, and general maintenance and repair workers. Their work involves diagnosing faults, replacing parts, calibrating systems, performing scheduled servicing, and testing equipment to confirm it runs correctly. Most roles require a high school diploma plus technical training, an apprenticeship, or certification. These workers keep vehicles, machines, and systems reliable across every industry.

Major group `49` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**52** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `49` accounts for **6,494,100** workers, or **3.81%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Predictive maintenance and AI diagnostics

AI that predicts equipment failures reduces the need for maintenance staff. It forecasts failures and schedules repairs.

This compresses maintenance and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Uptake](https://www.uptake.com/)
- [Augury](https://www.augury.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Predictive maintenance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predictive_maintenance)

### AR-guided repair and remote assistance

Augmented reality for repair guidance reduces the need for expert technicians. It overlays instructions on equipment.

This compresses repair and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Microsoft HoloLens](https://www.microsoft.com/hololens)
- [TeamViewer](https://www.teamviewer.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Augmented reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality)

### Autonomous inspection drones

Drones for equipment inspection reduce the need for inspectors. They capture imagery automatically.

This compresses inspection and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Percepto](https://percepto.co/)
- [Skydio](https://www.skydio.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Unmanned aerial vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle)

### Self-diagnosing and self-repairing systems

Systems that detect and fix their own faults reduce the need for repair staff. They maintain themselves.

This compresses repair and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/)
- [GE](https://www.ge.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Self-healing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-healing)

### Robotic maintenance platforms

Robots for maintenance tasks reduce the need for maintenance staff. They perform upkeep automatically.

This compresses maintenance and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Boston Dynamics](https://www.bostondynamics.com/)
- [ABB](https://www.abb.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robot)
