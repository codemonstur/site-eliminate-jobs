Major group `49` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**52** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Predictive maintenance and AI diagnostics** — AI that predicts equipment failures. Examples: [Uptake](https://www.uptake.com/), [Augury](https://www.augury.com/).
- **AR-guided repair and remote assistance** — Augmented reality for repair guidance. Examples: [Microsoft HoloLens](https://www.microsoft.com/hololens), [TeamViewer](https://www.teamviewer.com/).
- **Autonomous inspection drones** — Drones for equipment inspection. Examples: [Percepto](https://percepto.co/), [Skydio](https://www.skydio.com/).
- **Self-diagnosing and self-repairing systems** — Systems that detect and fix their own faults. Examples: [Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/), [GE](https://www.ge.com/).
- **Robotic maintenance platforms** — Robots for maintenance tasks. Examples: [Boston Dynamics](https://www.bostondynamics.com/), [ABB](https://www.abb.com/).
