## Description

Install, set up, rearrange, or remove switching, distribution, routing, and dialing equipment used in central offices or headends. Service or repair telephone, cable television, Internet, and other communications equipment on customers' property. May install communications equipment or communications wiring in buildings. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They note differences in wire and cable colors so that work can be performed correctly. They test circuits and components of malfunctioning telecommunications equipment to isolate sources of malfunctions, using test meters, circuit diagrams, polarity probes, and other hand tools. They drive crew trucks to and from work areas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
