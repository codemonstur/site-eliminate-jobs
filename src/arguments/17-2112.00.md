## Description

Design, develop, test, and evaluate integrated systems for managing industrial production processes, including human work factors, quality control, inventory control, logistics and material flow, cost analysis, and production coordination. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They estimate production costs, cost saving methods, and the effects of product design changes on expenditures for management review, action, and control. They plan and establish sequence of operations to fabricate and assemble parts or products and to promote efficient utilization. They analyze statistical data and product specifications to determine standards and establish quality and reliability objectives of finished product. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
