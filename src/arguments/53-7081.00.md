## Description

Collect and dump refuse or recyclable materials from containers into truck. May drive truck. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They inspect trucks prior to beginning routes to ensure safe operating condition. They drive trucks, following established routes, through residential streets or alleys or through business or industrial areas. They refuel trucks or add other fluids, such as oil or brake fluid. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
