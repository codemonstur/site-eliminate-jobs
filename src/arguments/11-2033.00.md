## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities to solicit and maintain funds for special projects or nonprofit organizations. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They develop strategies to encourage new or increased contributions. They manage fundraising budgets. They develop fundraising activity plans that maximize participation or contributions and minimize costs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
