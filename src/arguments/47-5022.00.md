## Description

Operate or tend machinery at surface mining site, equipped with scoops, shovels, or buckets to excavate and load loose materials. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They move levers, depress foot pedals, and turn dials to operate power machinery, such as power shovels, stripping shovels, scraper loaders, or backhoes. They set up or inspect equipment prior to operation. They become familiar with digging plans, machine capabilities and limitations, and efficient and safe digging procedures in a given application. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
