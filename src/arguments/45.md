Major group `45` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**14** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Precision agriculture and AI crop management** — Data-driven farming and crop optimization. Examples: [Climate FieldView](https://climate.com/), [Trimble](https://www.trimble.com/).
- **Autonomous tractors and harvesters** — Self-driving farm machinery. Examples: [John Deere](https://www.deere.com/), [Case IH](https://www.caseih.com/).
- **Harvesting and picking robots** — Robots that pick crops. Examples: [Abundant Robotics](https://www.abundantrobotics.com/), [FFRobotics](https://www.ffrobotics.com/).
- **Drone monitoring and spraying** — Aerial crop monitoring and spraying. Examples: [DJI Agriculture](https://ag.dji.com/), [PrecisionHawk](https://www.precisionhawk.com/).
- **Automated aquaculture and fishery systems** — Automated fish farming and monitoring. Examples: [Cermaq](https://www.cermaq.com/), [InnovaSea](https://www.innovasea.com/).
