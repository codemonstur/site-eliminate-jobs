Major group `45` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**14** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Precision agriculture and AI crop management

Data-driven farming and crop optimization reduce the need for manual farm management. They monitor and optimize crops.

This compresses management and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Climate FieldView](https://climate.com/)
- [Trimble](https://www.trimble.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Precision agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precision_agriculture)

### Autonomous tractors and harvesters

Self-driving farm machinery reduces the need for equipment operators. They operate autonomously.

This compresses operation and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [John Deere](https://www.deere.com/)
- [Case IH](https://www.caseih.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Autonomous tractor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_tractor)

### Harvesting and picking robots

Robots that pick crops reduce the need for pickers. They harvest automatically.

This compresses harvesting and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Abundant Robotics](https://www.abundantrobotics.com/)
- [FFRobotics](https://www.ffrobotics.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Agricultural robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_robot)

### Drone monitoring and spraying

Aerial crop monitoring and spraying reduce the need for scouts and sprayers. They monitor and treat crops.

This compresses monitoring and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [DJI Agriculture](https://ag.dji.com/)
- [PrecisionHawk](https://www.precisionhawk.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Agricultural drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_drone)

### Automated aquaculture and fishery systems

Automated fish farming and monitoring reduce the need for fishery workers. They manage aquaculture automatically.

This compresses aquaculture and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Cermaq](https://www.cermaq.com/)
- [InnovaSea](https://www.innovasea.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Aquaculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaculture)
