## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate the activities of helpers, laborers, or material movers, hand. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They maintain a safe working environment by monitoring safety procedures and equipment. They collaborate with workers and managers to solve work-related problems. They review work throughout the work process and at completion to ensure that it has been performed properly. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
