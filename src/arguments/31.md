## Description

Healthcare support occupations are the people who assist practitioners and care for patients in settings such as hospitals, clinics, and nursing homes. The group includes nursing assistants, home health and personal care aides, medical assistants, phlebotomists, and pharmacy technicians. Typical duties involve helping patients with daily activities, taking vital signs, collecting samples, preparing exam rooms, and supporting the smooth running of clinical workflow. Most roles require a certificate, postsecondary nondegree award, or on-the-job training rather than a four-year degree. These workers provide the hands-on care and practical support that clinical treatment depends on.

**20** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Care and service robots

Robots assisting with patient care tasks reduce the need for aides. They handle lifting, delivery, and monitoring.

This compresses care work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [SoftBank Robotics](https://www.softbankrobotics.com/)
- [Diligent Robotics](https://www.diligentrobots.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Care robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Care_robot)

### Automated patient monitoring and alerts

Continuous vitals monitoring with AI alerts reduces the need for manual monitoring. It detects deterioration early.

This compresses monitoring and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Philips](https://www.philips.com/)
- [Masimo](https://www.masimo.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Patient monitoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patient_monitoring)

### Robotic medication dispensing

Automated pharmacy and dispensing systems reduce the need for pharmacists and technicians. They handle counting and labeling.

This compresses dispensing and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Omnicell](https://www.omnicell.com/)
- [BD](https://www.bd.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Automated dispensing cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_dispensing_cabinet)

### AI scheduling and task routing

Automated staff scheduling and task assignment reduce the need for coordinators. They optimize shifts and routing.

This compresses scheduling and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [QGenda](https://www.qgenda.com/)
- [TCP](https://www.tcpsoftware.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Nurse scheduling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurse_scheduling_problem)

### Remote patient monitoring platforms

Home-based monitoring and telehealth reduce the need for in-person visits. They track patients remotely.

This compresses in-person care and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [TytoCare](https://www.tytocare.com/)
- [Biofourmis](https://www.biofourmis.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Remote patient monitoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_patient_monitoring)
