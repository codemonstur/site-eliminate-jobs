## Description

Set up and operate equipment that mixes or blends ingredients used in the manufacturing of food products. Includes candy makers and cheese makers. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They record production and test data for each food product batch, such as the ingredients used, temperature, test results, and time cycle. They clean and sterilize vats and factory processing areas. They set up, operate, and tend equipment that cooks, mixes, blends, or processes ingredients in the manufacturing of food products, according to formulas or recipes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
