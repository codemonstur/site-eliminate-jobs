## Description

Landscape or maintain grounds of property using hand or power tools or equipment. Workers typically perform a variety of tasks, which may include any combination of the following: sod laying, mowing, trimming, planting, watering, fertilizing, digging, raking, sprinkler installation, and installation of mortarless segmental concrete masonry wall units. It belongs to the Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance major group (code 37). They mow or edge lawns, using power mowers or edgers. They operate vehicles or powered equipment, such as mowers, tractors, twin-axle vehicles, snow blowers, chainsaws, electric clippers, sod cutters, or pruning saws. They use hand tools, such as shovels, rakes, pruning saws, saws, hedge or brush trimmers, or axes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
