## Description

Operate steam-, gas-, electric motor-, or internal combustion-engine driven compressors. Transmit, compress, or recover gases, such as butane, nitrogen, hydrogen, and natural gas. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They monitor meters and pressure gauges to determine consumption rate variations, temperatures, and pressures. They respond to problems by adjusting control room equipment or instructing other personnel to adjust equipment at problem locations or in other control areas. They record instrument readings and operational changes in operating logs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
