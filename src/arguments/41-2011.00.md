## Description

Receive and disburse money in establishments other than financial institutions. May use electronic scanners, cash registers, or related equipment. May process credit or debit card transactions and validate checks. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They receive payment by cash, check, credit cards, vouchers, or automatic debits. They greet customers entering establishments. They issue receipts, refunds, credits, or change due to customers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
