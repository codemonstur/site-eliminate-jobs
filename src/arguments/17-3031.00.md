## Description

Perform surveying and mapping duties, usually under the direction of an engineer, surveyor, cartographer, or photogrammetrist, to obtain data used for construction, mapmaking, boundary location, mining, or other purposes. May calculate mapmaking information and create maps from source data, such as surveying notes, aerial photography, satellite data, or other maps to show topographical features, political boundaries, and other features. May verify accuracy and completeness of maps. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They set out and recover stakes, marks, or other monumentation. They search for section corners, property irons, or survey points. They calculate latitudes, longitudes, angles, areas, or other information for mapmaking, using survey field notes or reference tables. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
