## Description

Design, model, or implement corporate data warehousing activities. Program and configure warehouses of database information and provide support to warehouse users. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They develop data warehouse process models, including sourcing, loading, transformation, and extraction. They verify the structure, accuracy, or quality of warehouse data. They map data between source systems, data warehouses, and data marts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
