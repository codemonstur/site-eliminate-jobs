## Description

Receive, store, and issue merchandise, materials, equipment, and other items from stockroom, warehouse, or storage yard to fill shelves, racks, tables, or customers' orders. May operate power equipment to fill orders. May mark prices on merchandise and set up sales displays. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They take inventory or examine merchandise to identify items to be reordered or replenished. They answer customers' questions about merchandise and advise customers on merchandise selection. They receive and count stock items, and record data manually or on computer. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
