## Description

Prepare, season, and cook dishes such as soups, meats, vegetables, or desserts in restaurants. May order supplies, keep records and accounts, price items on menu, or plan menu. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They ensure food is stored and cooked at correct temperature by regulating temperature of ovens, broilers, grills, and roasters. They inspect and clean food preparation areas, such as equipment, work surfaces, and serving areas, to ensure safe and sanitary food-handling practices. They portion, arrange, and garnish food, and serve food to waiters or patrons. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
