## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate student instruction, administration, and services, as well as other research and educational activities, at postsecondary institutions, including universities, colleges, and junior and community colleges. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They design or use assessments to monitor student learning outcomes. They recruit, hire, train, and terminate departmental personnel. They direct, coordinate, and evaluate the activities of personnel, including support staff engaged in administering academic institutions, departments, or alumni organizations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
