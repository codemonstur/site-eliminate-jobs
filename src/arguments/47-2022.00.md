## Description

Build stone structures, such as piers, walls, and abutments. Lay walks, curbstones, or special types of masonry for vats, tanks, and floors. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They set vertical and horizontal alignment of structures, using plumb bob, gauge line, and level. They lay out wall patterns or foundations, using straight edge, rule, or staked lines. They set stone or marble in place, according to layout or pattern. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
