## Description

Contact new or existing customers to determine their solar equipment needs, suggest systems or equipment, or estimate costs. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They prepare proposals, quotes, contracts, or presentations for potential solar customers. They select solar energy products, systems, or services for customers based on electrical energy requirements, site conditions, price, or other factors. They provide customers with information, such as quotes, orders, sales, shipping, warranties, credit, funding options, incentives, or tax rebates. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
