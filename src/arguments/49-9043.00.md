## Description

Lubricate machinery, change parts, or perform other routine machinery maintenance. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They dismantle machines and remove parts for repair, using hand tools, chain falls, jacks, cranes, or hoists. They reassemble machines after the completion of repair or maintenance work. They record production, repair, and machine maintenance information. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
