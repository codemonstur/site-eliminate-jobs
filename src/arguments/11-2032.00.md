## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities designed to create or maintain a favorable public image or raise issue awareness for their organization or client. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They establish and maintain effective working relationships with clients, government officials, and media representatives and use these relationships to develop new business opportunities. They assign, supervise, and review the activities of public relations staff. They identify main client groups and audiences, determine the best way to communicate publicity information to them, and develop and implement a communication plan. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
