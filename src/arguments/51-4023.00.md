## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines to roll steel or plastic forming bends, beads, knurls, rolls, or plate, or to flatten, temper, or reduce gauge of material. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They monitor machine cycles and mill operation to detect jamming and to ensure that products conform to specifications. They adjust and correct machine set-ups to reduce thicknesses, reshape products, and eliminate product defects. They start operation of rolling and milling machines to flatten, temper, form, and reduce sheet metal sections and to produce steel strips. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
