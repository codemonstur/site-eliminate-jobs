## Description

Plan and conduct food service or nutritional programs to assist in the promotion of health and control of disease. May supervise activities of a department providing quantity food services, counsel individuals, or conduct nutritional research. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They assess nutritional needs, diet restrictions, and current health plans to develop and implement dietary-care plans and provide nutritional counseling. They evaluate laboratory tests in preparing nutrition recommendations. They counsel individuals and groups on basic rules of good nutrition, healthy eating habits, and nutrition monitoring to improve their quality of life. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
