## Description

Help brickmasons, blockmasons, stonemasons, or tile and marble setters by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They mix mortar, plaster, and grout, manually or using machines, according to standard formulas. They cut materials to specified sizes for installation, using power saws or tile cutters. They erect scaffolding or other installation structures. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
