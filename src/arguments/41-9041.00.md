## Description

Solicit donations or orders for goods or services over the telephone. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They contact businesses or private individuals by telephone to solicit sales for goods or services, or to request donations for charitable causes. They obtain customer information such as name, address, and payment method, and enter orders into computers. They explain products or services and prices, and answer questions from customers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
