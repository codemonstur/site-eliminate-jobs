## Description

Lighting Technicians is an occupation classified within Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They assess safety of wiring or equipment set-up to determine the risk of fire or electrical shock. They consult with lighting director or production staff to determine lighting requirements. They disassemble and store equipment after performances. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
