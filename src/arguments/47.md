Major group `47` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**65** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Autonomous construction equipment

Self-driving bulldozers, excavators, etc. reduce the need for operators. They operate autonomously.

This compresses operation and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Caterpillar](https://www.caterpillar.com/)
- [Komatsu](https://www.komatsu.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Autonomous construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_construction)

### Robotic bricklaying and 3D-printed buildings

Robots that lay bricks and print structures reduce the need for masons. They build automatically.

This compresses construction and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Hadrian X](https://www.fbr.com.au/)
- [ICON](https://www.iconbuild.com/)
- [Wikipedia: 3D printing in construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_printing_in_construction)

### AI site planning and scheduling

Automated construction planning and scheduling reduce the need for planners. They optimize schedules automatically.

This compresses planning and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Procore](https://www.procore.com/)
- [Autodesk Construction Cloud](https://construction.autodesk.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Construction management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_management)

### Automated surveying and inspection drones

Drones for site survey and inspection reduce the need for surveyors. They capture data automatically.

This compresses surveying and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Skycatch](https://www.skycatch.com/)
- [DroneDeploy](https://www.dronedeploy.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Surveying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveying)

### Robotic excavation and tunneling

Automated digging and tunneling machines reduce the need for operators. They excavate automatically.

This compresses excavation and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [The Boring Company](https://www.boringcompany.com/)
- [Herrenknecht](https://www.herrenknecht.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Tunnel boring machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunnel_boring_machine)
