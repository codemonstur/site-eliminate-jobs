## Description

Operate equipment used for applying concrete, asphalt, or other materials to road beds, parking lots, or airport runways and taxiways or for tamping gravel, dirt, or other materials. Includes concrete and asphalt paving machine operators, form tampers, tamping machine operators, and stone spreader operators. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They start machine, engage clutch, and push and move levers to guide machine along forms or guidelines and to control the operation of machine attachments. They fill tanks, hoppers, or machines with paving materials. They control paving machines to push dump trucks and to maintain a constant flow of asphalt or other material into hoppers or screeds. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
