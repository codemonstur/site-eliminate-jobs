## Description

Teach one or more subjects to students at the secondary school level. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare students for later grades by encouraging them to explore learning opportunities and to persevere with challenging tasks. They instruct through lectures, discussions, and demonstrations in one or more subjects, such as English, mathematics, or social studies. They establish and enforce rules for behavior and procedures for maintaining order among students. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
