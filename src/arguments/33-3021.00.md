## Description

Conduct investigations related to suspected violations of federal, state, or local laws to prevent or solve crimes. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They prepare reports that detail investigation findings. They obtain and verify evidence by interviewing and observing suspects and witnesses or by analyzing records. They preserve, process, and analyze items of evidence obtained from crime scenes and suspects, placing them in proper containers and destroying evidence no longer needed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
