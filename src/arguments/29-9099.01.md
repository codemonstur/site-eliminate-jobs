## Description

Provide prenatal care and childbirth assistance. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They monitor maternal condition during labor by checking vital signs, monitoring uterine contractions, or performing physical examinations. They identify tubal and ectopic pregnancies and refer patients for treatments. They provide necessary medical care for infants at birth, including emergency care such as resuscitation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
