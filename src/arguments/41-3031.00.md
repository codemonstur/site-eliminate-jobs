## Description

Buy and sell securities or commodities in investment and trading firms, or provide financial services to businesses and individuals. May advise customers about stocks, bonds, mutual funds, commodities, and market conditions. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They make bids or offers to buy or sell securities. They monitor markets or positions. They agree on buying or selling prices at optimal levels for clients. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
