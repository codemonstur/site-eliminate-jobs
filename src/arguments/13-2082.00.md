## Description

Prepare tax returns for individuals or small businesses. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They compute taxes owed or overpaid, using adding machines or personal computers, and complete entries on forms, following tax form instructions and tax tables. They use all appropriate adjustments, deductions, and credits to keep clients' taxes to a minimum. They furnish taxpayers with sufficient information and advice to ensure correct tax form completion. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
