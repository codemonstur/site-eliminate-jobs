## Description

Play parts in stage, television, radio, video, or film productions, or other settings for entertainment, information, or instruction. Interpret serious or comic role by speech, gesture, and body movement to entertain or inform audience. May dance and sing. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They attend auditions and casting calls to audition for roles. They work closely with directors, other actors, and playwrights to find the interpretation most suited to the role. They portray and interpret roles, using speech, gestures, and body movements, to entertain, inform, or instruct radio, film, television, or live audiences. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
