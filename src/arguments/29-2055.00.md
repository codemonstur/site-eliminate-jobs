## Description

Assist in operations, under the supervision of surgeons, registered nurses, or other surgical personnel. May help set up operating room, prepare and transport patients for surgery, adjust lights and equipment, pass instruments and other supplies to surgeons and surgeons' assistants, hold retractors, cut sutures, and help count sponges, needles, supplies, and instruments. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They maintain a proper sterile field during surgical procedures. They count sponges, needles, and instruments before and after operation. They hand instruments and supplies to surgeons and surgeons' assistants, hold retractors and cut sutures, and perform other tasks as directed by surgeon during operation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
