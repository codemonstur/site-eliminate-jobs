## Description

Conduct qualitative and quantitative chemical analyses or experiments in laboratories for quality or process control or to develop new products or knowledge. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They develop, improve, or customize products, equipment, formulas, processes, or analytical methods. They analyze organic or inorganic compounds to determine chemical or physical properties, composition, structure, relationships, or reactions, using chromatography, spectroscopy, or spectrophotometry techniques. They induce changes in composition of substances by introducing heat, light, energy, or chemical catalysts for quantitative or qualitative analysis. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
