## Description

Collect, identify, classify, and analyze physical evidence related to criminal investigations. Perform tests on weapons or substances, such as fiber, hair, and tissue to determine significance to investigation. May testify as expert witnesses on evidence or crime laboratory techniques. May serve as specialists in area of expertise, such as ballistics, fingerprinting, handwriting, or biochemistry. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They collect evidence from crime scenes, storing it in conditions that preserve its integrity. They keep records and prepare reports detailing findings, investigative methods, and laboratory techniques. They use photographic or video equipment to document evidence or crime scenes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
