Major group `29` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**96** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### AI diagnosis and clinical decision support

Models that assist or automate diagnosis reduce the need for diagnosticians. They analyze symptoms and suggest conditions.

This compresses diagnosis and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Watson Health](https://www.ibm.com/watson-health)
- [Ada Health](https://ada.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Clinical decision support system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_decision_support_system)

### Surgical and diagnostic robotics

Robots that perform or assist in procedures reduce the need for surgeons. They operate with precision and consistency.

This compresses surgical work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [da Vinci](https://www.intuitive.com/)
- [Medtronic](https://www.medtronic.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Surgical robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surgical_robot)

### AI radiology and pathology screening

Image analysis for detecting disease reduces the need for radiologists and pathologists. It screens images automatically.

This compresses screening and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Aidoc](https://www.aidoc.com/)
- [Zebra Medical](https://www.zebra-med.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Radiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiology)

### Ambient AI scribes for medical documentation

AI that transcribes clinical encounters reduces the need for medical scribes. It captures notes automatically.

This compresses documentation and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Nuance DAX](https://www.nuance.com/healthcare/ambient-clinical-intelligence.html)
- [Abridge](https://www.abridge.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Medical scribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_scribe)

### AI drug discovery and trial optimization

Models that accelerate molecule and trial design reduce the need for researchers. They predict efficacy and optimize trials.

This compresses discovery and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Insilico Medicine](https://insilico.com/)
- [Recursion](https://www.recursion.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Drug discovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_discovery)
