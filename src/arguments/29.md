Major group `29` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**96** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **AI diagnosis and clinical decision support** — Models that assist or automate diagnosis. Examples: [Watson Health](https://www.ibm.com/watson-health), [Ada Health](https://ada.com/).
- **Surgical and diagnostic robotics** — Robots that perform or assist in procedures. Examples: [da Vinci](https://www.intuitive.com/), [Medtronic](https://www.medtronic.com/).
- **AI radiology and pathology screening** — Image analysis for detecting disease. Examples: [Aidoc](https://www.aidoc.com/), [Zebra Medical](https://www.zebra-med.com/).
- **Ambient AI scribes for medical documentation** — AI that transcribes clinical encounters. Examples: [Nuance DAX](https://www.nuance.com/healthcare/ambient-clinical-intelligence.html), [Abridge](https://www.abridge.com/).
- **AI drug discovery and trial optimization** — Models that accelerate molecule and trial design. Examples: [Insilico Medicine](https://insilico.com/), [Recursion](https://www.recursion.com/).
