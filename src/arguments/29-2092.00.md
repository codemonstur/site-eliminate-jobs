## Description

Select and fit hearing aids for customers. Administer and interpret tests of hearing. Assess hearing instrument efficacy. Take ear impressions and prepare, design, and modify ear molds. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They train clients to use hearing aids or other augmentative communication devices. They counsel patients and families on communication strategies and the effects of hearing loss. They select and administer tests to evaluate hearing or related disabilities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
