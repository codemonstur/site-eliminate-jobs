## Description

Set up, operate, or tend milling or planing machines to mill, plane, shape, groove, or profile metal or plastic work pieces. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They remove workpieces from machines, and check to ensure that they conform to specifications, using measuring instruments such as microscopes, gauges, calipers, and micrometers. They verify alignment of workpieces on machines, using measuring instruments such as rules, gauges, or calipers. They move controls to set cutting specifications, to position cutting tools and workpieces in relation to each other, and to start machines. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
