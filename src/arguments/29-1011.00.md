## Description

Assess, treat, and care for patients by manipulation of spine and musculoskeletal system. May provide spinal adjustment or address sacral or pelvic misalignment. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They perform a series of manual adjustments to the spine or other articulations of the body to correct the musculoskeletal system. They obtain and record patients' medical histories. They diagnose health problems by reviewing patients' health and medical histories, questioning, observing, and examining patients and interpreting x-rays. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
