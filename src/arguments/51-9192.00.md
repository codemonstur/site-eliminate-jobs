## Description

Operate or tend machines to wash or clean products, such as barrels or kegs, glass items, tin plate, food, pulp, coal, plastic, or rubber, to remove impurities. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They add specified amounts of chemicals to equipment at required times to maintain solution levels and concentrations. They observe machine operations, gauges, or thermometers, and adjust controls to maintain specified conditions. They set controls to regulate temperature and length of cycles, and start conveyors, pumps, agitators, and machines. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
