## Description

Research, design, develop, or test microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) devices. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They create schematics and physical layouts of integrated microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) components or packaged assemblies consistent with process, functional, or package constraints. They investigate characteristics such as cost, performance, or process capability of potential microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) device designs, using simulation or modeling software. They create or maintain formal engineering documents, such as schematics, bills of materials, components or materials specifications, or packaging requirements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
