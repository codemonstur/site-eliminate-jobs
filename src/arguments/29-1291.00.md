## Description

Diagnose, treat, and prevent disorders by stimulating specific acupuncture points within the body using acupuncture needles. May also use cups, nutritional supplements, therapeutic massage, acupressure, and other alternative health therapies. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They maintain and follow standard quality, safety, environmental, and infection control policies and procedures. They treat patients using tools, such as needles, cups, ear balls, seeds, pellets, or nutritional supplements. They adhere to local, state, and federal laws, regulations, and statutes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
