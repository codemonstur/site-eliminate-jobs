## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities of an organization or department that serves food and beverages. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They count money and make bank deposits. They establish standards for personnel performance and customer service. They keep records required by government agencies regarding sanitation or food subsidies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
