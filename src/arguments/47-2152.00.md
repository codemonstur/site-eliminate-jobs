## Description

Assemble, install, alter, and repair pipelines or pipe systems that carry water, steam, air, or other liquids or gases. May install heating and cooling equipment and mechanical control systems. Includes sprinkler fitters. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They shut off steam, water, or other gases or liquids from pipe sections, using valve keys or wrenches. They install underground storm, sanitary, or water piping systems, extending piping as needed to connect fixtures and plumbing. They assemble pipe sections, tubing, or fittings, using couplings, clamps, screws, bolts, cement, plastic solvent, caulking, or soldering, brazing, or welding equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
