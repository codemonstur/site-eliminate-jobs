## Description

Research, design, and develop computer and network software or specialized utility programs. Analyze user needs and develop software solutions, applying principles and techniques of computer science, engineering, and mathematical analysis. Update software or enhance existing software capabilities. May work with computer hardware engineers to integrate hardware and software systems, and develop specifications and performance requirements. May maintain databases within an application area, working individually or coordinating database development as part of a team. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They analyze user needs and software requirements to determine feasibility of design within time and cost constraints. They develop or direct software system testing or validation procedures, programming, or documentation. They confer with systems analysts, engineers, programmers and others to design systems and to obtain information on project limitations and capabilities, performance requirements and interfaces. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
