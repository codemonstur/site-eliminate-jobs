## Description

Supervise and coordinate activities of personal service workers. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They train workers in proper operational procedures and functions and explain company policies. They meet with managers or other supervisors to stay informed of changes affecting operations. They assign work schedules, following work requirements, to ensure quality and timely delivery of service. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
