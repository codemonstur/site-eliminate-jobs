## Description

Teach courses in drama, music, and the arts including fine and applied art, such as painting and sculpture, or design and crafts. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They explain and demonstrate artistic techniques. They evaluate and grade students' class work, performances, projects, assignments, and papers. They prepare students for performances, exams, or assessments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
