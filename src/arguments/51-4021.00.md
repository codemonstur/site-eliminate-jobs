## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines to extrude or draw thermoplastic or metal materials into tubes, rods, hoses, wire, bars, or structural shapes. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They measure and examine extruded products to locate defects and to check for conformance to specifications, adjusting controls as necessary to alter products. They start machines and set controls to regulate vacuum, air pressure, sizing rings, and temperature, and to synchronize speed of extrusion. They determine setup procedures and select machine dies and parts, according to specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
