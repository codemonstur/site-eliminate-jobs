Major group `23` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**8** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **LLM contract review and legal research** — Models that read contracts and surface risks and precedents. Examples: [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/), [LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/).
- **Automated discovery and document analysis** — AI that sorts and analyzes large document sets. Examples: [Relativity](https://www.relativity.com/), [DISCO](https://www.csdisco.com/).
- **AI drafting of briefs, wills, and filings** — Generative tools that draft legal documents. Examples: [LegalZoom](https://www.legalzoom.com/), [DoNotPay](https://donotpay.com/).
- **Compliance automation and regulatory monitoring** — Systems that track regulatory changes and compliance status. Examples: [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/), [ComplyAdvantage](https://complyadvantage.com/).
- **Online dispute resolution platforms** — Digital platforms that resolve conflicts without courts. Examples: [Modria](https://www.modria.com/), [Tyler Technologies](https://www.tylertech.com/).
