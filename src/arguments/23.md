## Description

Legal occupations are the people who interpret, apply, and uphold the law on behalf of individuals, organizations, and the state. The group includes lawyers, judges, paralegals, legal assistants, mediators, and title examiners and abstractors. Typical duties involve researching statutes and precedents, drafting contracts and filings, advising clients, representing parties in court or negotiation, and resolving disputes. Most professional legal roles require a law degree and admission to the bar, while support roles require a paralegal certificate or associate degree. Workers in this group safeguard rights, settle disagreements, and keep the justice system functioning.

Major group `23` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**8** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `23` accounts for **1,445,800** workers, or **0.85%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### LLM contract review and legal research

Models that read contracts and surface risks and precedents reduce the need for junior lawyers and paralegals. They compress document review.

This reduces staffing for routine legal work.

**Examples**

- [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/)
- [LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Legal research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_research)

### Automated discovery and document analysis

AI that sorts and analyzes large document sets reduces the need for document reviewers. It automates classification and extraction.

This compresses discovery work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Relativity](https://www.relativity.com/)
- [DISCO](https://www.csdisco.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Electronic discovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_discovery)

### AI drafting of briefs, wills, and filings

Generative tools that draft legal documents reduce the need for drafting lawyers. They produce first drafts from templates and facts.

This compresses drafting work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [LegalZoom](https://www.legalzoom.com/)
- [DoNotPay](https://donotpay.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Legal document](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_document)

### Compliance automation and regulatory monitoring

Systems that track regulatory changes and compliance status reduce the need for compliance officers. They automate monitoring and reporting.

This compresses compliance work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/)
- [ComplyAdvantage](https://complyadvantage.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Regulatory compliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulatory_compliance)

### Online dispute resolution platforms

Digital platforms that resolve conflicts without courts reduce the need for litigators. They automate negotiation and mediation.

This compresses dispute resolution and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Modria](https://www.modria.com/)
- [Tyler Technologies](https://www.tylertech.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Online dispute resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_dispute_resolution)
