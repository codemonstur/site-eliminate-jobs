## Description

Assess and treat persons with hearing and related disorders. May fit hearing aids and provide auditory training. May perform research related to hearing problems. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They maintain patient records at all stages, including initial and subsequent evaluation and treatment activities. They evaluate hearing and balance disorders to determine diagnoses and courses of treatment. They fit, dispense, and repair assistive devices, such as hearing aids. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
