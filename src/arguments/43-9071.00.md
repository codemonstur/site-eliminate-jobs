## Description

Operate one or more of a variety of office machines, such as photocopying, photographic, and duplicating machines, or other office machines. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They read job orders to determine the type of work to be done, the quantities to be produced, and the materials needed. They deliver completed work. They place original copies in feed trays, feed originals into feed rolls, or position originals on tables beneath camera lenses. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
