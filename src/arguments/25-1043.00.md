## Description

Teach courses in forestry and conservation science. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics, such as forest resource policy, forest pathology, and mapping. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
