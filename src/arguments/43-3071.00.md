## Description

Receive and pay out money. Keep records of money and negotiable instruments involved in a financial institution's various transactions. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They balance currency, coin, and checks in cash drawers at ends of shifts and calculate daily transactions, using computers, calculators, or adding machines. They receive checks and cash for deposit, verify amounts, and check accuracy of deposit slips. They monitor bank vaults to ensure cash balances are correct. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
