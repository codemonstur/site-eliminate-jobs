## Description

Facilitate food service. Clean tables; remove dirty dishes; replace soiled table linens; set tables; replenish supply of clean linens, silverware, glassware, and dishes; supply service bar with food; and serve items such as water, condiments, and coffee to patrons. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They run cash registers. They serve ice water, coffee, rolls, or butter to patrons. They scrape and stack dirty dishes and carry dishes and other tableware to kitchens for cleaning. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
