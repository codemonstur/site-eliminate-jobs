## Description

Operate machines to build tires. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They build semi-raw rubber treads onto buffed tire casings to prepare tires for vulcanization in recapping or retreading processes. They trim excess rubber and imperfections during retreading processes. They fill cuts and holes in tires, using hot rubber. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
