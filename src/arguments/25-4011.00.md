## Description

Appraise, edit, and direct safekeeping of permanent records and historically valuable documents. Participate in research activities based on archival materials. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They organize archival records and develop classification systems to facilitate access to archival materials. They provide reference services and assistance for users needing archival materials. They prepare archival records, such as document descriptions, to allow easy access to information. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
