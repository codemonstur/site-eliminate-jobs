## Description

Prepare incoming and outgoing mail for distribution. Time-stamp, open, read, sort, and route incoming mail; and address, seal, stamp, fold, stuff, and affix postage to outgoing mail or packages. Duties may also include keeping necessary records and completed forms. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They lift and unload containers of mail or parcels onto equipment for transportation to sortation stations. They verify that items are addressed correctly, marked with the proper postage, and in suitable condition for processing. They clear jams in sortation equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
