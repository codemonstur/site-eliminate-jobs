## Description

Implement procedures and systems to prevent merchandise loss. Conduct audits and investigations of employee activity. May assist in developing policies, procedures, and systems for safeguarding assets. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They investigate known or suspected internal theft, external theft, or vendor fraud. They implement or monitor processes to reduce property or financial losses. They identify and report merchandise or stock shortages. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
