## Description

Teach courses in health specialties, in fields such as dentistry, laboratory technology, medicine, pharmacy, public health, therapy, and veterinary medicine. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They keep abreast of developments in the field by reading current literature, talking with colleagues, and participating in professional conferences. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
