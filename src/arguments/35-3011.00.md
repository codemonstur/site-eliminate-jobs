## Description

Mix and serve drinks to patrons, directly or through waitstaff. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They clean glasses, utensils, and bar equipment. They collect money for drinks served. They balance cash receipts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
