## Description

Plan, develop, and conduct programs to inform public of historical, natural, and scientific features of national, state, or local park. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They provide care for park program animals. They conduct field trips to point out scientific, historic, and natural features of parks, forests, historic sites, or other attractions. They provide visitor services, such as explaining regulations, answering visitor requests, needs and complaints, and providing information about the park and surrounding areas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
