## Description

Use verbatim methods and equipment to capture, store, retrieve, and transcribe pretrial and trial proceedings or other information. Includes stenocaptioners who operate computerized stenographic captioning equipment to provide captions of live or prerecorded broadcasts for hearing-impaired viewers. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They provide transcripts of proceedings upon request of judges, lawyers, or the public. They record symbols on computer storage media and use computer aided transcription to translate and display them as text. They record verbatim proceedings of courts, legislative assemblies, committee meetings, and other proceedings, using computerized recording equipment, electronic stenograph machines, or stenomasks. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
