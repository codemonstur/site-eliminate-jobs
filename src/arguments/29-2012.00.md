## Description

Perform routine medical laboratory tests for the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of disease. May work under the supervision of a medical technologist. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They perform quality control analyses to ensure accuracy of test results. They conduct blood tests for transfusion purposes and perform blood counts. They conduct chemical analyses of body fluids, such as blood or urine, using microscope or automatic analyzer to detect abnormalities or diseases and enter findings into computer. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
