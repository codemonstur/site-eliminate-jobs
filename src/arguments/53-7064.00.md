## Description

Pack or package by hand a wide variety of products and materials. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They examine and inspect containers, materials, or products to ensure that product quality and packing specifications are met. They measure, weigh, and count products and materials. They record product, packaging, and order information on specified forms and records. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
