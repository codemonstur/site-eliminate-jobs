## Description

Teach languages and literature courses in languages other than English. Includes teachers of American Sign Language (ASL). Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They maintain student attendance records, grades, and other required records. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
