## Description

Feed materials into or remove materials from machines or equipment that is automatic or tended by other workers. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They inspect materials and products for defects, and to ensure conformance to specifications. They record production and operational data, such as amount of materials processed. They push dual control buttons and move controls to start, stop, or adjust machinery and equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
