## Description

Study human society and social behavior by examining the groups and social institutions that people form, as well as various social, religious, political, and business organizations. May study the behavior and interaction of groups, trace their origin and growth, and analyze the influence of group activities on individual members. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They analyze and interpret data to increase the understanding of human social behavior. They prepare publications and reports containing research findings. They develop, implement, and evaluate methods of data collection, such as questionnaires or interviews. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
