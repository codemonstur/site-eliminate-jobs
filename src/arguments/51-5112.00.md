## Description

Set up and operate digital, letterpress, lithographic, flexographic, gravure, or other printing machines. Includes short-run offset printing presses. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They start presses and pull proofs to check for ink coverage and density, alignment, and registration. They examine job orders to determine quantities to be printed, stock specifications, colors, or special printing instructions. They adjust ink fountain flow rates. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
