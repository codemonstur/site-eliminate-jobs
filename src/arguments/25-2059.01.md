## Description

Provide individualized physical education instruction or services to children, youth, or adults with exceptional physical needs due to gross motor developmental delays or other impairments. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They provide adapted physical education services to students with intellectual disabilities, autism, traumatic brain injury, orthopedic impairments, or other disabling condition. They provide students positive feedback to encourage them and help them develop an appreciation for physical education. They provide individual or small groups of students with adapted physical education instruction that meets desired physical needs or goals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
