## Description

Conduct recreation activities with groups in public, private, or volunteer agencies or recreation facilities. Organize and promote activities, such as arts and crafts, sports, games, music, dramatics, social recreation, camping, and hobbies, taking into account the needs and interests of individual members. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They enforce rules and regulations of recreational facilities to maintain discipline and ensure safety. They organize, lead, and promote interest in recreational activities, such as arts, crafts, sports, games, camping, and hobbies. They assess the needs and interests of individuals and groups and plan activities accordingly, given the available equipment or facilities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
