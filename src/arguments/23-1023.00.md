## Description

Arbitrate, advise, adjudicate, or administer justice in a court of law. May sentence defendant in criminal cases according to government statutes or sentencing guidelines. May determine liability of defendant in civil cases. May perform wedding ceremonies. It belongs to the Legal major group (code 23). They sentence defendants in criminal cases, on conviction by jury, according to applicable government statutes. They monitor proceedings to ensure that all applicable rules and procedures are followed. They instruct juries on applicable laws, direct juries to deduce the facts from the evidence presented, and hear their verdicts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
