## Description

Build, install, test, or maintain optical or fiber optic equipment, such as lasers, lenses, or mirrors, using spectrometers, interferometers, or related equipment. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They compute or record photonic test data. They maintain clean working environments, according to clean room standards. They adjust or maintain equipment, such as lasers, laser systems, microscopes, oscilloscopes, pulse generators, power meters, beam analyzers, or energy measurement devices. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
