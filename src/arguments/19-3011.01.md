## Description

Conduct economic analysis related to environmental protection and use of the natural environment, such as water, air, land, and renewable energy resources. Evaluate and quantify benefits, costs, incentives, and impacts of alternative options using economic principles and statistical techniques. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They write technical documents or academic articles to communicate study results or economic forecasts. They conduct research on economic and environmental topics, such as alternative fuel use, public and private land use, soil conservation, air and water pollution control, and endangered species protection. They collect and analyze data to compare the environmental implications of economic policy or practice alternatives. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
