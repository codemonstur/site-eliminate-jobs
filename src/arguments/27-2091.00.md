## Description

Play prerecorded music for live audiences at venues or events such as clubs, parties, or wedding receptions. May use techniques such as mixing, cutting, or sampling to manipulate recordings. May also perform as emcee (master of ceremonies). It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They operate disc jockey controller and other equipment, such as microphones. They communicate with clients or venue owners to determine event information, such as music preferences, scheduling, and anticipated attendance. They adhere to schedules to keep events running on time. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
