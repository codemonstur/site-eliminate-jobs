## Description

Study the origin, development, and behavior of human beings. May study the way of life, language, or physical characteristics of people in various parts of the world. May engage in systematic recovery and examination of material evidence, such as tools or pottery remaining from past human cultures, in order to determine the history, customs, and living habits of earlier civilizations. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They collect information and make judgments through observation, interviews, and review of documents. They teach or mentor undergraduate and graduate students in anthropology or archeology. They write about and present research findings for a variety of specialized and general audiences. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
