## Description

Analyze and coordinate the schedule, timeline, procurement, staffing, and budget of a product or service on a per project basis. Lead and guide the work of technical staff. May serve as a point of contact for the client or customer. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They communicate with key stakeholders to determine project requirements and objectives. They identify project needs such as resources, staff, or finances by reviewing project objectives and schedules. They submit project deliverables to clients, ensuring adherence to quality standards. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
