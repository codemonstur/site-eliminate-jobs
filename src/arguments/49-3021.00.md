## Description

Repair and refinish automotive vehicle bodies and straighten vehicle frames. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They file, grind, sand, and smooth filled or repaired surfaces, using power tools and hand tools. They inspect repaired vehicles for proper functioning, completion of work, dimensional accuracy, and overall appearance of paint job, and test-drive vehicles to ensure proper alignment and handling. They fit and weld replacement parts into place, using wrenches and welding equipment, and grind down welds to smooth them, using power grinders and other tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
