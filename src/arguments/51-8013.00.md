## Description

Control, operate, or maintain machinery to generate electric power. Includes auxiliary equipment operators. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They control generator output to match the phase, frequency, or voltage of electricity supplied to panels. They take regulatory action, based on readings from charts, meters and gauges, at established intervals. They control power generating equipment, including boilers, turbines, generators, or reactors, using control boards or semi-automatic equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
