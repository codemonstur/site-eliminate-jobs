## Description

Restore, maintain, or prepare objects in museum collections for storage, research, or exhibit. May work with specimens such as fossils, skeletal parts, or botanicals; or artifacts, textiles, or art. May identify and record objects or install and arrange them in exhibits. Includes book or document conservators. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They determine whether objects need repair and choose the safest and most effective method of repair. They specialize in particular materials or types of object, such as documents and books, paintings, decorative arts, textiles, metals, or architectural materials. They recommend preservation procedures, such as control of temperature and humidity, to curatorial and building staff. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
