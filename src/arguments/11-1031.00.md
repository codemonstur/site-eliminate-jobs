## Description

Legislators is an occupation classified within Management in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They analyze and understand the local and national implications of proposed legislation. They appoint nominees to leadership posts, or approve such appointments. They confer with colleagues to formulate positions and strategies pertaining to pending issues. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
