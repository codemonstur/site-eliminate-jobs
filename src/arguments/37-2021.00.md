## Description

Apply or release chemical solutions or toxic gases and set traps to kill or remove pests and vermin that infest buildings and surrounding areas. It belongs to the Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance major group (code 37). They record work activities performed. They clean work site after completion of job. They measure area dimensions requiring treatment, calculate fumigant requirements, and estimate cost for service. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
