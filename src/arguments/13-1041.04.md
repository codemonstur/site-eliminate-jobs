## Description

Investigate or inspect government property to ensure compliance with contract agreements and government regulations. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They prepare correspondence, reports of inspections or investigations, or recommendations for action. They examine records, reports, or other documents to establish facts or detect discrepancies. They inspect government property, such as construction sites or public housing, to ensure compliance with contract specifications or legal requirements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
