## Description

Repair, maintain, or install computers, word processing systems, automated teller machines, and electronic office machines, such as duplicating and fax machines. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They travel to customers' stores or offices to service machines or to provide emergency repair service. They reassemble machines after making repairs or replacing parts. They advise customers concerning equipment operation, maintenance, or programming. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
