## Description

Select and breed animals according to their genealogy, characteristics, and offspring. May require knowledge of artificial insemination techniques and equipment use. May involve keeping records on heats, birth intervals, or pedigree. It belongs to the Farming, Fishing, and Forestry major group (code 45). They feed and water animals, and clean and disinfect pens, cages, yards, and hutches. They observe animals in heat to detect approach of estrus and exercise animals to induce or hasten estrus, if necessary. They treat minor injuries and ailments and contact veterinarians to obtain treatment for animals with serious illnesses or injuries. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
