## Description

Apply remote sensing principles and methods to analyze data and solve problems in areas such as natural resource management, urban planning, or homeland security. May develop new sensor systems, analytical techniques, or new applications for existing systems. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They manage or analyze data obtained from remote sensing systems to obtain meaningful results. They analyze data acquired from aircraft, satellites, or ground-based platforms, using statistical analysis software, image analysis software, or Geographic Information Systems (GIS). They integrate other geospatial data sources into projects. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
