## Description

Assemble or modify electromechanical equipment or devices, such as servomechanisms, gyros, dynamometers, magnetic drums, tape drives, brakes, control linkage, actuators, and appliances. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They inspect, test, and adjust completed units to ensure that units meet specifications, tolerances, and customer order requirements. They position, align, and adjust parts for proper fit and assembly. They assemble parts or units, and position, align, and fasten units to assemblies, subassemblies, or frames, using hand tools and power tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
