## Description

Provide healthcare services typically performed by a physician, under the supervision of a physician. Conduct complete physicals, provide treatment, and counsel patients. May, in some cases, prescribe medication. Must graduate from an accredited educational program for physician assistants. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They make tentative diagnoses and decisions about management and treatment of patients. They interpret diagnostic test results for deviations from normal. They prescribe therapy or medication with physician approval. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
