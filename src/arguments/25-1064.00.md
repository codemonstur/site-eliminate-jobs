## Description

Teach courses in geography. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as urbanization, environmental systems, and cultural geography. They conduct research in a particular field of knowledge and publish findings in professional journals, books, or electronic media. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
