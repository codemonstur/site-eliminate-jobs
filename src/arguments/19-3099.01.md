## Description

Prepare studies for proposed transportation projects. Gather, compile, and analyze data. Study the use and operation of transportation systems. Develop transportation models or simulations. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They define regional or local transportation planning problems or priorities. They participate in public meetings or hearings to explain planning proposals, to gather feedback from those affected by projects, or to achieve consensus on project designs. They prepare reports or recommendations on transportation planning. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
