## Description

Prepare and cook large quantities of food for institutions, such as schools, hospitals, or cafeterias. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They monitor and record food temperatures to ensure food safety. They cook foodstuffs according to menus, special dietary or nutritional restrictions, or numbers of portions to be served. They rotate and store food supplies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
