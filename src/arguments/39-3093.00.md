## Description

Provide personal items to patrons or customers in locker rooms, dressing rooms, or coatrooms. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They clean facilities such as floors or locker rooms. They provide towels and sheets to clients in public baths, steam rooms, and restrooms. They collect soiled linen or clothing for laundering. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
