## Description

Design special exhibits and sets for film, video, television, and theater productions. May study scripts, confer with directors, and conduct research to determine appropriate architectural styles. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They develop set designs, based on evaluation of scripts, budgets, research information, and available locations. They prepare rough drafts and scale working drawings of sets, including floor plans, scenery, and properties to be constructed. They prepare preliminary renderings of proposed exhibits, including detailed construction, layout, and material specifications, and diagrams relating to aspects such as special effects or lighting. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
