## Description

Inspect, test, sort, sample, or weigh nonagricultural raw materials or processed, machined, fabricated, or assembled parts or products for defects, wear, and deviations from specifications. May use precision measuring instruments and complex test equipment. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They discard or reject products, materials, or equipment not meeting specifications. They mark items with details, such as grade or acceptance-rejection status. They measure dimensions of products to verify conformance to specifications, using measuring instruments, such as rulers, calipers, gauges, or micrometers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
