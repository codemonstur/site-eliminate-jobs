## Description

Sell services to individuals or businesses. May describe options or resolve client problems. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They consult with clients after sales or contract signings to resolve problems and provide ongoing support. They answer customers' questions about services, prices, availability, or credit terms. They develop sales presentations or proposals to explain service specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
