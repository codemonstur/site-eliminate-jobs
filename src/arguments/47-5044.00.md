## Description

Operate underground loading or moving machine to load or move coal, ore, or rock using shuttle or mine car or conveyors. Equipment may include power shovels, hoisting engines equipped with cable-drawn scraper or scoop, or machines equipped with gathering arms and conveyor. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They handle high voltage sources and hang electrical cables. They drive loaded shuttle cars to ramps and move controls to discharge loads into mine cars or onto conveyors. They pry off loose material from roofs and move it into the paths of machines, using crowbars. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
