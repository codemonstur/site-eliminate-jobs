## Description

Set up, maintain, and dismantle audio and video equipment, such as microphones, sound speakers, connecting wires and cables, sound and mixing boards, video cameras, video monitors and servers, and related electronic equipment for live or recorded events, such as concerts, meetings, conventions, presentations, podcasts, news conferences, and sporting events. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They notify supervisors when major equipment repairs are needed. They diagnose and resolve media system problems. They direct and coordinate activities of assistants and other personnel during production. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
