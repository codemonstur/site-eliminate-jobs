## Description

Drive a light vehicle, such as a truck or van, with a capacity of less than 26,001 pounds Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW), primarily to pick up merchandise or packages from a distribution center and deliver. May load and unload vehicle. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They obey traffic laws and follow established traffic and transportation procedures. They report any mechanical problems encountered with vehicles. They verify the contents of inventory loads against shipping papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
