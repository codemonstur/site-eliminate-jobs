Major group `35` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**18** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Kitchen and food-preparation robots

Robotic cooking and food assembly reduce the need for cooks and prep staff. They handle repetitive kitchen tasks.

This compresses food prep and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Moley Robotics](https://www.moley.com/)
- [Creator](https://www.creator.rest/)
- [Wikipedia: Kitchen robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitchen_robot)

### Self-service kiosks and automated ordering

Touchscreen ordering and payment systems reduce the need for cashiers and servers. They handle ordering and payment.

This compresses ordering and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/)
- [Square](https://squareup.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Self-service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-service)

### Delivery robots and drones

Autonomous last-mile delivery reduces the need for delivery drivers. They transport food and goods.

This compresses delivery and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Starship](https://www.starship.xyz/)
- [Zipline](https://www.flyzipline.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Delivery drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delivery_drone)

### AI inventory and demand forecasting

Predictive stock and demand management reduce the need for inventory staff. They forecast and reorder automatically.

This compresses inventory work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Blue Yonder](https://blueyonder.com/)
- [RELEX](https://www.relexsolutions.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Demand forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demand_forecasting)

### Robotic baristas and beverage systems

Automated coffee and drink preparation reduce the need for baristas. They prepare beverages consistently.

This compresses beverage prep and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Cafe X](https://www.cafexapp.com/)
- [Briggo](https://www.briggo.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Automated cafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_cafe)
