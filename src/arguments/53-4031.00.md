## Description

Coordinate activities of switch-engine crew within railroad yard, industrial plant, or similar location. Conductors coordinate activities of train crew on passenger or freight trains. Yardmasters review train schedules and switching orders and coordinate activities of workers engaged in railroad traffic operations, such as the makeup or breakup of trains and yard switching. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They observe yard traffic to determine tracks available to accommodate inbound and outbound traffic. They receive instructions from dispatchers regarding trains' routes, timetables, and cargoes. They direct and instruct workers engaged in yard activities, such as switching tracks, coupling and uncoupling cars, and routing inbound and outbound traffic. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
