## Description

Assist mentally impaired or emotionally disturbed patients, working under direction of nursing and medical staff. May assist with daily living activities, lead patients in educational and recreational activities, or accompany patients to and from examinations and treatments. May restrain violent patients. Includes psychiatric orderlies. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They listen and provide emotional support and encouragement to psychiatric patients. They provide patients with cognitive, intellectual, or developmental disabilities with routine physical, emotional, psychological, or rehabilitation care under the direction of nursing or medical staff. They complete physical checks and monitor patients to detect unusual or harmful behavior and report observations to professional staff. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
