## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of correctional officers and jailers. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They take, receive, or check periodic inmate counts. They maintain order, discipline, and security within assigned areas in accordance with relevant rules, regulations, policies, and laws. They maintain knowledge of, comply with, and enforce all institutional policies, rules, procedures, and regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
