## Description

Plan, design, and furnish the internal space of rooms or buildings. Design interior environments or create physical layouts that are practical, aesthetic, and conducive to the intended purposes. May specialize in a particular field, style, or phase of interior design. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They design plans to be safe and to be compliant with the American Disabilities Act (ADA). They use computer-aided drafting (CAD) and related software to produce construction documents. They research health and safety code requirements to inform design. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
