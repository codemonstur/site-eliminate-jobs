## Description

Apply knowledge of nursing and informatics to assist in the design, development, and ongoing modification of computerized health care systems. May educate staff and assist in problem solving to promote the implementation of the health care system. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They translate nursing practice information between nurses and systems engineers, analysts, or designers, using object-oriented models or other techniques. They use informatics science to design or implement health information technology applications for resolution of clinical or health care administrative problems. They develop or implement policies or practices to ensure the privacy, confidentiality, or security of patient information. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
