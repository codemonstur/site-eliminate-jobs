## Description

Make or form wax or sand cores or molds used in the production of metal castings in foundries. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They clean and smooth molds, cores, and core boxes, and repair surface imperfections. They sift and pack sand into mold sections, core boxes, and pattern contours, using hand or pneumatic ramming tools. They position patterns inside mold sections, and clamp sections together. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
