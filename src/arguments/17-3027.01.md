## Description

Assist engineers in determining the practicality of proposed product design changes and plan and carry out tests on experimental test devices or equipment for performance, durability, or efficiency. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They document test results, using cameras, spreadsheets, documents, or other tools. They set up mechanical, hydraulic, or electric test equipment in accordance with engineering specifications, standards, or test procedures. They read and interpret blueprints, schematics, work specifications, drawings, or charts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
