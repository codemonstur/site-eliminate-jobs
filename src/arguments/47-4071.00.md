## Description

Clean and repair septic tanks, sewer lines, or drains. May patch walls and partitions of tank, replace damaged drain tile, or repair breaks in underground piping. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They communicate with supervisors and other workers, using equipment such as wireless phones, pagers, or radio telephones. They drive trucks to transport crews, materials, and equipment. They inspect manholes to locate sewer line stoppages. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
