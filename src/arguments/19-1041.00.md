## Description

Investigate and describe the determinants and distribution of disease, disability, or health outcomes. May develop the means for prevention and control. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They communicate research findings on various types of diseases to health practitioners, policy makers, and the public. They oversee public health programs, including statistical analysis, health care planning, surveillance systems, and public health improvement. They investigate diseases or parasites to determine cause and risk factors, progress, life cycle, or mode of transmission. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
