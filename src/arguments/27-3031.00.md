## Description

Promote or create an intended public image for individuals, groups, or organizations. May write or select material for release to various communications media. May specialize in using social media. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They respond to requests for information from the media or designate an appropriate spokesperson or information source. They plan or direct development or communication of programs to maintain favorable public or stockholder perceptions of an organization's accomplishments, agenda, or environmental responsibility. They post and update content on the company's Web site and social media outlets. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
