## Description

Apply principles of psychology to human resources, administration, management, sales, and marketing problems. Activities may include policy planning; employee testing and selection, training, and development; and organizational development and analysis. May work with management to organize the work setting to improve worker productivity. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They provide advice on best practices and implementation for selection. They develop and implement employee selection or placement programs. They analyze data, using statistical methods and applications, to evaluate the outcomes and effectiveness of workplace programs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
