## Description

Operate one or several types of power construction equipment, such as motor graders, bulldozers, scrapers, compressors, pumps, derricks, shovels, tractors, or front-end loaders to excavate, move, and grade earth, erect structures, or pour concrete or other hard surface pavement. May repair and maintain equipment in addition to other duties. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They learn and follow safety regulations. They take actions to avoid potential hazards or obstructions, such as utility lines, other equipment, other workers, or falling objects. They start engines, move throttles, switches, or levers, or depress pedals to operate machines, such as bulldozers, trench excavators, road graders, or backhoes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
