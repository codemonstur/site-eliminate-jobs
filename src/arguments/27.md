## Description

Arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media occupations are the people who create, perform, and communicate visual, performing, and written work. The group includes artists, designers, actors, musicians, athletes, writers, editors, photographers, and journalists. Typical duties involve developing original concepts, producing and performing work, editing and refining content, and presenting it to an audience through stage, print, broadcast, or digital channels. Entry requirements vary widely, from natural talent and a portfolio to formal training, and many workers are self-employed or freelance. Workers in this group shape culture, tell stories, and inform and entertain the public.

Major group `27` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**45** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `27` accounts for **2,985,900** workers, or **1.75%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Generative AI for image, video, and music

Models that create media from text prompts reduce the need for human artists and composers. They generate content automatically.

This compresses creative production and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/)
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Generative artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_artificial_intelligence)

### AI editing, dubbing, and post-production

Automated editing, translation, and voice cloning reduce the need for editors and dubbing artists. They process media automatically.

This compresses post-production and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/)
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/)
- [Wikipedia: Video editing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_editing)

### Automated content and copy generation

AI that writes articles, ads, and marketing copy reduces the need for copywriters. It generates text automatically.

This compresses content creation and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/)
- [Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai/)
- [Wikipedia: Copywriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copywriting)

### Virtual influencers and synthetic media

AI-generated personas and digital avatars reduce the need for human performers. They create synthetic media automatically.

This compresses media production and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/)
- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Virtual influencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_influencer)

### AI game and level design

Procedural and AI-assisted game content creation reduces the need for level designers. It generates content automatically.

This compresses game design and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Unity](https://unity.com/)
- [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Procedural generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procedural_generation)
