## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Generative AI for image, video, and music** — Models that create media from text prompts. Examples: [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/), [Runway](https://runwayml.com/).
- **AI editing, dubbing, and post-production** — Automated editing, translation, and voice cloning. Examples: [Descript](https://www.descript.com/), [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/).
- **Automated content and copy generation** — AI that writes articles, ads, and marketing copy. Examples: [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/), [Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai/).
- **Virtual influencers and synthetic media** — AI-generated personas and digital avatars. Examples: [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/), [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/).
- **AI game and level design** — Procedural and AI-assisted game content creation. Examples: [Unity](https://unity.com/), [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/).
