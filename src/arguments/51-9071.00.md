## Description

Design, fabricate, adjust, repair, or appraise jewelry, gold, silver, other precious metals, or gems. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They clean and polish metal items and jewelry pieces, using jewelers' tools, polishing wheels, and chemical baths. They smooth soldered joints and rough spots, using hand files and emery paper, and polish smoothed areas with polishing wheels or buffing wire. They create jewelry from materials such as gold, silver, platinum, and precious or semiprecious stones. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
