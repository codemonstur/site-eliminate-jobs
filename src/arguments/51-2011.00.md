## Description

Assemble, fit, fasten, and install parts of airplanes, space vehicles, or missiles, such as tails, wings, fuselage, bulkheads, stabilizers, landing gear, rigging and control equipment, or heating and ventilating systems. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They read blueprints, illustrations, or specifications to determine layouts, sequences of operations, or identities or relationships of parts. They inspect or test installed units, parts, systems, or assemblies for fit, alignment, performance, defects, or compliance with standards, using measuring instruments or test equipment. They assemble parts, fittings, or subassemblies on aircraft, using layout tools, hand tools, power tools, or fasteners, such as bolts, screws, rivets, or clamps. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
