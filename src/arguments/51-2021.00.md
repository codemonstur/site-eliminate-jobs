## Description

Wind wire coils used in electrical components, such as resistors and transformers, and in electrical equipment and instruments, such as field cores, bobbins, armature cores, electrical motors, generators, and control equipment. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They operate or tend wire-coiling machines to wind wire coils used in electrical components such as resistors and transformers, and in electrical equipment and instruments such as bobbins and generators. They attach, alter, and trim materials such as wire, insulation, and coils, using hand tools. They cut, strip, and bend wire leads at ends of coils, using pliers and wire scrapers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
