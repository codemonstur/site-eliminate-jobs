## Description

Advise and educate individuals or organizations on acquiring and managing debt. May provide guidance in determining the best type of loan and explain loan requirements or restrictions. May help develop debt management plans or student financial aid packages. May advise on credit issues, or provide budget, mortgage, bankruptcy, or student financial aid counseling. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They create debt management plans, spending plans, or budgets to assist clients to meet financial goals. They assess clients' overall financial situations by reviewing income, assets, debts, expenses, credit reports, or other financial information. They calculate clients' available monthly income to meet debt obligations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
