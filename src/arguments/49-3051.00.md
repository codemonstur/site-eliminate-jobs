## Description

Repair and adjust electrical and mechanical equipment of inboard or inboard-outboard boat engines. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They start motors and monitor performance for signs of malfunctioning, such as smoke, excessive vibration, or misfiring. They document inspection and test results and work performed or to be performed. They mount motors to boats, and operate boats at various speeds on waterways to conduct operational tests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
