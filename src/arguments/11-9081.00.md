## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities of an organization or department that provides lodging and other accommodations. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They answer inquiries pertaining to hotel policies and services, and resolve occupants' complaints. They participate in financial activities, such as the setting of room rates, the establishment of budgets, and the allocation of funds to departments. They confer and cooperate with other managers to ensure coordination of hotel activities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
