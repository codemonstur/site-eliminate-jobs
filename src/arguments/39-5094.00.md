## Description

Provide skincare treatments to face and body to enhance an individual's appearance. Includes electrologists and laser hair removal specialists. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They sterilize equipment and clean work areas. They cleanse clients' skin with water, creams, or lotions. They demonstrate how to clean and care for skin properly and recommend skin-care regimens. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
