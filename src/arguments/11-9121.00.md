## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities in such fields as life sciences, physical sciences, mathematics, statistics, and research and development in these fields. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They hire, supervise, or evaluate engineers, technicians, researchers, or other staff. They design or coordinate successive phases of problem analysis, solution proposals, or testing. They plan or direct research, development, or production activities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
