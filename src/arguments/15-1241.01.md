## Description

Design or configure wired, wireless, and satellite communications systems for voice, video, and data services. Supervise installation, service, and maintenance. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They document technical specifications and operating standards for telecommunications equipment. They provide user support by diagnosing network and device problems and implementing technical or procedural solutions. They implement system renovation projects in collaboration with technical staff, engineering consultants, installers, and vendors. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
