## Description

Care for ill, injured, or convalescing patients or persons with disabilities in hospitals, nursing homes, clinics, private homes, group homes, and similar institutions. May work under the supervision of a registered nurse. Licensing required. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They observe patients, charting and reporting changes in patients' conditions, such as adverse reactions to medication or treatment, and taking any necessary action. They measure and record patients' vital signs, such as height, weight, temperature, blood pressure, pulse, or respiration. They administer prescribed medications or start intravenous fluids, noting times and amounts on patients' charts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
