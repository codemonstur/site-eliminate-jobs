## Description

Operate or control nuclear reactors. Move control rods, start and stop equipment, monitor and adjust controls, and record data in logs. Implement emergency procedures when needed. May respond to abnormalities, determine cause, and recommend corrective action. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They operate nuclear power reactors in accordance with policies and procedures to protect workers from radiation and to ensure environmental safety. They adjust controls to position rod and to regulate flux level, reactor period, coolant temperature, or rate of power flow, following standard procedures. They develop or implement actions such as lockouts, tagouts, or clearances to allow equipment to be safely repaired. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
