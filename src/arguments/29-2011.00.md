## Description

Perform complex medical laboratory tests for diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of disease. May train or supervise staff. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They analyze samples of biological material for chemical content or reaction. They analyze laboratory findings to check the accuracy of the results. They conduct chemical analysis of body fluids, including blood, urine, or spinal fluid, to determine presence of normal or abnormal components. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
