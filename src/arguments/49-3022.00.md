## Description

Replace or repair broken windshields and window glass in motor vehicles. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They select appropriate tools, safety equipment, and parts, according to job requirements. They prime all scratches on pinchwelds with primer and allow to dry. They remove all dirt, foreign matter, and loose glass from damaged areas, apply primer along windshield or window edges, and allow primer to dry. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
