## Description

Financial and Investment Analysts is an occupation classified within Business and Financial Operations in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They advise clients on aspects of capitalization, such as amounts, sources, or timing. They analyze financial or operational performance of companies facing financial difficulties to identify or recommend remedies. They assess companies as investments for clients by examining company facilities. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
