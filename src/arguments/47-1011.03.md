## Description

Direct work crews installing residential or commercial solar photovoltaic or thermal systems. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They plan and coordinate installations of photovoltaic (PV) solar and solar thermal systems to ensure conformance to codes. They supervise solar installers, technicians, and subcontractors for solar installation projects to ensure compliance with safety standards. They estimate materials, equipment, and personnel needed for residential or commercial solar installation projects. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
