## Description

Provide beauty services, such as cutting, coloring, and styling hair, and massaging and treating scalp. May shampoo hair, apply makeup, dress wigs, remove hair, and provide nail and skincare services. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They keep work stations clean and sanitize tools, such as scissors and combs. They schedule client appointments. They bleach, dye, or tint hair, using applicator or brush. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
