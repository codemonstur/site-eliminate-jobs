## Description

Emergency Medical Technicians is an occupation classified within Healthcare Practitioners and Technical in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They administer first aid treatment or life support care to sick or injured persons in prehospital settings. They assess nature and extent of illness or injury to establish and prioritize medical procedures. They attend training classes to maintain certification licensure, keep abreast of new developments in the field, or maintain existing knowledge. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
