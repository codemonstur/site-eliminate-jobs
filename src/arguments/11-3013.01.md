## Description

Direct an organization's security functions, including physical security and safety of employees and facilities. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They develop budgets for security operations. They identify, investigate, or resolve security breaches. They plan, direct, or coordinate security activities to safeguard company employees, guests, or others on company property. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
