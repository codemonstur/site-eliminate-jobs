## Description

Move or install mobile homes or prefabricated buildings. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They move and set up mobile homes or prefabricated buildings on owners' lots or at mobile home parks. They seal open sides of modular units to prepare them for shipment, using polyethylene sheets, nails, and hammers. They repair leaks in plumbing or gas lines, using caulking compounds and plastic or copper pipe. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
