## Description

Teach courses in the physical sciences, except chemistry and physics. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching, and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They maintain student attendance records, grades, and other required records. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as structural geology, micrometeorology, and atmospheric thermodynamics. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
