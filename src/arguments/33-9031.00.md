## Description

Observe gambling operation for irregular activities such as cheating or theft by either employees or patrons. Investigate potential threats to gambling assets such as money, chips, and gambling equipment. Act as oversight and security agent for management and customers. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They monitor establishment activities to ensure adherence to all state gaming regulations and company policies and procedures. They observe casino or casino hotel operations for irregular activities, such as cheating or theft by employees or patrons, using audio and video equipment and one-way mirrors. They report all violations and suspicious behaviors to supervisors, verbally or in writing. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
