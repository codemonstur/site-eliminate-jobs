## Description

Cover roofs of structures with shingles, slate, asphalt, aluminum, wood, or related materials. May spray roofs, sidings, and walls with material to bind, seal, insulate, or soundproof sections of structures. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They inspect problem roofs to determine the best repair procedures. They waterproof or damp-proof walls, floors, roofs, foundations, or basements by painting or spraying surfaces with waterproof coatings or by attaching waterproofing membranes to surfaces. They cover exposed nailheads with roofing cement or caulking to prevent water leakage or rust. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
