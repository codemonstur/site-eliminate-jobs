## Description

Design strategies for enterprise databases, data warehouse systems, and multidimensional networks. Set standards for database operations, programming, query processes, and security. Model, design, and construct large relational databases or data warehouses. Create and optimize data models for warehouse infrastructure and workflow. Integrate new systems with existing warehouse structure and refine system performance and functionality. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They develop and document database architectures. They collaborate with system architects, software architects, design analysts, and others to understand business or industry requirements. They develop database architectural strategies at the modeling, design and implementation stages to address business or industry requirements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
