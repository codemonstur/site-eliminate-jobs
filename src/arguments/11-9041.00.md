## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities in such fields as architecture and engineering or research and development in these fields. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They manage the coordination and overall integration of technical activities in architecture or engineering projects. They direct, review, or approve project design changes. They consult or negotiate with clients to prepare project specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
