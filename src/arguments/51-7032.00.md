## Description

Plan, lay out, and construct wooden unit or sectional patterns used in forming sand molds for castings. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They read blueprints, drawings, or written specifications to determine sizes and shapes of patterns and required machine setups. They fit, fasten, and assemble wood parts together to form patterns, models, or sections, using glue, nails, dowels, bolts, and screws. They lay out patterns on wood stock and draw outlines of units, sectional patterns, or full-scale mock-ups of products, based on blueprint specifications and sketches, and using marking and measuring devices. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
