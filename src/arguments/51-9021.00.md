## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines to crush, grind, or polish materials, such as coal, glass, grain, stone, food, or rubber. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They observe operation of equipment to ensure continuity of flow, safety, and efficient operation, and to detect malfunctions. They clean, adjust, and maintain equipment, using hand tools. They tend accessory equipment, such as pumps and conveyors, to move materials or ingredients through production processes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
