## Description

Post information enabling patrons to wager on various races and sporting events. Assist in the operation of games such as keno and bingo. May operate random number-generating equipment and announce the numbers for patrons. Receive, verify, and record patrons' wagers. Scan and process winning tickets presented by patrons and pay out winnings for those wagers. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They answer questions about game rules or casino policies. They collect cards or tickets from players. They compute and verify amounts won or lost, paying out winnings or referring patrons to workers, such as gaming cashiers, so that winnings can be collected. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
