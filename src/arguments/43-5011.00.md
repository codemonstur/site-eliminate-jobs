## Description

Expedite and route movement of incoming and outgoing cargo and freight shipments in airline, train, and trucking terminals and shipping docks. Take orders from customers and arrange pickup of freight and cargo for delivery to loading platform. Prepare and examine bills of lading to determine shipping charges and tariffs. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They negotiate and arrange transport of goods with shipping or freight companies. They determine method of shipment and prepare bills of lading, invoices, and other shipping documents. They track delivery progress of shipments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
