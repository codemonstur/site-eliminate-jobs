## Description

Design core features of video games. Specify innovative game and role-play mechanics, story lines, and character biographies. Create and maintain design documentation. Guide and collaborate with production staff to produce games as designed. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They balance and adjust gameplay experiences to ensure the critical and commercial success of the product. They devise missions, challenges, or puzzles to be encountered in game play. They create core game features, including storylines, role-play mechanics, and character biographies for a new video game or game franchise. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
