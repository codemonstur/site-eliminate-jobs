## Description

Diagnose and perform surgery to treat and help prevent disorders and diseases of the eye. May also provide vision services for treatment including glasses and contacts. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They perform comprehensive examinations of the visual system to determine the nature or extent of ocular disorders. They diagnose or treat injuries, disorders, or diseases of the eye and eye structures including the cornea, sclera, conjunctiva, or eyelids. They provide or direct the provision of postoperative care. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
