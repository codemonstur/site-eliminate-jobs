Major group `55` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**19** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Autonomous systems and drone swarms

Autonomous military systems and drones reduce the need for personnel. They operate without human crews.

This compresses operations and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Anduril](https://www.anduril.com/)
- [Shield AI](https://shield.ai/)
- [Wikipedia: Military robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_robot)

### AI command-and-control and decision support

AI for military command and decisions reduces the need for staff officers. It analyzes and recommends.

This compresses command work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Palantir](https://www.palantir.com/)
- [Scale AI](https://scale.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Command and control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_and_control)

### Robotic logistics and resupply

Robotic supply and logistics reduce the need for logistics personnel. They handle resupply automatically.

This compresses logistics and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Boston Dynamics](https://www.bostondynamics.com/)
- [Ghost Robotics](https://www.ghostrobotics.io/)
- [Wikipedia: Military logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_logistics)

### Unmanned vehicles and platforms

Unmanned military vehicles reduce the need for crews. They operate without onboard personnel.

This compresses vehicle operation and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [General Atomics](https://www.ga.com/)
- [Northrop Grumman](https://www.northropgrumman.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Unmanned combat aerial vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_combat_aerial_vehicle)
