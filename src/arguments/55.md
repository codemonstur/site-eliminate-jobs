- **Autonomous systems and drone swarms** — Autonomous military systems and drones. Examples: [Anduril](https://www.anduril.com/), [Shield AI](https://shield.ai/).
- **AI command-and-control and decision support** — AI for military command and decisions. Examples: [Palantir](https://www.palantir.com/), [Scale AI](https://scale.com/).
- **Robotic logistics and resupply** — Robotic supply and logistics. Examples: [Boston Dynamics](https://www.bostondynamics.com/), [Ghost Robotics](https://www.ghostrobotics.io/).
- **Unmanned vehicles and platforms** — Unmanned military vehicles. Examples: [General Atomics](https://www.ga.com/), [Northrop Grumman](https://www.northropgrumman.com/).
