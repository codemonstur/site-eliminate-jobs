## Description

Operate or tend a variety of machines to join, decorate, reinforce, or finish shoes and shoe parts. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They inspect finished products to ensure that shoes have been completed according to specifications. They align parts to be stitched, following seams, edges, or markings, before positioning them under needles. They operate or tend machines to join, decorate, reinforce, or finish shoes and shoe parts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
