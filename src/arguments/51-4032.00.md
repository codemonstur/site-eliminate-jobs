## Description

Set up, operate, or tend drilling machines to drill, bore, ream, mill, or countersink metal or plastic work pieces. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They verify conformance of machined work to specifications, using measuring instruments, such as calipers, micrometers, or fixed or telescoping gauges. They study machining instructions, job orders, or blueprints to determine dimensional or finish specifications, sequences of operations, setups, or tooling requirements. They move machine controls to lower tools to workpieces and to engage automatic feeds. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
