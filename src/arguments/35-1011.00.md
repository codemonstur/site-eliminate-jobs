## Description

Direct and may participate in the preparation, seasoning, and cooking of salads, soups, fish, meats, vegetables, desserts, or other foods. May plan and price menu items, order supplies, and keep records and accounts. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They monitor sanitation practices to ensure that employees follow standards and regulations. They instruct cooks or other workers in the preparation, cooking, garnishing, or presentation of food. They supervise or coordinate activities of cooks or workers engaged in food preparation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
