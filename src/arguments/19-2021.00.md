## Description

Investigate atmospheric phenomena and interpret meteorological data, gathered by surface and air stations, satellites, and radar to prepare reports and forecasts for public and other uses. Includes weather analysts and forecasters whose functions require the detailed knowledge of meteorology. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They develop or use mathematical or computer models for weather forecasting. They interpret data, reports, maps, photographs, or charts to predict long- or short-range weather conditions, using computer models and knowledge of climate theory, physics, and mathematics. They conduct meteorological research into the processes or determinants of atmospheric phenomena, weather, or climate. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
