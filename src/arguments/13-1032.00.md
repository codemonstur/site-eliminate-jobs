## Description

Appraise automobile or other vehicle damage to determine repair costs for insurance claim settlement. Prepare insurance forms to indicate repair cost or cost estimates and recommendations. May seek agreement with automotive repair shop on repair costs. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They evaluate practicality of repair as opposed to payment of market value of vehicle before accident. They review repair cost estimates with automobile repair shop to secure agreement on cost of repairs. They examine damaged vehicle to determine extent of structural, body, mechanical, electrical, or interior damage. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
