## Description

Perform secretarial duties using legal terminology, procedures, and documents. Prepare legal papers and correspondence, such as summonses, complaints, motions, and subpoenas. May also assist with legal research. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They organize and maintain law libraries, documents, and case files. They mail, fax, or arrange for delivery of legal correspondence to clients, witnesses, and court officials. They prepare and distribute invoices to bill clients or pay account expenses. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
