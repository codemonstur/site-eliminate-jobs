## Description

Enforce fire regulations, inspect forest for fire hazards, and recommend forest fire prevention or control measures. May report forest fires and weather conditions. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They relay messages about emergencies, accidents, locations of crew and personnel, and fire hazard conditions. They conduct wildland firefighting training. They estimate sizes and characteristics of fires, and report findings to base camps by radio or telephone. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
