## Description

Examine budget estimates for completeness, accuracy, and conformance with procedures and regulations. Analyze budgeting and accounting reports. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They analyze monthly department budgeting and accounting reports to maintain expenditure controls. They provide advice and technical assistance with cost analysis, fiscal allocation, and budget preparation. They review operating budgets to analyze trends affecting budget needs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
