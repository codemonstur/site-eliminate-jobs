## Description

Research, design, plan, or perform engineering duties in the prevention, control, and remediation of environmental hazards using various engineering disciplines. Work may include waste treatment, site remediation, or pollution control technology. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They design, or supervise the design of, systems, processes, or equipment for control, management, or remediation of water, air, or soil quality. They assess the existing or potential environmental impact of land use projects on air, water, or land. They collaborate with environmental scientists, planners, hazardous waste technicians, engineers, experts in law or business, or other specialists to address environmental problems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
