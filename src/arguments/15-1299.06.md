## Description

Conduct investigations on computer-based crimes establishing documentary or physical evidence, such as digital media and logs associated with cyber intrusion incidents. Analyze digital evidence and investigate computer security incidents to derive information in support of system and network vulnerability mitigation. Preserve and present computer-related evidence in support of criminal, fraud, counterintelligence, or law enforcement investigations. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They duplicate digital evidence to use for data recovery and analysis procedures. They preserve and maintain digital forensic evidence for analysis. They adhere to legal policies and procedures related to handling digital media. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
