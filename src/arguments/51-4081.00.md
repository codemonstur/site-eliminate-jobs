## Description

Set up, operate, or tend more than one type of cutting or forming machine tool or robot. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They inspect workpieces for defects, and measure workpieces to determine accuracy of machine operation, using rules, templates, or other measuring instruments. They position, adjust, and secure stock material or workpieces against stops, on arbors, or in chucks, fixtures, or automatic feeding mechanisms, manually or using hoists. They read blueprints or job orders to determine product specifications and tooling instructions and to plan operational sequences. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
