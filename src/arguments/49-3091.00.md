## Description

Repair and service bicycles. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They install and adjust speed and gear mechanisms. They install and adjust brakes and brake pads. They install new tires and tubes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
