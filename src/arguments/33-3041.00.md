## Description

Patrol assigned area, such as public parking lot or city streets to issue tickets to overtime parking violators and illegally parked vehicles. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They enter and retrieve information pertaining to vehicle registration, identification, and status, using hand-held computers. They maintain close communications with dispatching personnel, using two-way radios or cell phones. They patrol an assigned area by vehicle or on foot to ensure public compliance with existing parking ordinance. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
