## Description

Smooth and finish surfaces of poured concrete, such as floors, walks, sidewalks, roads, or curbs using a variety of hand and power tools. Align forms for sidewalks, curbs, or gutters; patch voids; and use saws to cut expansion joints. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They check the forms that hold the concrete to see that they are properly constructed. They set the forms that hold concrete to the desired pitch and depth, and align them. They spread, level, and smooth concrete, using rake, shovel, hand or power trowel, hand or power screed, and float. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
