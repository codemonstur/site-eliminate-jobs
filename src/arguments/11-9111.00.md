## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate medical and health services in hospitals, clinics, managed care organizations, public health agencies, or similar organizations. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They direct, supervise and evaluate work activities of medical, nursing, technical, clerical, service, maintenance, and other personnel. They develop and maintain computerized record management systems to store and process data, such as personnel activities and information, and to produce reports. They plan, implement, and administer programs and services in a health care or medical facility, including personnel administration, training, and coordination of medical, nursing and physical plant staff. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
