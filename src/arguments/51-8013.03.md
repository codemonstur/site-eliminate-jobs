## Description

Control and monitor biomass plant activities and perform maintenance as needed. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They operate biomass fuel-burning boiler or biomass fuel gasification system equipment in accordance with specifications or instructions. They perform tests of water chemistry in boilers. They operate high-pressure steam boiler or water chiller equipment for electrical cogeneration operations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
