## Description

Diagnose and treat diseases and injuries using medical imaging techniques, such as x rays, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), nuclear medicine, and ultrasounds. May perform minimally invasive medical procedures and tests. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They prepare comprehensive interpretive reports of findings. They perform or interpret the outcomes of diagnostic imaging procedures including magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), computer tomography (CT), positron emission tomography (PET), nuclear cardiology treadmill studies, mammography, or ultrasound. They document the performance, interpretation, or outcomes of all procedures performed. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
