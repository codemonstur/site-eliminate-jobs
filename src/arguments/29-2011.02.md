## Description

Stain, mount, and study cells to detect evidence of cancer, hormonal abnormalities, and other pathological conditions following established standards and practices. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They examine cell samples to detect abnormalities in the color, shape, or size of cellular components and patterns. They document specimens by verifying patients' and specimens' information. They submit slides with abnormal cell structures to pathologists for further examination. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
