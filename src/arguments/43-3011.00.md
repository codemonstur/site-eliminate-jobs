## Description

Locate and notify customers of delinquent accounts by mail, telephone, or personal visit to solicit payment. Duties include receiving payment and posting amount to customer's account, preparing statements to credit department if customer fails to respond, initiating repossession proceedings or service disconnection, and keeping records of collection and status of accounts. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They record information about financial status of customers and status of collection efforts. They locate and notify customers of delinquent accounts by mail, telephone, or personal visits to solicit payment. They locate and monitor overdue accounts, using computers and a variety of automated systems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
