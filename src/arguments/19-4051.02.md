## Description

Collect and test samples to monitor results of nuclear experiments and contamination of humans, facilities, and environment. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They brief workers on radiation levels in work areas. They calculate safe radiation exposure times for personnel using plant contamination readings and prescribed safe levels of radiation. They monitor personnel to determine the amounts and intensities of radiation exposure. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
