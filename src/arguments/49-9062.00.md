## Description

Test, adjust, or repair biomedical or electromedical equipment. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They test or calibrate components or equipment, following manufacturers' manuals and troubleshooting techniques, using hand tools, power tools, or measuring devices. They perform preventive maintenance or service, such as cleaning, lubricating, or adjusting equipment. They inspect, test, or troubleshoot malfunctioning medical or related equipment, following manufacturers' specifications and using test and analysis instruments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
