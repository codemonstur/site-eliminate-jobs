## Description

Counsel and advise individuals and groups to promote optimum mental and emotional health, with an emphasis on prevention. May help individuals deal with a broad range of mental health issues, such as those associated with addictions and substance abuse; family, parenting, and marital problems; stress management; self-esteem; or aging. It belongs to the Community and Social Service major group (code 21). They maintain confidentiality of records relating to clients' treatment. They encourage clients to express their feelings and discuss what is happening in their lives, helping them to develop insight into themselves or their relationships. They counsel clients or patients, individually or in group sessions, to assist in overcoming dependencies, adjusting to life, or making changes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
