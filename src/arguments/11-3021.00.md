## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities in such fields as electronic data processing, information systems, systems analysis, and computer programming. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They direct daily operations of department, analyzing workflow, establishing priorities, developing standards and setting deadlines. They meet with department heads, managers, supervisors, vendors, and others, to solicit cooperation and resolve problems. They review project plans to plan and coordinate project activity. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
