## Description

Teach courses in psychology, such as child, clinical, and developmental psychology, and psychological counseling. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as abnormal psychology, cognitive processes, and work motivation. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. They evaluate and grade students' class work, laboratory work, assignments, and papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
