## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of construction or extraction workers. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They inspect work progress, equipment, or construction sites to verify safety or to ensure that specifications are met. They read specifications, such as blueprints, to determine construction requirements or to plan procedures. They supervise, coordinate, or schedule the activities of construction or extractive workers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
