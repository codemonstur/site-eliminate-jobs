## Description

Administer oral hygiene care to patients. Assess patient oral hygiene problems or needs and maintain health records. Advise patients on oral health maintenance and disease prevention. May provide advanced care such as providing fluoride treatment or administering topical anesthesia. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They record and review patient medical histories. They feel and visually examine gums for sores and signs of disease. They examine gums, using probes, to locate periodontal recessed gums and signs of gum disease. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
