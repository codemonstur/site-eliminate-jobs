## Description

Maintain order and protect life and property by enforcing local, tribal, state, or federal laws and ordinances. Perform a combination of the following duties: patrol a specific area; direct traffic; issue traffic summonses; investigate accidents; apprehend and arrest suspects, or serve legal processes of courts. Includes police officers working at educational institutions. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They provide for public safety by maintaining order, responding to emergencies, protecting people and property, enforcing motor vehicle and criminal laws, and promoting good community relations. They identify, pursue, and arrest suspects and perpetrators of criminal acts. They verify that the proper legal charges have been made against law offenders. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
