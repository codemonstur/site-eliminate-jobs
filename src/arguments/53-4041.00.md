## Description

Operate subway or elevated suburban trains with no separate locomotive, or electric-powered streetcar, to transport passengers. May handle fares. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They monitor lights indicating obstructions or other trains ahead and watch for car and truck traffic at crossings to stay alert to potential hazards. They operate controls to open and close transit vehicle doors. They drive and control rail-guided public transportation, such as subways, elevated trains, and electric-powered streetcars, trams, or trolleys, to transport passengers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
