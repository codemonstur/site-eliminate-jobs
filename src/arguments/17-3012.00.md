## Description

Prepare wiring diagrams, circuit board assembly diagrams, and layout drawings used for the manufacture, installation, or repair of electrical equipment. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They draft detail and assembly drawings of design components, circuitry or printed circuit boards, using computer-assisted equipment or standard drafting techniques and devices. They draft working drawings, wiring diagrams, wiring connection specifications, or cross-sections of underground cables, as required for instructions to installation crew. They assemble documentation packages and produce drawing sets to be checked by an engineer or an architect. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
