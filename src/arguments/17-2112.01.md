## Description

Design objects, facilities, and environments to optimize human well-being and overall system performance, applying theory, principles, and data regarding the relationship between humans and respective technology. Investigate and analyze characteristics of human behavior and performance as it relates to the use of technology. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They collect data through direct observation of work activities or witnessing the conduct of tests. They conduct interviews or surveys of users or customers to collect information on topics, such as requirements, needs, fatigue, ergonomics, or interfaces. They advocate for end users in collaboration with other professionals, including engineers, designers, managers, or customers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
