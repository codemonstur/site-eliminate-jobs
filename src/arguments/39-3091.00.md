## Description

Perform a variety of attending duties at amusement or recreation facility. May schedule use of recreation facilities, maintain and provide equipment to participants of sporting events or recreational pursuits, or operate amusement concessions and rides. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They sell tickets and collect fees from customers. They provide information about facilities, entertainment options, and rules and regulations. They keep informed of shut-down and emergency evacuation procedures. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
