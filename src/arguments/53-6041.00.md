## Description

Conduct field studies to determine traffic volume, speed, effectiveness of signals, adequacy of lighting, and other factors influencing traffic conditions, under direction of traffic engineer. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They study factors affecting traffic conditions, such as lighting or sign and marking visibility, to assess their effectiveness. They interact with the public to answer traffic-related questions, respond to complaints or requests, or discuss traffic control ordinances, plans, policies, or procedures. They prepare work orders for repair, maintenance, or changes in traffic systems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
