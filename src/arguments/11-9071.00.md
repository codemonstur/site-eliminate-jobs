## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate gambling operations in a casino. May formulate house rules. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They resolve customer complaints regarding problems, such as payout errors. They remove suspected cheaters, such as card counters or other players who may have systems that shift the odds of winning to their favor. They track supplies of money to tables and perform any required paperwork. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
