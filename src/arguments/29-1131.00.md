## Description

Diagnose, treat, or research diseases and injuries of animals. Includes veterinarians who conduct research and development, inspect livestock, or care for pets and companion animals. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They treat sick or injured animals by prescribing medication, setting bones, dressing wounds, or performing surgery. They inoculate animals against various diseases, such as rabies or distemper. They examine animals to detect and determine the nature of diseases or injuries. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
