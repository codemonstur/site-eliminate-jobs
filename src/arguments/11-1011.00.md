## Description

Determine and formulate policies and provide overall direction of companies or private and public sector organizations within guidelines set up by a board of directors or similar governing body. Plan, direct, or coordinate operational activities at the highest level of management with the help of subordinate executives and staff managers. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They direct or coordinate an organization's financial or budget activities to fund operations, maximize investments, or increase efficiency. They confer with board members, organization officials, or staff members to discuss issues, coordinate activities, or resolve problems. They prepare budgets for approval, including those for funding or implementation of programs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
