## Description

Sell goods for wholesalers or manufacturers where technical or scientific knowledge is required in such areas as biology, engineering, chemistry, and electronics, normally obtained from at least 2 years of postsecondary education. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They negotiate prices or terms of sales or service agreements. They prepare and submit sales contracts for orders. They visit establishments to evaluate needs or to promote product or service sales. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
