## Description

Teach one or more subjects to students at the middle, intermediate, or junior high school level. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare materials and classrooms for class activities. They observe and evaluate students' performance, behavior, social development, and physical health. They instruct through lectures, discussions, and demonstrations in one or more subjects, such as English, mathematics, or social studies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
