## Description

Direct activities such as autopsies, pathological and toxicological analyses, and inquests relating to the investigation of deaths occurring within a legal jurisdiction to determine cause of death or to fix responsibility for accidental, violent, or unexplained deaths. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They complete death certificates, including the assignment of cause and manner of death. They perform medicolegal examinations and autopsies, conducting preliminary examinations of the body to identify victims, locate signs of trauma, and identify factors that would indicate time of death. They interview persons present at death scenes to obtain information useful in determining the manner of death. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
