## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of workers engaged in firefighting and fire prevention and control. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They provide emergency medical services as required, and perform light to heavy rescue functions at emergencies. They maintain fire suppression equipment in good condition, checking equipment periodically to ensure that it is ready for use. They assign firefighters to jobs at strategic locations to facilitate rescue of persons and maximize application of extinguishing agents. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
