## Description

Manage web environment design, deployment, development and maintenance activities. Perform testing and quality assurance of web sites and web applications. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They monitor systems for intrusions or denial of service attacks, and report security breaches to appropriate personnel. They identify or document backup or recovery plans. They back up or modify applications and related data to provide for disaster recovery. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
