## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate one or more administrative services of an organization, such as records and information management, mail distribution, and other office support services. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They prepare and review operational reports and schedules to ensure accuracy and efficiency. They set goals and deadlines for the department. They acquire, distribute and store supplies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
