## Description

Clean and shape customers' fingernails and toenails. May polish or decorate nails. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They clean and sanitize tools and work environment. They apply undercoat and clear or colored polish onto nails with brush. They maintain supply inventories and records of client services. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
