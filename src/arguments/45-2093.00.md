## Description

Attend to live farm, ranch, open range or aquacultural animals that may include cattle, sheep, swine, goats, horses and other equines, poultry, rabbits, finfish, shellfish, and bees. Attend to animals produced for animal products, such as meat, fur, skins, feathers, eggs, milk, and honey. Duties may include feeding, watering, herding, grazing, milking, castrating, branding, de-beaking, weighing, catching, and loading animals. May maintain records on animals; examine animals to detect diseases and injuries; assist in birth deliveries; and administer medications, vaccinations, or insecticides as appropriate. May clean and maintain animal housing areas. Includes workers who shear wool from sheep and collect eggs in hatcheries. It belongs to the Farming, Fishing, and Forestry major group (code 45). They feed and water livestock and monitor food and water supplies. They herd livestock to pastures for grazing or to scales, trucks, or other enclosures. They examine animals to detect illness, injury, or disease, and to check physical characteristics, such as rate of weight gain. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
