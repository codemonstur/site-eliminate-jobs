## Description

Apply interior or exterior plaster, cement, stucco, or similar materials. May also set ornamental plaster. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They cover surfaces such as windows, doors, or sidewalks to protect from splashing. They apply coats of plaster or stucco to walls, ceilings, or partitions of buildings, using trowels, brushes, or spray guns. They clean and prepare surfaces for applications of plaster, cement, stucco, or similar materials, such as by drywall taping. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
