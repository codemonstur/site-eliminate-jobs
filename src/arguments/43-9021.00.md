## Description

Operate data entry device, such as keyboard or photo composing perforator. Duties may include verifying data and preparing materials for printing. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They locate and correct data entry errors, or report them to supervisors. They compile, sort, and verify the accuracy of data before it is entered. They compare data with source documents, or re-enter data in verification format to detect errors. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
