## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of members of police force. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They supervise and coordinate the investigation of criminal cases, offering guidance and expertise to investigators, and ensuring that procedures are conducted in accordance with laws and regulations. They prepare work schedules and assign duties to subordinates. They direct collection, preparation, and handling of evidence and personal property of prisoners. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
