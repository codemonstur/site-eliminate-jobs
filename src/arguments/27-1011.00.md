## Description

Formulate design concepts and presentation approaches for visual productions and media, such as print, broadcasting, video, and film. Direct workers engaged in artwork or layout design. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They work with creative directors to develop design solutions. They present final layouts to clients for approval. They manage own accounts and projects, working within budget and scheduling requirements. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
