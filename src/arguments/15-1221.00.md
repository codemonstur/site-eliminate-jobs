## Description

Conduct research into fundamental computer and information science as theorists, designers, or inventors. Develop solutions to problems in the field of computer hardware and software. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They analyze problems to develop solutions involving computer hardware and software. They apply theoretical expertise and innovation to create or apply new technology, such as adapting principles for applying computers to new uses. They assign or schedule tasks to meet work priorities and goals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
