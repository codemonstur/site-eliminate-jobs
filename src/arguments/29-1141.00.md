## Description

Assess patient health problems and needs, develop and implement nursing care plans, and maintain medical records. Administer nursing care to ill, injured, convalescent, or disabled patients. May advise patients on health maintenance and disease prevention or provide case management. Licensing or registration required. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They record patients' medical information and vital signs. They administer medications to patients and monitor patients for reactions or side effects. They maintain accurate, detailed reports and records. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
