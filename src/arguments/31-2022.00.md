## Description

Under close supervision of a physical therapist or physical therapy assistant, perform only delegated, selected, or routine tasks in specific situations. These duties include preparing the patient and the treatment area. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They clean and organize work area and disinfect equipment after treatment. They secure patients into or onto therapy equipment. They instruct, motivate, safeguard, or assist patients practicing exercises or functional activities, under direction of medical staff. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
