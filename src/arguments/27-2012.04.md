## Description

Audition and interview performers to select most appropriate talent for parts in stage, television, radio, or motion picture productions. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They audition and interview performers to match their attributes to specific roles or to increase the pool of available acting talent. They prepare actors for auditions by providing scripts and information about roles and casting requirements. They select performers for roles or submit lists of suitable performers to producers or directors for final selection. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
