## Description

Compile, compute, and record billing, accounting, statistical, and other numerical data for billing purposes. Prepare billing invoices for services rendered or for delivery or shipment of goods. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They verify accuracy of billing data and revise any errors. They prepare itemized statements, bills, or invoices and record amounts due for items purchased or services rendered. They keep records of invoices and support documents. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
