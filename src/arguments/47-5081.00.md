## Description

Help extraction craft workers, such as earth drillers, blasters and explosives workers, derrick operators, and mining machine operators, by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include supplying equipment or cleaning work area. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They observe and monitor equipment operation during the extraction process to detect any problems. They drive moving equipment to transport materials and parts to excavation sites. They unload materials, devices, and machine parts, using hand tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
