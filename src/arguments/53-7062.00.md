## Description

Manually move freight, stock, luggage, or other materials, or perform other general labor. Includes all manual laborers not elsewhere classified. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They maintain equipment storage areas to ensure that inventory is protected. They read work orders or receive oral instructions to determine work assignments or material or equipment needs. They move freight, stock, or other materials to and from storage or production areas, loading docks, delivery vehicles, ships, or containers, by hand or using trucks, tractors, or other equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
