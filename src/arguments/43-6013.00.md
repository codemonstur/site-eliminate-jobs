## Description

Perform secretarial duties using specific knowledge of medical terminology and hospital, clinic, or laboratory procedures. Duties may include scheduling appointments, billing patients, and compiling and recording medical charts, reports, and correspondence. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They answer telephones and direct calls to appropriate staff. They schedule and confirm patient diagnostic appointments, surgeries, or medical consultations. They complete insurance or other claim forms. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
