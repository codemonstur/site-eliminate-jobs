## Description

Collect data on work environments for analysis by occupational health and safety specialists. Implement and conduct evaluation of programs designed to limit chemical, physical, biological, and ergonomic risks to workers. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They evaluate situations or make determinations when a worker has refused to work on the grounds that danger or potential harm exists. They supply, operate, or maintain personal protective equipment. They train workers in safety procedures related to green jobs, such as the use of fall protection devices or maintenance of proper ventilation during wind turbine construction. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
