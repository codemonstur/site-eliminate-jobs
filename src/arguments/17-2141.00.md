## Description

Perform engineering duties in planning and designing tools, engines, machines, and other mechanically functioning equipment. Oversee installation, operation, maintenance, and repair of equipment such as centralized heat, gas, water, and steam systems. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They read and interpret blueprints, technical drawings, schematics, or computer-generated reports. They research, design, evaluate, install, operate, or maintain mechanical products, equipment, systems or processes to meet requirements. They specify system components or direct modification of products to ensure conformance with engineering design, performance specifications, or environmental regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
