## Description

Teach courses in childcare, family relations, finance, nutrition, and related subjects pertaining to home management. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They evaluate and grade students' class work, laboratory work, projects, assignments, and papers. They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as food science, nutrition, and child care. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
