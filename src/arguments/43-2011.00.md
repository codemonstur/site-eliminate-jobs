## Description

Operate telephone business systems equipment or switchboards to relay incoming, outgoing, and interoffice calls. May supply information to callers and record messages. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They operate communication systems, such as telephone, switchboard, intercom, two-way radio, or public address. They answer incoming calls, greeting callers, providing information, transferring calls or taking messages as necessary. They greet visitors, log them in and out of the facility, assign them security badges, and contact employee escorts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
