## Description

Prepare histological slides from tissue sections for microscopic examination and diagnosis by pathologists. May assist with research studies. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They cut sections of body tissues for microscopic examination, using microtomes. They embed tissue specimens into paraffin wax blocks, or infiltrate tissue specimens with wax. They mount tissue specimens on glass slides. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
