## Description

Gather, analyze, compile, and report information regarding individuals or organizations to clients, or detect occurrences of unlawful acts or infractions of rules in private establishment. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They write reports or case summaries to document investigations. They conduct private investigations on a paid basis. They search computer databases, credit reports, public records, tax or legal filings, or other resources to locate persons or to compile information for investigations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
