## Description

Conduct research, prepare reports, or formulate plans to address economic problems related to the production and distribution of goods and services or monetary and fiscal policy. May collect and process economic and statistical data using sampling techniques and econometric methods. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They study economic and statistical data in area of specialization, such as finance, labor, or agriculture. They compile, analyze, and report data to explain economic phenomena and forecast market trends, applying mathematical models and statistical techniques. They study the socioeconomic impacts of new public policies, such as proposed legislation, taxes, services, and regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
