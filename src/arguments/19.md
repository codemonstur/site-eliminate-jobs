## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **AI literature review and hypothesis generation** — Models that scan research and propose new questions. Examples: [Elicit](https://elicit.com/), [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/).
- **Robotic laboratories and automated experiments** — Self-driving labs that run experiments without human hands. Examples: [Emerald Cloud Lab](https://www.emeraldcloudlab.com/), [Strateos](https://strateos.com/).
- **Computational modeling and simulation** — In-silico experiments replacing physical trials. Examples: [MATLAB](https://www.mathworks.com/), [COMSOL](https://www.comsol.com/).
- **AI-driven data collection and remote sensing** — Automated gathering of field and satellite data. Examples: [Planet Labs](https://www.planet.com/), [Maxar](https://www.maxar.com/).
- **Automated statistical analysis and reporting** — Tools that analyze datasets and generate reports. Examples: [R](https://www.r-project.org/), [Python](https://www.python.org/).
