## Description

Operate computer-controlled tools, machines, or robots to machine or process parts, tools, or other work pieces made of metal, plastic, wood, stone, or other materials. May also set up and maintain equipment. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They measure dimensions of finished workpieces to ensure conformance to specifications, using precision measuring instruments, templates, and fixtures. They set up and operate computer-controlled machines or robots to perform one or more machine functions on metal or plastic workpieces. They mount, install, align, and secure tools, attachments, fixtures, and workpieces on machines, using hand tools and precision measuring instruments. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
