## Description

Coordinate activities of staff, convention personnel, or clients to make arrangements for group meetings, events, or conventions. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They consult with customers to determine objectives and requirements for events, such as meetings, conferences, and conventions. They review event bills for accuracy and approve payment. They coordinate services for events, such as accommodation and transportation for participants, facilities, catering, signage, displays, special needs requirements, printing and event security. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
