**59** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Generative design and AI-assisted engineering** — AI that proposes optimized structures and components from constraints. Examples: [Autodesk Generative Design](https://www.autodesk.com/solutions/generative-design), [nTopology](https://ntopology.com/).
- **Building information modeling (BIM) automation** — Automated generation and coordination of building models. Examples: [Autodesk Revit](https://www.autodesk.com/products/revit/overview), [ArchiCAD](https://graphisoft.com/solutions/archicad).
- **Autonomous surveying with drones and LiDAR** — Unmanned aerial and laser scanning of sites and terrain. Examples: [DJI](https://www.dji.com/), [Leica Geosystems](https://leica-geosystems.com/).
- **AI structural optimization and simulation** — Machine learning to test and optimize designs under load. Examples: [Ansys](https://www.ansys.com/), [SimScale](https://www.simscale.com/).
- **Robotic construction planning and scheduling** — Automated sequencing and resource planning for builds. Examples: [Procore](https://www.procore.com/), [PlanGrid](https://www.plangrid.com/).
