## Description

Architecture and engineering occupations are the people who design, test, and oversee the construction of structures, machines, systems, and processes. The group includes architects, civil, mechanical, electrical, and chemical engineers, as well as surveyors, drafters, and environmental engineers. Their work involves drawing up plans, running simulations, analyzing materials and loads, preparing specifications, and checking that designs meet safety and performance standards. Most occupations require a bachelor's degree in engineering or architecture, and licensure is often required to sign off on public work. These professionals turn ideas and requirements into buildable, safe, and efficient real-world designs.

Major group `17` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**59** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `17` accounts for **2,690,200** workers, or **1.58%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Generative design and AI-assisted engineering

AI can propose optimized structures and components from constraints, reducing the manual iteration of engineers. Generative design explores thousands of variants automatically.

This compresses the design phase and reduces the need for large engineering teams.

**Examples**

- [Autodesk Generative Design](https://www.autodesk.com/solutions/generative-design)
- [nTopology](https://ntopology.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Generative design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_design)

### Building information modeling (BIM) automation

Automated generation and coordination of building models reduces manual drafting and coordination work. AI can detect clashes and optimize layouts.

This streamlines architecture and engineering workflows.

**Examples**

- [Autodesk Revit](https://www.autodesk.com/products/revit/overview)
- [ArchiCAD](https://graphisoft.com/solutions/archicad)
- [Wikipedia: Building information modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Building_information_modeling)

### Autonomous surveying with drones and LiDAR

Unmanned aerial and laser scanning of sites and terrain replaces manual surveying. Drones capture high-resolution data quickly and safely.

This reduces the need for survey crews and speeds up site assessment.

**Examples**

- [DJI](https://www.dji.com/)
- [Leica Geosystems](https://leica-geosystems.com/)
- [Wikipedia: LiDAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lidar)

### AI structural optimization and simulation

Machine learning tests and optimizes designs under load, reducing manual analysis. Simulations run automatically across design variants.

This compresses engineering analysis and reduces specialist time.

**Examples**

- [Ansys](https://www.ansys.com/)
- [SimScale](https://www.simscale.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Finite element method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element_method)

### Robotic construction planning and scheduling

Automated sequencing and resource planning for builds reduces manual project planning. AI optimizes schedules and detects conflicts.

This streamlines construction management.

**Examples**

- [Procore](https://www.procore.com/)
- [PlanGrid](https://www.plangrid.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Construction management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_management)
