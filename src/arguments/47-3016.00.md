## Description

Help roofers by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They check to ensure that completed roofs are watertight. They set ladders, scaffolds, and hoists in place for taking supplies to roofs. They clean work areas and equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
