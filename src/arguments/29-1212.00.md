## Description

Cardiologists is an occupation classified within Healthcare Practitioners and Technical in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They administer emergency cardiac care for life-threatening heart problems, such as cardiac arrest and heart attack. They advise patients and community members concerning diet, activity, hygiene, or disease prevention. They answer questions that patients have about their health and well-being. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
