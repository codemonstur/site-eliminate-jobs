## Description

Provide barbering services, such as cutting, trimming, shampooing, and styling hair; trimming beards; or giving shaves. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They clean and sterilize scissors, combs, clippers, and other instruments. They cut and trim hair according to clients' instructions or current hairstyles, using clippers, combs, hand-held blow driers, and scissors. They question patrons regarding desired services and haircut styles. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
