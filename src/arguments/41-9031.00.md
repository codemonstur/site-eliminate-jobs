## Description

Sell business goods or services, the selling of which requires a technical background equivalent to a baccalaureate degree in engineering. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They develop, present, or respond to proposals for specific customer requirements, including request for proposal responses and industry-specific solutions. They collaborate with sales teams to understand customer requirements, to promote the sale of company products, and to provide sales support. They create sales or service contracts for products or services. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
