## Description

Take orders and serve food and beverages to patrons at tables in dining establishment. It belongs to the Food Preparation and Serving Related major group (code 35). They collect payments from customers. They check patrons' identification to ensure that they meet minimum age requirements for consumption of alcoholic beverages. They write patrons' food orders on order slips, memorize orders, or enter orders into computers for transmittal to kitchen staff. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
