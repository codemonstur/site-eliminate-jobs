## Description

Control and extinguish fires or respond to emergency situations where life, property, or the environment is at risk. Duties may include fire prevention, emergency medical service, hazardous material response, search and rescue, and disaster assistance. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They rescue survivors from burning buildings, accident sites, and water hazards. They administer first aid and cardiopulmonary resuscitation to injured persons or provide emergency medical care such as basic or advanced life support. They dress with equipment such as fire-resistant clothing and breathing apparatus. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
