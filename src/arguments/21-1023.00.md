## Description

Assess and treat individuals with mental, emotional, or substance abuse problems, including abuse of alcohol, tobacco, and/or other drugs. Activities may include individual and group therapy, crisis intervention, case management, client advocacy, prevention, and education. It belongs to the Community and Social Service major group (code 21). They counsel clients in individual or group sessions to assist them in dealing with substance abuse, mental or physical illness, poverty, unemployment, or physical abuse. They collaborate with counselors, physicians, or nurses to plan or coordinate treatment, drawing on social work experience and patient needs. They monitor, evaluate, and record client progress with respect to treatment goals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
