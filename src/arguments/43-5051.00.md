## Description

Perform any combination of tasks in a United States Postal Service (USPS) post office, such as receive letters and parcels; sell postage and revenue stamps, postal cards, and stamped envelopes; fill out and sell money orders; place mail in pigeon holes of mail rack or in bags; and examine mail for correct postage. Includes postal service clerks employed by USPS contractors. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They weigh letters and parcels, compute mailing costs based on type, weight, and destination, and affix correct postage. They check mail to ensure correct postage and that packages and letters are in proper condition for mailing. They sort incoming and outgoing mail, according to type and destination, by hand or by operating electronic mail-sorting and scanning devices. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
