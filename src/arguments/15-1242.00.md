## Description

Administer, test, and implement computer databases, applying knowledge of database management systems. Coordinate changes to computer databases. Identify, investigate, and resolve database performance issues, database capacity, and database scalability. May plan, coordinate, and implement security measures to safeguard computer databases. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They modify existing databases and database management systems or direct programmers and analysts to make changes. They plan, coordinate, and implement security measures to safeguard information in computer files against accidental or unauthorized damage, modification or disclosure. They plan and install upgrades of database management system software to enhance database performance. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
