## Description

Install and repair telecommunications cable, including fiber optics. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They splice cables, using hand tools, epoxy, or mechanical equipment. They inspect or test lines or cables, recording and analyzing test results, to assess transmission characteristics and locate faults or malfunctions. They clean or maintain tools or test equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
