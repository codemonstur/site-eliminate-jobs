## Description

Originate and prepare written material, such as scripts, stories, advertisements, and other material. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They write fiction or nonfiction prose, such as short stories, novels, biographies, articles, descriptive or critical analyses, and essays. They revise written material to meet personal standards and to satisfy needs of clients, publishers, directors, or producers. They present drafts and ideas to clients. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
