## Description

Set up, operate, or tend machines to mix or blend materials, such as chemicals, tobacco, liquids, color pigments, or explosive ingredients. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They weigh or measure materials, ingredients, or products to ensure conformance to requirements. They read work orders to determine production specifications or information. They observe production or monitor equipment to ensure safe and efficient operation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
