## Description

Coordinate activities in resident facilities in secondary school and college dormitories, group homes, or similar establishments. Order supplies and determine need for maintenance, repairs, and furnishings. May maintain household records and assign rooms. May assist residents with problem solving or refer them to counseling resources. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They communicate with other staff to resolve problems with individual students. They observe students to detect and report unusual behavior. They supervise, train, and evaluate residence hall staff, including resident assistants, participants in work-study programs, and other student workers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
