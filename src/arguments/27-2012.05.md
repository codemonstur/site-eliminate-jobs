## Description

Coordinate activities of technical departments, such as taping, editing, engineering, and maintenance, to produce radio or television programs. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They switch between video sources in a studio or on multi-camera remotes, using equipment such as switchers, video slide projectors, and video effects generators. They observe pictures through monitors and direct camera and video staff concerning shading and composition. They supervise and assign duties to workers engaged in technical control and production of radio and television programs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
