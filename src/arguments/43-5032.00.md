## Description

Schedule and dispatch workers, work crews, equipment, or service vehicles for conveyance of materials, freight, or passengers, or for normal installation, service, or emergency repairs rendered outside the place of business. Duties may include using radio, telephone, or computer to transmit assignments and compiling statistics and reports on work progress. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They schedule or dispatch workers, work crews, equipment, or service vehicles to appropriate locations, according to customer requests, specifications, or needs, using radios or telephones. They prepare daily work and run schedules. They confer with customers or supervising personnel to address questions, problems, or requests for service or equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
