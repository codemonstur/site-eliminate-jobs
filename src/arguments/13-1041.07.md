## Description

Coordinate and document internal regulatory processes, such as internal audits, inspections, license renewals, or registrations. May compile and prepare materials for submission to regulatory agencies. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They coordinate, prepare, or review regulatory submissions for domestic or international projects. They communicate with regulatory agencies regarding pre-submission strategies, potential regulatory pathways, compliance test requirements, or clarification and follow-up of submissions under review. They maintain current knowledge base of existing and emerging regulations, standards, or guidance documents. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
