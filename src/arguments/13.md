Major group `13` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**50** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### AI accounting, audit, and reconciliation automation

AI systems can categorize transactions, detect anomalies, and reconcile accounts automatically, performing the core work of bookkeepers and auditors. They learn from historical data and improve accuracy over time.

Continuous auditing by AI replaces periodic manual reviews. As trust in automated verification grows, the need for human accountants and auditors diminishes.

**Examples**

- [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/)
- [Xero](https://www.xero.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accounting)

### Algorithmic trading and robo-advisory

Algorithms execute trades and manage portfolios faster and more consistently than human traders. Robo-advisors build and rebalance portfolios automatically based on client goals and risk tolerance.

These systems remove the need for human traders and financial advisors for routine investment decisions. Human oversight shifts to exceptions and client relationships.

**Examples**

- [Betterment](https://www.betterment.com/)
- [Wealthfront](https://www.wealthfront.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Robo-advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robo-advisor)

### Automated financial reporting and forecasting

Tools that generate financial statements, budgets, and forecasts from live data reduce the manual work of financial analysts. They update projections automatically as new data arrives.

Predictive models improve accuracy and speed, making the traditional analyst role less necessary for routine reporting.

**Examples**

- [Anaplan](https://www.anaplan.com/)
- [Workday Adaptive Planning](https://www.workday.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Financial forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_forecasting)

### LLM tax preparation and compliance review

Language models can prepare tax returns and check regulatory compliance by interpreting tax codes and financial documents. They reduce the manual work of tax preparers and compliance officers.

As accuracy improves, routine tax and compliance work can be handled end-to-end by AI, with humans reviewing only edge cases.

**Examples**

- [TurboTax](https://turbotax.intuit.com/)
- [H&R Block](https://www.hrblock.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Tax preparation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax_preparation)

### RPA for invoice and expense processing

Bots capture, validate, and post invoices and expenses, replacing manual data entry and verification. They integrate with accounting systems to keep records current.

This automates the repetitive back-office work of accounts payable and expense management.

**Examples**

- [Bill.com](https://www.bill.com/)
- [Expensify](https://www.expensify.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Accounts payable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accounts_payable)
