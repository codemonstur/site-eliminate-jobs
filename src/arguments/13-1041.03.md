## Description

Monitor and evaluate compliance with equal opportunity laws, guidelines, and policies to ensure that employment practices and contracting arrangements give equal opportunity without regard to race, religion, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They investigate employment practices or alleged violations of laws to document and correct discriminatory factors. They prepare reports related to investigations of equal opportunity complaints. They interview persons involved in equal opportunity complaints to verify case information. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
