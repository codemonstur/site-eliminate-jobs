## Description

Plan and erect commercial displays, such as those in windows and interiors of retail stores and at trade exhibitions. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They plan commercial displays to entice and appeal to customers. They arrange properties, furniture, merchandise, backdrops, or other accessories, as shown in prepared sketches. They change or rotate window displays, interior display areas, or signage to reflect changes in inventory or promotion. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
