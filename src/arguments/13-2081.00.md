## Description

Determine tax liability or collect taxes from individuals or business firms according to prescribed laws and regulations. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They send notices to taxpayers when accounts are delinquent. They confer with taxpayers or their representatives to discuss the issues, laws, and regulations involved in returns, and to resolve problems with returns. They notify taxpayers of any overpayment or underpayment, and either issue a refund or request further payment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
