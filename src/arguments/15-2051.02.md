## Description

Apply knowledge of health care and database management to analyze clinical data, and to identify and report trends. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They design and validate clinical databases, including designing or testing logic checks. They process clinical data, including receipt, entry, verification, or filing of information. They generate data queries, based on validation checks or errors and omissions identified during data entry, to resolve identified problems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
