## Description

Diagnose, treat, and provide preventive care to individuals and families across the lifespan. May refer patients to specialists when needed for further diagnosis or treatment. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They prescribe or administer treatment, therapy, medication, vaccination, and other specialized medical care to treat or prevent illness, disease, or injury. They order, perform, and interpret tests and analyze records, reports, and examination information to diagnose patients' condition. They collect, record, and maintain patient information, such as medical history, reports, or examination results. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
