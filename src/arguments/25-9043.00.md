## Description

Assist a preschool, elementary, middle, or secondary school teacher to provide academic, social, or life skills to students who have learning, emotional, or physical disabilities. Serve in a position for which a teacher has primary responsibility for the design and implementation of educational programs and services. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They provide assistance to students with special needs. They teach socially acceptable behavior, employing techniques such as behavior modification or positive reinforcement. They supervise students in classrooms, halls, cafeterias, school yards, and gymnasiums, or on field trips. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
