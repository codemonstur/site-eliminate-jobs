## Description

Protective service occupations are the people who protect people, property, and information from harm, crime, and emergencies. The group includes police officers, firefighters, security guards, correctional officers, and private investigators. Their work involves patrolling and monitoring, responding to incidents, enforcing rules and laws, restraining threats, and investigating suspected wrongdoing. Most public-sector roles require academy training and state certification, while many support roles require a high school diploma plus on-the-job instruction. Workers in this group keep communities safe and maintain public order.

Major group `33` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**28** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Workforce prevalence

Major group `33` accounts for **3,871,400** workers, or **2.27%** of the U.S. workforce (2025). Employment and projected growth vary by occupation; see each profession's page for its own prevalence and projected change.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### AI video analytics and surveillance

Computer vision for threat detection reduces the need for human monitors. It analyzes footage in real time.

This compresses surveillance and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Verkada](https://www.verkada.com/)
- [Milestone](https://www.milestone-systems.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Video surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_surveillance)

### Autonomous security robots and drones

Patrol robots and drones for security reduce the need for guards. They patrol and detect anomalies.

This compresses patrol work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Knightscope](https://www.knightscope.com/)
- [Cobalt Robotics](https://www.cobaltrobotics.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Security robot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_robot)

### Predictive policing and risk scoring

Data-driven crime prediction and risk assessment reduce the need for analysts. They forecast and prioritize.

This compresses analysis and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [PredPol](https://www.predpol.com/)
- [Palantir](https://www.palantir.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Predictive policing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predictive_policing)

### Automated access control and screening

Biometric and automated entry systems reduce the need for security staff. They verify identity and grant access.

This compresses screening and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Clear](https://www.clearme.com/)
- [IDEMIA](https://www.idemia.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Access control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_control)
