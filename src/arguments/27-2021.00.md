## Description

Compete in athletic events. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They assess performance following athletic competition, identifying strengths and weaknesses and making adjustments to improve future performance. They maintain equipment used in a particular sport. They attend scheduled practice or training sessions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
