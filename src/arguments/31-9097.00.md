## Description

Draw blood for tests, transfusions, donations, or research. May explain the procedure to patients and assist in the recovery of patients with adverse reactions. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They confirm the identities of patients by verifying their personal information. They match laboratory requisition forms to specimen tubes. They dispose of contaminated sharps, in accordance with applicable laws, standards, and policies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
