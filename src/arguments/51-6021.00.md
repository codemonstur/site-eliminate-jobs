## Description

Press or shape articles by hand or machine. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They hang, fold, package, and tag finished articles for delivery to customers. They operate steam, hydraulic, or other pressing machines to remove wrinkles from garments and flatwork items, or to shape, form, or patch articles. They straighten, smooth, or shape materials to prepare them for pressing. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
