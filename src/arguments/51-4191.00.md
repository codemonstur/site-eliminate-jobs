## Description

Set up, operate, or tend heating equipment, such as heat-treating furnaces, flame-hardening machines, induction machines, soaking pits, or vacuum equipment to temper, harden, anneal, or heat treat metal or plastic objects. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They read production schedules and work orders to determine processing sequences, furnace temperatures, and heat cycle requirements for objects to be heat-treated. They record times that parts are removed from furnaces to document that objects have attained specified temperatures for specified times. They adjust controls to maintain temperatures and heating times, using thermal instruments and charts, dials and gauges of furnaces, and color of stock in furnaces to make setting determinations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
