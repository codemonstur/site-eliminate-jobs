## Description

Assist ophthalmologists by performing ophthalmic clinical functions. May administer eye exams, administer eye medications, and instruct the patient in care and use of corrective lenses. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They take and document patients' medical histories. They conduct tonometry or tonography tests to measure intraocular pressure. They operate ophthalmic equipment, such as autorefractors, phoropters, tomographs, or retinoscopes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
