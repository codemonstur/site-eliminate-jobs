## Description

Diagnose and treat acute, episodic, or chronic illness, independently or as part of a healthcare team. May focus on health promotion and disease prevention. May order, perform, or interpret diagnostic tests such as lab work and x rays. May prescribe medication. Must be registered nurses who have specialized graduate education. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They maintain complete and detailed records of patients' health care plans and prognoses. They develop treatment plans, based on scientific rationale, standards of care, and professional practice guidelines. They provide patients with information needed to promote health, reduce risk factors, or prevent disease or disability. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
