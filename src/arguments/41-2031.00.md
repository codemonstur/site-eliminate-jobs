## Description

Sell merchandise, such as furniture, motor vehicles, appliances, or apparel to consumers. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They greet customers and ascertain what each customer wants or needs. They recommend, select, and help locate or obtain merchandise based on customer needs and desires. They compute sales prices, total purchases, and receive and process cash or credit payment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
