## Description

Appraise and estimate the fair value of tangible personal or business property, such as jewelry, art, antiques, collectibles, and equipment. May also appraise land. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They write descriptions of the property being appraised. They determine the appropriate type of valuation to make, such as fair market, replacement, or liquidation, based on the needs of the property owner. They document physical characteristics of property such as measurements, quality, and design. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
