## Description

Analyze statistical data, such as mortality, accident, sickness, disability, and retirement rates and construct probability tables to forecast risk and liability for payment of future benefits. May ascertain insurance rates required and cash reserves necessary to ensure payment of future benefits. It belongs to the Computer and Mathematical major group (code 15). They analyze data to determine premium rates required and cash reserves and liabilities necessary to ensure payment of future benefits. They analyze statistical information to estimate mortality, accident, sickness, disability, and retirement rates. They design, review, and help administer insurance, annuity and pension plans, determining financial soundness and calculating premiums. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
