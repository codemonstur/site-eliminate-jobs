## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities of an organization to ensure compliance with ethical or regulatory standards. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They report violations of compliance or regulatory standards to duly authorized enforcement agencies as appropriate or required. They identify compliance issues that require follow-up or investigation. They discuss emerging compliance issues to ensure that management and employees are informed about compliance reporting systems, policies, and practices. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
