## Description

Nuclear Technicians is an occupation classified within Life, Physical, and Social Science in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They follow nuclear equipment operational policies and procedures that ensure environmental safety. They conduct surveillance testing to determine safety of nuclear equipment. They monitor nuclear reactor equipment performance to identify operational inefficiencies, hazards, or needs for maintenance or repair. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
