## Description

Manage biofuels production and plant operations. Collect and process information on plant production and performance, diagnose problems, and design corrective procedures. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They supervise production employees in the manufacturing of biofuels, such as biodiesel or ethanol. They manage operations at biofuels power generation facilities, including production, shipping, maintenance, or quality assurance activities. They provide direction to employees to ensure compliance with biofuels plant safety, environmental, or operational standards and regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
