## Description

Diagnose and treat diseases and deformities of the human foot. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They treat bone, muscle, and joint disorders affecting the feet and ankles. They diagnose diseases and deformities of the foot using medical histories, physical examinations, x-rays, and laboratory test results. They advise patients about treatments and foot care techniques necessary for prevention of future problems. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
