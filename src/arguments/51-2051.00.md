## Description

Laminate layers of fiberglass on molds to form boat decks and hulls, bodies for golf carts, automobiles, or other products. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They release air bubbles and smooth seams, using rollers. They spray chopped fiberglass, resins, and catalysts onto prepared molds or dies using pneumatic spray guns with chopper attachments. They mix catalysts into resins, and saturate cloth and mats with mixtures, using brushes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
