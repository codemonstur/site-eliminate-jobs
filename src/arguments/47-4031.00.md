## Description

Erect and repair fences and fence gates, using hand and power tools. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They establish the location for a fence, and gather information needed to ensure that there are no electric cables or water lines in the area. They set metal or wooden posts in upright positions in postholes. They measure and lay out fence lines and mark posthole positions, following instructions, drawings, or specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
