## Description

Guide or control vehicular or pedestrian traffic at such places as streets, schools, railroad crossings, or construction sites. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They direct or escort pedestrians across streets, stopping traffic, as necessary. They guide or control vehicular or pedestrian traffic at such places as street and railroad crossings and construction sites. They monitor traffic flow to locate safe gaps through which pedestrians can cross streets. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
