## Description

Conduct research on nuclear engineering projects or apply principles and theory of nuclear science to problems concerned with release, control, and use of nuclear energy and nuclear waste disposal. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They design or develop nuclear equipment, such as reactor cores, radiation shielding, or associated instrumentation or control mechanisms. They monitor nuclear facility operations to identify any design, construction, or operation practices that violate safety regulations and laws or could jeopardize safe operations. They initiate corrective actions or order plant shutdowns in emergency situations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
