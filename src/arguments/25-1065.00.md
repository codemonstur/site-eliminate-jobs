## Description

Teach courses in political science, international affairs, and international relations. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as classical political thought, international relations, and democracy and citizenship. They keep abreast of developments in the field by reading current literature, talking with colleagues, and participating in professional conferences. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
