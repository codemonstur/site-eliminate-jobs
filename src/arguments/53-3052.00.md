## Description

Drive bus or motor coach, including regular route operations, charters, and private carriage. May assist passengers with baggage. May collect fares or tickets. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They drive vehicles over specified routes or to specified destinations according to time schedules, complying with traffic regulations to ensure that passengers have a smooth and safe ride. They park vehicles at loading areas so that passengers can board. They inspect vehicles and check gas, oil, and water levels prior to departure. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
