## Description

Operate self-propelled mining machines that rip coal, metal and nonmetal ores, rock, stone, or sand from the mine face and load it onto conveyors, shuttle cars, or trucks in a continuous operation. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They hang ventilation tubing and ventilation curtains to ensure that the mining face area is kept properly ventilated. They conduct methane gas checks to ensure breathing quality of air. They check the stability of roof and rib support systems before mining face areas. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
