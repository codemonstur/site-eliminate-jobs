## Description

Repair, clean, and adjust mechanisms of timing instruments, such as watches and clocks. Includes watchmakers, watch technicians, and mechanical timepiece repairers. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They clean, rinse, and dry timepiece parts, using solutions and ultrasonic or mechanical watch-cleaning machines. They adjust timing regulators, using truing calipers, watch-rate recorders, and tweezers. They reassemble timepieces, replacing glass faces and batteries, before returning them to customers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
