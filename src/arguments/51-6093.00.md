## Description

Make, repair, or replace upholstery for household furniture or transportation vehicles. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They fit, install, and secure material on frames, using hand tools, power tools, glue, cement, or staples. They measure and cut new covering materials, using patterns and measuring and cutting instruments, following sketches and design specifications. They build furniture up with loose fiber stuffing, cotton, felt, or foam padding to form smooth, rounded surfaces. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
