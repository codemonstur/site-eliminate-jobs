## Description

Develop programs to control machining or processing of materials by automatic machine tools, equipment, or systems. May also set up, operate, or maintain equipment. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They determine the sequence of machine operations, and select the proper cutting tools needed to machine workpieces into the desired shapes. They analyze job orders, drawings, blueprints, specifications, printed circuit board pattern films, and design data to calculate dimensions, tool selection, machine speeds, and feed rates. They observe machines on trial runs or conduct computer simulations to ensure that programs and machinery will function properly and produce items that meet specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
