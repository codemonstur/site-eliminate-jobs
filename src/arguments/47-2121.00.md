## Description

Install glass in windows, skylights, store fronts, and display cases, or on surfaces, such as building fronts, interior walls, ceilings, and tabletops. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They read and interpret blueprints or specifications to determine size, shape, color, type, or thickness of glass, location of framing, installation procedures, or staging or scaffolding materials required. They determine plumb of walls or ceilings, using plumb lines and levels. They install pre-assembled metal or wood frameworks for windows or doors to be fitted with glass panels, using hand tools. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
