## Description

Perform limited clinical duties under the direction of a dentist. Clinical duties may include equipment preparation and sterilization, preparing patients for treatment, assisting the dentist during treatment, and providing patients with instructions for oral healthcare procedures. May perform administrative duties such as scheduling appointments, maintaining medical records, billing, and coding information for insurance purposes. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They prepare patient, sterilize or disinfect instruments, set up instrument trays, prepare materials, or assist dentist during dental procedures. They record treatment information in patient records. They assist dentist in management of medical or dental emergencies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
