## Description

Teach vocational courses intended to provide occupational training below the baccalaureate level in subjects such as construction, mechanics/repair, manufacturing, transportation, or cosmetology, primarily to students who have graduated from or left high school. Teaching takes place in public or private schools whose primary business is academic or vocational education. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They observe and evaluate students' work to determine progress, provide feedback, and make suggestions for improvement. They present lectures and conduct discussions to increase students' knowledge and competence using visual aids, such as graphs, charts, videotapes, and slides. They supervise and monitor students' use of tools and equipment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
