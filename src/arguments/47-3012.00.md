## Description

Help carpenters by performing duties requiring less skill. Duties include using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They erect scaffolding, shoring, or braces. They clean work areas, machines, or equipment, to maintain a clean and safe job site. They align, straighten, plumb, or square forms for installation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
