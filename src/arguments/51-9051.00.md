## Description

Operate or tend heating equipment other than basic metal, plastic, or food processing equipment. Includes activities such as annealing glass, drying lumber, curing rubber, removing moisture from materials, or boiling soap. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They monitor equipment operation, gauges, and panel lights to detect deviations from standards. They confer with supervisors or other equipment operators to report equipment malfunctions or to resolve production problems. They press and adjust controls to activate, set, and regulate equipment according to specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
