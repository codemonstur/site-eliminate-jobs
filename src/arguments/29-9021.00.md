## Description

Health Information Technologists and Medical Registrars is an occupation classified within Healthcare Practitioners and Technical in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They assign the patient to diagnosis-related groups (DRGs), using appropriate computer software. They compile medical care and census data for statistical reports on diseases treated, surgery performed, or use of hospital beds. They design databases to support healthcare applications, ensuring security, performance and reliability. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
