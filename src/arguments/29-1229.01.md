## Description

Diagnose, treat, and help prevent allergic diseases and disease processes affecting the immune system. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They diagnose or treat allergic or immunologic conditions. They educate patients about diagnoses, prognoses, or treatments. They order or perform diagnostic tests such as skin pricks and intradermal, patch, or delayed hypersensitivity tests. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
