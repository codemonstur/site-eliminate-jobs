## Description

Teach courses in human history and historiography. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as ancient history, postwar civilizations, and the history of third-world countries. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
