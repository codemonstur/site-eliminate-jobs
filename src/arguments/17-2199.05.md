## Description

Research, design, develop, or test automation, intelligent systems, smart devices, or industrial systems control. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They create mechanical design documents for parts, assemblies, or finished products. They design advanced precision equipment for accurate or controlled applications. They design engineering systems for the automation of industrial tasks. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
