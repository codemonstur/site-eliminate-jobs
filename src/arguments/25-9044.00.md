## Description

Assist faculty or other instructional staff in postsecondary institutions by performing instructional support activities, such as developing teaching materials, leading discussion groups, preparing and giving examinations, and grading examinations or papers. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They teach undergraduate-level courses. They evaluate and grade examinations, assignments, or papers, and record grades. They lead discussion sections, tutorials, or laboratory sections. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
