## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate the services or resources of funeral homes. Includes activities such as determining prices for services or merchandise and managing the facilities of funeral homes. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They consult with families or friends of the deceased to arrange funeral details, such as obituary notice wording, casket selection, or plans for services. They schedule funerals, burials, or cremations. They deliver death certificates to medical facilities or offices to obtain signatures from legally authorized persons. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
