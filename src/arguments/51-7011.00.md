## Description

Cut, shape, and assemble wooden articles or set up and operate a variety of woodworking machines, such as power saws, jointers, and mortisers to surface, cut, or shape lumber or to fabricate parts for wood products. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They verify dimensions or check the quality or fit of pieces to ensure adherence to specifications. They produce or assemble components of articles, such as store fixtures, office equipment, cabinets, or high-grade furniture. They trim, sand, or scrape surfaces or joints to prepare articles for finishing. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
