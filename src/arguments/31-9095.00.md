## Description

Record drugs delivered to the pharmacy, store incoming merchandise, and inform the supervisor of stock needs. May operate cash register and accept prescriptions for filling. It belongs to the Healthcare Support major group (code 31). They process medical insurance claims, posting bill amounts and calculating copayments. They answer telephone inquiries, referring callers to pharmacist when necessary. They prepare prescription labels by typing or operating a computer and printer. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
