## Description

Molders, Shapers, and Casters, Except Metal and Plastic is an occupation classified within Production in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They read work orders or examine parts to determine parts or sections of products to be produced. They trim or remove excess material, using scrapers, knives, or band saws. They brush or spray mold surfaces with parting agents or insert paper into molds to ensure smoothness and prevent sticking or seepage. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
