## Description

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul automotive vehicles. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They follow checklists to ensure all important parts are examined, including belts, hoses, steering systems, spark plugs, brake and fuel systems, wheel bearings, and other potentially troublesome areas. They test and adjust repaired systems to meet manufacturers' performance specifications. They perform routine and scheduled maintenance services, such as oil changes, lubrications, and tune-ups. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
