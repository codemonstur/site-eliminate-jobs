## Description

Resolve disputes between workers and managers, negotiate collective bargaining agreements, or coordinate grievance procedures to handle employee complaints. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They negotiate collective bargaining agreements. They investigate and evaluate union complaints or arguments to determine viability. They propose resolutions for collective bargaining or other labor or contract negotiations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
