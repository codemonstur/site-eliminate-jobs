## Description

Apply makeup to performers to reflect period, setting, and situation of their role. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They clean supplies such as makeup brushes. They duplicate work precisely to replicate characters' appearances on a daily basis. They apply makeup to enhance or alter the appearance of people appearing in productions such as movies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
