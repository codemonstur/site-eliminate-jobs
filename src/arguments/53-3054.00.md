## Description

Taxi Drivers is an occupation classified within Transportation and Material Moving in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They collect fares or vouchers from passengers, and make change or issue receipts as necessary. They communicate with dispatchers by radio, telephone, or computer to exchange information and receive requests for passenger service. They complete accident reports when necessary. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
