## Description

Administer anesthetics and analgesics for pain management prior to, during, or after surgery. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They examine patient, obtain medical history, and use diagnostic tests to determine risk during surgical, obstetrical, and other medical procedures. They administer anesthetic or sedation during medical procedures, using local, intravenous, spinal, or caudal methods. They record type and amount of anesthesia and patient condition throughout procedure. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
