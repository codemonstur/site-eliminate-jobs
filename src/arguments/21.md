Major group `21` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**18** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### AI triage chatbots for counseling and intake

Conversational agents assess needs and route cases, reducing the need for intake staff. They handle initial screening and referrals.

This compresses intake work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Woebot](https://woebothealth.com/)
- [Wysa](https://www.wysa.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Chatbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot)

### Automated case management and eligibility checks

Systems that process applications and verify eligibility reduce the need for case workers. They automate verification and routing.

This compresses casework and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Salesforce Nonprofit](https://www.salesforce.org/)
- [Casebook](https://www.casebook.net/)
- [Wikipedia: Case management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_management_(mental_health))

### Telehealth triage and remote support

Remote assessment and care coordination platforms reduce the need for in-person staff. They enable remote service delivery.

This compresses service delivery and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Teladoc](https://www.teladoc.com/)
- [Amwell](https://business.amwell.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Telehealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telehealth)

### AI-assisted benefit and service navigation

Guides that match people to programs and services reduce the need for navigators. They automate matching and enrollment.

This compresses navigation work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Benefits Data Trust](https://bdtrust.org/)
- [mRelief](https://www.mrelief.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Social work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_work)
