## Description

Create or reproduce handmade objects for sale and exhibition using a variety of techniques, such as welding, weaving, pottery, and needlecraft. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They select materials for use based on strength, color, texture, balance, weight, size, malleability and other characteristics. They create functional or decorative objects by hand, using a variety of methods and materials. They develop concepts or creative ideas for craft objects. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
