## Description

Coordinate, regulate, or distribute electricity or steam. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They coordinate with engineers, planners, field personnel, or other utility workers to provide information such as clearances, switching orders, or distribution process changes. They respond to emergencies, such as transformer or transmission line failures, and route current around affected areas. They control, monitor, or operate equipment that regulates or distributes electricity or steam, using data obtained from instruments or computers. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
