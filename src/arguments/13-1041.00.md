## Description

Compliance Officers is an occupation classified within Business and Financial Operations in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They warn violators of infractions or penalties. They evaluate applications, records, or documents to gather information about eligibility or liability issues. They advise licensees or other individuals or groups concerning licensing, permit, or passport regulations. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
