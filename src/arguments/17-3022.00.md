## Description

Apply theory and principles of civil engineering in planning, designing, and overseeing construction and maintenance of structures and facilities under the direction of engineering staff or physical scientists. It belongs to the Architecture and Engineering major group (code 17). They calculate dimensions, square footage, profile and component specifications, and material quantities, using calculator or computer. They read and review project blueprints and structural specifications to determine dimensions of structure or system and material requirements. They draft detailed dimensional drawings and design layouts for projects to ensure conformance to specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
