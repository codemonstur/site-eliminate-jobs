## Description

Design or create graphics to meet specific commercial or promotional needs, such as packaging, displays, or logos. May use a variety of mediums to achieve artistic or decorative effects. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They key information into computer equipment to create layouts for client or supervisor. They review final layouts and suggest improvements, as needed. They determine size and arrangement of illustrative material and copy, and select style and size of type. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
