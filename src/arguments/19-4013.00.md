## Description

Work with food scientists or technologists to perform standardized qualitative and quantitative tests to determine physical or chemical properties of food or beverage products. Includes technicians who assist in research and development of production technology, quality control, packaging, processing, and use of foods. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They taste or smell foods or beverages to ensure that flavors meet specifications or to select samples with specific characteristics. They measure, test, or weigh bottles, cans, or other containers to ensure that hardness, strength, or dimensions meet specifications. They maintain records of testing results or other documents as required by state or other governing agencies. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
