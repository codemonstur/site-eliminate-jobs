## Description

Set up and operate motion picture projection and related sound reproduction equipment. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They monitor operations to ensure that standards for sound and image projection quality are met. They start projectors and open shutters to project images onto screens. They open and close facilities according to rules and schedules. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
