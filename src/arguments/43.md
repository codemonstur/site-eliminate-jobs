Major group `43` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**55** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

### Robotic process automation (RPA)

Bots that automate repetitive office tasks reduce the need for clerks and admins. They handle routine workflows.

This compresses office work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [UiPath](https://www.uipath.com/)
- [Blue Prism](https://www.blueprism.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Robotic process automation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_process_automation)

### LLM document processing and data entry

AI that extracts and enters document data reduces the need for data entry clerks. It processes documents automatically.

This compresses data entry and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [ABBYY](https://www.abbyy.com/)
- [Hyperscience](https://www.hyperscience.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Data entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_entry)

### AI email drafting and triage

AI that writes and sorts email reduces the need for administrative assistants. It handles correspondence automatically.

This compresses email work and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Superhuman](https://superhuman.com/)
- [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Email](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email)

### Automated scheduling and calendar management

AI scheduling assistants reduce the need for schedulers. They manage calendars and bookings automatically.

This compresses scheduling and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Calendly](https://calendly.com/)
- [Motion](https://www.usemotion.com/)
- [Wikipedia: Appointment scheduling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appointment_scheduling)

### Voice assistants and transcription

Speech-to-text and voice-driven tools reduce the need for transcriptionists. They convert speech to text automatically.

This compresses transcription and reduces staffing.

**Examples**

- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/)
- [Dragon](https://www.nuance.com/dragon.html)
- [Wikipedia: Speech recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_recognition)
