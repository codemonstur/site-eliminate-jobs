Major group `43` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**55** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Robotic process automation (RPA)** — Bots that automate repetitive office tasks. Examples: [UiPath](https://www.uipath.com/), [Blue Prism](https://www.blueprism.com/).
- **LLM document processing and data entry** — AI that extracts and enters document data. Examples: [ABBYY](https://www.abbyy.com/), [Hyperscience](https://www.hyperscience.com/).
- **AI email drafting and triage** — AI that writes and sorts email. Examples: [Superhuman](https://superhuman.com/), [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
- **Automated scheduling and calendar management** — AI scheduling assistants. Examples: [Calendly](https://calendly.com/), [Motion](https://www.usemotion.com/).
- **Voice assistants and transcription** — Speech-to-text and voice-driven tools. Examples: [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/), [Dragon](https://www.nuance.com/dragon.html).
