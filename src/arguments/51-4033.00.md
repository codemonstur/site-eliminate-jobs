## Description

Set up, operate, or tend grinding and related tools that remove excess material or burrs from surfaces, sharpen edges or corners, or buff, hone, or polish metal or plastic work pieces. It belongs to the Production major group (code 51). They measure workpieces and lay out work, using precision measuring devices. They inspect or measure finished workpieces to determine conformance to specifications, using measuring instruments, such as gauges or micrometers. They set and adjust machine controls according to product specifications, using knowledge of machine operation. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
