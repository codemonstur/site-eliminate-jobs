## Description

Provide technical assistance regarding the conservation of soil, water, forests, or related natural resources. May compile data pertaining to size, content, condition, and other characteristics of forest tracts under the direction of foresters, or train and lead forest workers in forest propagation and fire prevention and suppression. May assist conservation scientists in managing, improving, and protecting rangelands and wildlife habitats. It belongs to the Life, Physical, and Social Science major group (code 19). They thin and space trees and control weeds and undergrowth, using manual tools and chemicals, or supervise workers performing these tasks. They train and lead forest and conservation workers in seasonal activities, such as planting tree seedlings, putting out forest fires, and maintaining recreational facilities. They provide information about, and enforce, regulations, such as those concerning environmental protection, resource utilization, fire safety, and accident prevention. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
