## Description

Repair and adjust cameras and photographic equipment, including commercial video and motion picture camera equipment. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They adjust cameras, photographic mechanisms, or equipment such as range and view finders, shutters, light meters, or lens systems, using hand tools. They disassemble equipment to gain access to defect, using hand tools. They test equipment performance, focus of lens system, diaphragm alignment, lens mounts, or film transport, using precision gauges. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
