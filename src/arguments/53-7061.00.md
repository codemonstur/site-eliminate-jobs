## Description

Wash or otherwise clean vehicles, machinery, and other equipment. Use such materials as water, cleaning agents, brushes, cloths, and hoses. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They rinse objects and place them on drying racks or use cloth, squeegees, or air compressors to dry surfaces. They apply paints, dyes, polishes, reconditioners, waxes, or masking materials to vehicles to preserve, protect, or restore color or condition. They clean and polish vehicle windows. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
