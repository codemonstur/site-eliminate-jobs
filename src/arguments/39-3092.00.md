## Description

Select, fit, and take care of costumes for cast members, and aid entertainers. May assist with multiple costume changes during performances. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They create worksheets for dressing lists, show notes, or costume checks. They provide dressing assistance to cast members or assign cast dressers to assist specific cast members with costume changes. They arrange costumes in order of use to facilitate quick-change procedures for performances. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
