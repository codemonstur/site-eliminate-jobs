## Description

Write technical materials, such as equipment manuals, appendices, or operating and maintenance instructions. May assist in layout work. It belongs to the Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media major group (code 27). They organize material and complete writing assignment according to set standards regarding order, clarity, conciseness, style, and terminology. They maintain records and files of work and revisions. They edit, standardize, or make changes to material prepared by other writers or establishment personnel. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
