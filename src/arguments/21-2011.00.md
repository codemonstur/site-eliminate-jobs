## Description

Conduct religious worship and perform other spiritual functions associated with beliefs and practices of religious faith or denomination. Provide spiritual and moral guidance and assistance to members. It belongs to the Community and Social Service major group (code 21). They pray and promote spirituality. They prepare and deliver sermons or other talks. They read from sacred texts, such as the Bible, Torah, or Koran. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
