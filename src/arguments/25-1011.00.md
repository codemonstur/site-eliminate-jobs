## Description

Teach courses in business administration and management, such as accounting, finance, human resources, labor and industrial relations, marketing, and operations research. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They prepare and deliver lectures to undergraduate or graduate students on topics such as financial accounting, principles of marketing, and operations management. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. They initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
