## Description

Care for children in private households and provide support and expertise to parents in satisfying children's physical, emotional, intellectual, and social needs. Duties may include meal planning and preparation, laundry and clothing care, organization of play activities and outings, discipline, intellectual stimulation, language activities, and transportation. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They instruct children in safe behavior, such as seeking adult assistance when crossing the street and avoiding contact with unsafe objects. They remove hazards and develop appropriate boundaries and rules to create a safe environment for children. They perform first aid or cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) when required. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
