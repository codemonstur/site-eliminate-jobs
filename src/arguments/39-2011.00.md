## Description

Train animals for riding, harness, security, performance, or obedience, or for assisting persons with disabilities. Accustom animals to human voice and contact, and condition animals to respond to commands. Train animals according to prescribed standards for show or competition. May train animals to carry pack loads or work as part of pack team. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They cue or signal animals during performances. They talk to or interact with animals to familiarize them to human voices or contact. They conduct training programs to develop or maintain desired animal behaviors for competition, entertainment, obedience, security, riding, or related purposes. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
