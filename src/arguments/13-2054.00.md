## Description

Financial Risk Specialists is an occupation classified within Business and Financial Operations in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They analyze areas of potential risk to the assets, earning capacity, or success of organizations. They analyze new legislation to determine impact on risk exposure. They conduct statistical analyses to quantify risk, using statistical analysis software or econometric models. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
