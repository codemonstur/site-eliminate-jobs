## Description

Operate a variety of drills such as rotary, churn, and pneumatic to tap subsurface water and salt deposits, to remove core samples during mineral exploration or soil testing, and to facilitate the use of explosives in mining or construction. Includes horizontal and earth boring machine operators. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They operate controls to stabilize machines and to position and align drills. They start, stop, and control drilling speed of machines and insertion of casings into holes. They regulate air pressure, rotary speed, and downward pressure, according to the type of rock or concrete being drilled. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
