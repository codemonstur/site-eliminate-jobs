## Description

Perform surgery and related procedures on the hard and soft tissues of the oral and maxillofacial regions to treat diseases, injuries, or defects. May diagnose problems of the oral and maxillofacial regions. May perform surgery to improve function or appearance. It belongs to the Healthcare Practitioners and Technical major group (code 29). They administer general and local anesthetics. They remove impacted, damaged, and non-restorable teeth. They evaluate the position of the wisdom teeth to determine whether problems exist currently or might occur in the future. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
