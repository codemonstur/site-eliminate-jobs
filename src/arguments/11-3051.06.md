## Description

Manage operations at hydroelectric power generation facilities. Maintain and monitor hydroelectric plant equipment for efficient and safe plant operations. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They direct operations, maintenance, or repair of hydroelectric power facilities. They identify and communicate power system emergencies. They maintain records of hydroelectric facility operations, maintenance, or repairs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
