## Description

Represent and promote artists, performers, and athletes in dealings with current or prospective employers. May handle contract negotiation and other business matters for clients. It belongs to the Business and Financial Operations major group (code 13). They collect fees, commissions, or other payments, according to contract terms. They send samples of clients' work and other promotional material to potential employers to obtain auditions, sponsorships, or endorsement deals. They keep informed of industry trends and deals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
