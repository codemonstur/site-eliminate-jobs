## Description

Supervise and coordinate activities of passenger attendants. It belongs to the Transportation and Material Moving major group (code 53). They enforce safety rules and regulations. They resolve customer complaints regarding worker performance or services rendered. They inspect materials, stock, vehicles, equipment, or facilities to ensure that they are safe, free of defects, and consistent with specifications. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
