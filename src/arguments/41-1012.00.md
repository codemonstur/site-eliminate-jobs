## Description

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of sales workers other than retail sales workers. May perform duties such as budgeting, accounting, and personnel work, in addition to supervisory duties. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They monitor sales staff performance to ensure that goals are met. They provide staff with assistance in performing difficult or complicated duties. They direct and supervise employees engaged in sales, inventory-taking, reconciling cash receipts, or performing specific services. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 4 (substantial preparation).
