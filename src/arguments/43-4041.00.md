## Description

Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks is an occupation classified within Office and Administrative Support in the O*NET-SOC register. It belongs to the Office and Administrative Support major group (code 43). They keep records of customers' charges and payments. They compile and analyze credit information gathered by investigation. They obtain information about potential creditors from banks, credit bureaus, and other credit services, and provide reciprocal information if requested. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
