## Description

Plan, direct, or coordinate investment strategy or operations for a large pool of liquid assets supplied by institutional investors or individual investors. It belongs to the Management major group (code 11). They manage investment funds to maximize return on client investments. They select specific investments or investment mixes for purchase by an investment fund. They monitor financial or operational performance of individual investments to ensure portfolios meet risk goals. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
