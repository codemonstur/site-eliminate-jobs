## Description

Teach courses in English language and literature, including linguistics and comparative literature. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. It belongs to the Educational Instruction and Library major group (code 25). They teach writing or communication classes. They evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments, and papers. They prepare course materials, such as syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 5 (extensive preparation).
