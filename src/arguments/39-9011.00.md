## Description

Attend to children at schools, businesses, private households, and childcare institutions. Perform a variety of tasks, such as dressing, feeding, bathing, and overseeing play. It belongs to the Personal Care and Service major group (code 39). They maintain a safe play environment. They observe and monitor children's play activities. They communicate with children's parents or guardians about daily activities, behaviors, and related issues. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
