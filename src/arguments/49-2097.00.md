## Description

Install, repair, or adjust audio or television receivers, stereo systems, camcorders, video systems, or other electronic entertainment equipment in homes or other venues. May perform routine maintenance. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They install, service, and repair electronic equipment or instruments such as televisions, radios, and videocassette recorders. They calibrate and test equipment, and locate circuit and component faults, using hand and power tools and measuring and testing instruments such as resistance meters and oscilloscopes. They confer with customers to determine the nature of problems or to explain repairs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 3 (medium preparation).
