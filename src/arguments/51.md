Major group `51` of the U.S. O\*NET-SOC register, containing
**114** of the 1,016 professions listed on this site.

## Ways these jobs could be eliminated

- **Industrial and collaborative robots** — Robots for manufacturing and assembly. Examples: [FANUC](https://www.fanuc.com/), [Universal Robots](https://www.universal-robots.com/).
- **AI visual quality control** — Computer vision for defect detection. Examples: [Cognex](https://www.cognex.com/), [Keyence](https://www.keyence.com/).
- **Automated assembly and packaging** — Automated assembly lines and packaging. Examples: [Bosch](https://www.bosch.com/), [Rockwell Automation](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/).
- **Additive manufacturing (3D printing)** — 3D printing for production. Examples: [Stratasys](https://www.stratasys.com/), [3D Systems](https://www.3dsystems.com/).
- **Autonomous warehouse and logistics systems** — Automated warehouses and logistics. Examples: [Amazon Robotics](https://www.amazonrobotics.com/), [Locus Robotics](https://locusrobotics.com/).
