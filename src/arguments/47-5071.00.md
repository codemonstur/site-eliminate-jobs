## Description

Assemble or repair oil field equipment using hand and power tools. Perform other tasks as needed. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They unscrew or tighten pipes, casing, tubing, and pump rods, using hand and power wrenches and tongs. They dismantle and repair oil field machinery, boilers, and steam engine parts, using hand tools and power tools. They guide cranes to move loads about decks. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
