## Description

Install or repair cables or wires used in electrical power or distribution systems. May erect poles and light or heavy duty transmission towers. It belongs to the Installation, Maintenance, and Repair major group (code 49). They adhere to safety practices and procedures, such as checking equipment regularly and erecting barriers around work areas. They drive vehicles equipped with tools and materials to job sites. They open switches or attach grounding devices to remove electrical hazards from disturbed or fallen lines or to facilitate repairs. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
