## Description

Sell goods or services door-to-door or on the street. It belongs to the Sales and Related major group (code 41). They explain products or services and prices and demonstrate use of products. They develop prospect lists. They deliver merchandise and collect payment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
