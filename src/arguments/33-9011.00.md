## Description

Handle animals for the purpose of investigations of mistreatment, or control of abandoned, dangerous, or unattended animals. It belongs to the Protective Service major group (code 33). They write reports of activities, and maintain files of impoundments and dispositions of animals. They investigate reports of animal attacks or animal cruelty, interviewing witnesses, collecting evidence, and writing reports. They examine animals for injuries or malnutrition, and arrange for any necessary medical treatment. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
