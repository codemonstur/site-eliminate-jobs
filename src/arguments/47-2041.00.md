## Description

Lay and install carpet from rolls or blocks on floors. Install padding and trim flooring materials. It belongs to the Construction and Extraction major group (code 47). They plan the layout of the carpet, allowing for expected traffic patterns and placing seams for best appearance and longest wear. They join edges of carpet and seam edges where necessary, by sewing or by using tape with glue and heated carpet iron. They cut carpet padding to size and install padding, following prescribed method. Most roles in this occupation sit at job zone 1-2 (little or moderate preparation).
